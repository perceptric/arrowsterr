# Semrush vs SpyFu: Pricing and Features Compared in 2026

Type a rival's domain into SpyFu and you see the keywords it ranks for and the Google Ads it pays for, with 18+ years of history behind them. Semrush can show you the same things. It does it as one part of a large suite, with separate toolkits for SEO, ads, AI search and more.

Choose SpyFu if your main job is studying rivals' keywords and Google Ads, with unlimited searches. Choose Semrush if you want one suite for SEO, site audits, daily rank tracking and AI search tracking, and you have the budget for it.

## Semrush vs SpyFu: main points

- Semrush's SEO plan is $139 a month. SpyFu sells 3 plans: Basic, Pro + AI and Team/Agency.
- Semrush has more than 26 billion keywords and more than 43 trillion backlinks. SpyFu has 13.1 billion keywords and 18+ years of history, in 38 countries.
- SpyFu includes competitor ad and paid keyword data on its plans, with ad history. Semrush puts Advertising Research in a separate Advertising Toolkit that starts at $99 a month billed yearly.
- Every paid SpyFu plan has unlimited searches. Semrush sets report limits by plan.
- For AI search, Semrush has an AI Visibility Toolkit and Semrush One. SpyFu has an AI Tracker and AI brand monitoring on Pro + AI.
- Semrush has a free account and a 7 day trial. SpyFu has a 30 day money back guarantee.

[Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/) is one of the largest SEO suites. It is split into toolkits, such as SEO, AI Visibility, Advertising and Local. You can buy each toolkit on its own. Semrush One bundles the SEO and AI Visibility toolkits on one plan. Semrush has more than 28 million users, from startups to Fortune 500 companies.

### Semrush history

Semrush has its roots in SEOquake, a browser addon for SEO data. Oleg Shchegolev and Dmitri Melnikov first released the product as Seodigger, then as a Firefox extension. The company took the SeoQuake name in 2007. The two founders started Semrush in 2008. Its head office is in Boston.

Semrush grew from a keyword and competitor tool into a full marketing suite. In April 2018 it raised $40 million, with Greycroft among the lead investors. It listed on the New York Stock Exchange on March 25, 2021, under the ticker SEMR. Backlinko joined in 2022 and Traffic Think Tank in 2023. In 2024 Semrush added more brands, among them Brand24 and Exploding Topics. In March 2025 Bill Wagner became CEO, and Shchegolev moved to CTO.

### Semrush and AI search

Semrush launched its AI Toolkit on March 4, 2025. It showed how often AI tools such as ChatGPT and Perplexity name a brand. On October 29, 2025, Semrush launched Semrush One, which puts SEO and AI search tracking on one plan. In November 2025 Adobe agreed to buy Semrush for about $1.9 billion. The deal closed on April 28, 2026, and the company now runs as "Semrush, an Adobe company."

[SpyFu](https://www.spyfu.com/) is a competitor research tool for SEO and Google Ads. You type in a domain and see the keywords it ranks for and pays for in Google Ads. Its plans are Basic, Pro + AI and Team/Agency. It also includes rank tracking and backlink research, and SpyFu added a site audit in 2026. SpyFu indexes over 7.2 trillion search results across 152 million domains, and it rolls up its data each month. The tool suits PPC managers and SEO teams who want to learn from what works for rivals.

### SpyFu history

SpyFu's founder, Mike Roberts, started a tech and consulting firm called Velocityscape in 1999. Its clients included Microsoft and Wells Fargo. He built SpyFu in his spare time, working late hours. The business started with spying on the competition, and the tool began life as GoogSpy.

Roberts founded SpyFu in 2006, and it is based in Scottsdale, Arizona. The Washington Post used SpyFu data during the 2008 US presidential election. In 2010 SpyFu introduced Recon Files, to help search marketers build PPC and SEO plans.

### SpyFu and AI search

SpyFu now holds 18+ years of history. In November 2024 it introduced SpyGPT, a search across ChatGPT answers built from 250 million questions it asked ChatGPT. Today the AI Tracker comes with Pro + AI, and SpyFu GPT brings SpyFu data into ChatGPT. In October 2026 SpyFu added an AI site audit.

## Semrush vs SpyFu PPC and Google Ads research

| Feature | Semrush | SpyFu |
|---|---|---|
| Where PPC data lives | Advertising Toolkit (separate) | Included in plans |
| Price | Base $99/mo or Pro $220/mo, billed yearly | Part of every plan |
| Ad history | Position Changes report | 18+ years of history on Pro + AI and Team |

| Shopping, display and social ads | Shopping on Base, display and social on Pro | Google search ads |

This is SpyFu's home ground. Enter any domain and SpyFu shows its paid keywords and ad copy, with years of ad history behind them. Pro + AI and Team plans include historical data on a domain's ad spend, keyword buys, rankings and past ads. Basic shows only current snapshots.

Semrush keeps its ads data in the Advertising Toolkit, sold apart from its SEO plans. Base and Pro both include Advertising Research, which covers ad positions, position changes, competitors and ad copies. Pro adds competitor landing pages, along with display and social ads.

For Google search ads, SpyFu wins, because PPC data comes with every plan. If you also buy display or social ads, Semrush covers more ground.

## Semrush vs SpyFu FAQ

### Is SpyFu cheaper than Semrush?

Ask SpyFu for current prices, then compare the plan you need. Semrush's SEO plan is $139 a month, and its free account allows 10 reports a day.

### Is SpyFu better than Semrush for PPC?

For Google Ads competitor research, yes. SpyFu includes long ad history in its plans. Semrush sells ads data in a separate Advertising Toolkit from $99 a month billed yearly, and its Pro plan covers more ad types, such as display and social.

### Does SpyFu have a free trial?

No. SpyFu has a 30 day money back guarantee instead. Contact SpyFu within your first 30 days to cancel and get your refund. Semrush has a 7 day trial and a free account.

### Does SpyFu track AI search?

Yes. SpyFu's AI Tracker counts brand mentions in Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini. It comes with the Pro + AI plan, and its prompt limits are not public.

### Can SpyFu replace Semrush?

For competitor and Google Ads research, it can. For backlinks and daily rank tracking, Semrush does more, and its site audit has a longer track record. Many teams keep Semrush for SEO and add SpyFu for ads research.

### Who founded SpyFu?

Mike Roberts founded SpyFu in 2006. The tool started as GoogSpy, and the company is based in Scottsdale, Arizona.

## Summary: Semrush vs SpyFu

Look at where your hours go. If most of them go into Google Ads and rivals' ad history, try SpyFu, and use the 30 day money back guarantee as your trial.

If most of them go into SEO, open a free Semrush account first. Move to the 7 day trial when 10 reports a day stops being enough.
