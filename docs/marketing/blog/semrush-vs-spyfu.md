# Semrush vs SpyFu: Pricing and Features Compared in 2026

Semrush and SpyFu both help you see what your competitors rank for and what they pay for in Google Ads. Semrush is a large suite with separate toolkits for SEO, ads, AI search and more. SpyFu is a smaller tool built around competitor research, with long keyword and ad history.

Here is the quick answer. Pick Semrush if you want one suite for SEO, site audits, daily rank tracking and AI search tracking, and you have the budget. Pick SpyFu if your main job is studying rivals' keywords and Google Ads, with unlimited searches.

## Semrush vs SpyFu: main points

- **Price:** Semrush's SEO plan is $139 a month. SpyFu lists current prices on its pricing page.
- **Data:** Semrush lists more than 26 billion keywords and more than 43 trillion backlinks. SpyFu lists 13.1 billion keywords and 18+ years of history, in 38 countries.
- **PPC research:** SpyFu includes competitor ad and paid keyword data on its plans, with ad history. Semrush puts Advertising Research in a separate Advertising Toolkit that starts at $99 a month billed yearly.
- **Searches:** SpyFu says every paid plan has unlimited searches. Semrush sets report limits by plan.
- **AI search:** Semrush has an AI Visibility Toolkit and Semrush One. SpyFu has an AI Tracker and AI brand monitoring on Pro + AI.
- **Trying it out:** Semrush has a free account and a 7-day trial. SpyFu offers a 30-day money-back guarantee.

[Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/) is one of the largest SEO suites. It is split into toolkits, such as SEO, AI Visibility, Advertising and Local. You can buy each toolkit on its own. Semrush One bundles the SEO and AI Visibility toolkits on one plan. Adobe says Semrush has more than 28 million users, from startups to Fortune 500 companies.

### Semrush history

Semrush has its roots in SEOquake, a browser add-on for SEO data. Oleg Shchegolev and Dmitri Melnikov first released the product as Seodigger, then as a Firefox extension. The company took the SeoQuake name in 2007. The two founders started Semrush in 2008. Its head office is in Boston.

Semrush grew from a keyword and competitor tool into a full marketing suite. In April 2018 it raised $40 million, with Greycroft among the lead investors. It listed on the New York Stock Exchange on March 25, 2021, under the ticker SEMR. Backlinko joined in 2022 and Traffic Think Tank in 2023. In 2024 Semrush added more brands, among them Brand24 and Exploding Topics. In March 2025 Bill Wagner became CEO, and Shchegolev moved to CTO.

### Semrush and AI search

Semrush launched its AI Toolkit on March 4, 2025. It showed how often AI tools such as ChatGPT and Perplexity name a brand. On October 29, 2025, Semrush launched Semrush One, which puts SEO and AI search tracking on one plan. In November 2025 Adobe agreed to buy Semrush for about $1.9 billion. The deal closed on April 28, 2026, and the company now runs as "Semrush, an Adobe company."

[SpyFu](https://www.spyfu.com/) is a competitor research tool for SEO and Google Ads. You type in a domain and see the keywords it ranks for and pays for in Google Ads. Its plans are Basic, Pro + AI and Team/Agency. It also includes rank tracking and backlink research. A site audit was added in 2026. SpyFu says it indexes over 7.2 trillion search results across 152 million domains. It rolls up its data each month. The tool suits PPC managers and SEO teams who want to learn from what works for rivals.

### SpyFu history

SpyFu's founder, Mike Roberts, started a tech and consulting firm called Velocityscape in 1999. Its clients included Microsoft and Wells Fargo. He built SpyFu in his spare time, working late hours. SpyFu says the business started with spying on the competition, and the tool began life as GoogSpy.

Roberts founded SpyFu in 2006, and it is based in Scottsdale, Arizona. The Washington Post used SpyFu data during the 2008 US presidential election. In 2010 SpyFu introduced Recon Files, which it says changed how search marketers build PPC and SEO plans.

### SpyFu and AI search

SpyFu now lists 18+ years of history. In November 2024 it described SpyGPT, a search across ChatGPT answers built from 250 million questions it asked ChatGPT. Today the AI Tracker comes with Pro + AI, and SpyFu GPT brings SpyFu data into ChatGPT. In October 2026 SpyFu added an AI site audit.

## Semrush vs SpyFu FAQ

### Is SpyFu cheaper than Semrush?

SpyFu lists its current prices on its pricing page. Semrush's SEO plan is $139 a month, and its free account allows 10 reports a day. Compare the plan you need on both sites before you buy.

### Is SpyFu better than Semrush for PPC?

SpyFu is a strong pick for Google Ads competitor research, with long ad history included in its plans. Semrush sells ads data in a separate Advertising Toolkit from $99 a month billed yearly. Semrush covers more ad types, such as display and social, on its Pro plan.

### Does SpyFu have a free trial?

SpyFu offers a 30-day money-back guarantee. You contact SpyFu within your first 30 days to cancel and get your refund. Semrush offers a 7-day trial and a free account.

### Does SpyFu track AI search?

Yes. SpyFu's AI Tracker counts brand mentions in Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini. SpyFu ties it to the Pro + AI plan and does not publish prompt limits.

### Can SpyFu replace Semrush?

For competitor and Google Ads research, it can. For backlinks and daily rank tracking, Semrush does more, and its site audit has a longer track record. Many teams keep Semrush for SEO and add SpyFu for ads research.

### Who founded SpyFu?

Mike Roberts founded SpyFu in 2006. The tool started as GoogSpy, and the company is based in Scottsdale, Arizona.

## Summary: Semrush vs SpyFu

Semrush is the wider suite. It has more keyword and backlink data and daily rank tracking. SpyFu is narrower, with unlimited searches and years of competitor ad history.

If your work is mostly SEO, start with Semrush's free account. If your work is mostly Google Ads and rival research, try SpyFu with its 30-day money-back guarantee.
