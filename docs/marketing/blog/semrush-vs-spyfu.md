# Semrush vs SpyFu: Pricing and Features Compared in 2026

Semrush and SpyFu both help you see what your competitors rank for and what they pay for in Google Ads. Semrush is a large suite with separate toolkits for SEO, ads, AI search and more. SpyFu is a smaller tool built around competitor research, with long keyword and ad history.

Here is the quick answer. Pick Semrush if you want one suite for SEO, site audits, daily rank tracking and AI search tracking, and you have the budget. Pick SpyFu if your main job is studying rivals' keywords and Google Ads at a low monthly price.

## Semrush vs SpyFu: main points

- **Price:** SpyFu's plans start well below Semrush's. Semrush's SEO plan is $139 a month. SpyFu's Basic plan is commonly listed at $39 a month (check).
- **Data:** Semrush lists more than 26 billion keywords and more than 43 trillion backlinks. SpyFu lists 13.1 billion keywords and 18+ years of history, in 38 countries.
- **PPC research:** SpyFu includes competitor ad and paid keyword data on its plans, with ad history. Semrush puts Advertising Research in a separate Advertising Toolkit that starts at $99 a month billed yearly.
- **Searches:** SpyFu says every paid plan has unlimited searches. Semrush sets report limits by plan.
- **AI search:** Semrush has an AI Visibility Toolkit and Semrush One. SpyFu has an AI Tracker and AI brand monitoring on Pro + AI.
- **Trying it out:** Semrush has a free account and a 7-day trial. SpyFu offers a 30-day money-back guarantee.

## What is Semrush?

Semrush is one of the largest SEO suites. It is split into toolkits, such as SEO, AI Visibility, Advertising and Local. You can buy each toolkit on its own. Semrush One bundles the SEO and AI Visibility toolkits on one plan.

## What is SpyFu?

SpyFu is a competitor research tool for SEO and Google Ads. You type in a domain and see the keywords it ranks for and pays for in Google Ads. Its plans are Basic, Pro + AI and Team/Agency. It also includes rank tracking and backlink research. A site audit was added in 2026.

## Semrush vs SpyFu keyword research

Semrush's Keyword Magic Tool lists more than 26 billion keywords. SpyFu lists 13.1 billion keywords with full metrics and 152 million websites for competitor research. SpyFu's data covers 38 countries.

SpyFu's keyword tools lean toward competitors. You start from a rival's domain and work out which of its keywords you should target. Semrush gives you that too, plus broad keyword ideas, intent and topic research.

Semrush is stronger for general keyword research, since its database is larger. SpyFu is a good fit when your research always starts from a competitor's site. Its unlimited searches also help when you run many lookups a day.

## Semrush vs SpyFu PPC and Google Ads research

This is SpyFu's home ground. SpyFu shows a domain's paid keywords, ad copy and ad history. It says you can download competitor PPC keywords with no download limits. Pro + AI and Team plans include historical data on a domain's ad spend, keyword buys, rankings and past ads. Basic shows only current snapshots.

SpyFu's home page also claims more paid keyword and ad data than Semrush and Ahrefs. We have not tested that claim.

Semrush's ads data lives in the Advertising Toolkit. Base is $99 a month billed yearly, and Pro is $220 a month billed yearly. Both include Advertising Research, which covers ad positions, position changes, competitors and ad copies. Base also covers Google Shopping ads research. Pro adds display and social ads, plus competitor landing pages.

| | Semrush | SpyFu |
|---|---|---|
| Where PPC data lives | Advertising Toolkit (separate) | Included in plans |
| Ad history | Position Changes report | 18+ years of history on Pro + AI and Team |
| Display, video and social ads | Advertising Pro | Not listed |
| Downloads | Unlimited Google Ads exports | No download limits on PPC keywords |

SpyFu is stronger for Google Ads research at a low price. Semrush is stronger if you also need display, social or Shopping ads data.

## Semrush vs SpyFu backlinks and site audit

Semrush lists more than 43 trillion backlinks and 390 million referring domains, and it says the database refreshes at least hourly. SpyFu includes backlink research, but its pages do not list an index size. Semrush is clearly stronger for link building.

Both tools now have a site audit. Semrush includes Site Audit in its SEO Toolkit, and Semrush One adds checks for AI crawlers. SpyFu added an AI site audit in October 2026 for new plans and Standard and higher accounts. SpyFu says it scans for 100+ issues and drafts fixes. Semrush's audit has been part of its suite for years, while SpyFu's is new.

## Semrush vs SpyFu rank tracking

Semrush tracks keywords daily. The SEO plan includes 500 tracked keywords across 5 websites. Semrush One Pro+ raises that to 1,500 and adds tracking by location and device.

SpyFu tracks keyword rankings weekly inside a project. Third-party pages list 5,000 weekly tracked keywords on Basic and 15,000 on Pro + AI (check).

Semrush is stronger if you need daily movement. SpyFu gives you more keywords if a weekly check is enough.

## Semrush vs SpyFu AI visibility tracking

Semrush's AI Visibility Toolkit is $99 a month. It includes 1 project, 1 Brand Performance domain and 25 tracked prompts. Extra prompts cost $60 a month for each 50, and each extra user needs a $99 license. The toolkit has no free trial. Semrush One raises the limits to 50 prompts on Starter and 100 on Pro+. Semrush's AI reports cover Google AI Overviews, AI Mode, ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity, with coverage that varies by report.

SpyFu's AI Tracker counts how often your brand is named in Google AI Overviews and in ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini answers. SpyFu says this comes with Pro + AI, along with RivalFlow AI for content ideas. SpyFu does not publish prompt limits or refresh rates for it (check). SpyFu GPT also lets you use SpyFu data inside ChatGPT.

Semrush is stronger for AI tracking today. Its limits are published, and it has more AI reports. Neither tool lists Claude among the AIs it tracks.

## Which should you choose: Semrush or SpyFu?

**Pick Semrush if:**

- You want one suite for SEO, site audits, links and AI tracking.
- You need daily rank tracking.
- Link building is part of your work.
- You research many countries and want the largest keyword database.

**Pick SpyFu if:**

- Google Ads research is your main job.
- You want years of competitor ad and ranking history.
- You run many lookups a day and want unlimited searches.
- You need a low monthly price to start.

## Semrush vs SpyFu FAQ

### Is SpyFu cheaper than Semrush?

Yes, at the entry level. SpyFu's Basic plan is commonly listed at $39 a month (check). Semrush's SEO plan is $139 a month, and its free account allows 10 reports a day.

### Is SpyFu better than Semrush for PPC?

SpyFu is a strong pick for Google Ads competitor research, with long ad history included in its plans. Semrush sells ads data in a separate Advertising Toolkit from $99 a month billed yearly. Semrush covers more ad types, such as display and social, on its Pro plan.

### Does SpyFu have a free trial?

SpyFu offers a 30-day money-back guarantee. You contact SpyFu within your first 30 days to cancel and get your refund. Semrush offers a 7-day trial and a free account.

### Does SpyFu track AI search?

Yes. SpyFu's AI Tracker counts brand mentions in Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini. SpyFu ties it to the Pro + AI plan and does not publish prompt limits (check).

### Can SpyFu replace Semrush?

For competitor and Google Ads research, it can. For backlinks, daily rank tracking and deep site audits, Semrush does more. Many teams keep Semrush for SEO and add SpyFu for ads research.

## Summary: Semrush vs SpyFu

Semrush is the wider suite. It has more keyword and backlink data and daily rank tracking. It also costs more. SpyFu is narrower and cheaper, with unlimited searches and years of competitor ad history.

If your work is mostly SEO, start with Semrush's free account. If your work is mostly Google Ads and rival research, try SpyFu with its 30-day money-back guarantee.
