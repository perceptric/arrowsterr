# SEO PowerSuite vs Semrush: Features, AI Tracking and Pricing Compared in 2026

SEO PowerSuite and Semrush cover the same core SEO jobs. Both do keyword research, rank tracking, site audits and backlink analysis. They work in very different ways. SEO PowerSuite is desktop software you install on Windows, Mac or Linux, sold as a yearly license. Semrush runs in your browser, bills monthly or yearly, and bundles AI search tracking into its higher plans.

Here is the quick answer. Pick SEO PowerSuite if you track a lot of keywords or crawl big sites on a small budget, and you are fine running the work from your own computer. Pick Semrush if you want large cloud databases for competitor research and daily rankings with no setup. Semrush is also the pick if you need to track how ChatGPT and other AIs mention your brand.

### What SEO PowerSuite does today

[SEO PowerSuite](https://www.link-assistant.com/) is a set of four desktop apps from Link-Assistant.Com. Rank Tracker handles rankings and keyword research. WebSite Auditor crawls sites and checks on-page SEO. SEO SpyGlass analyzes backlinks, and LinkAssistant runs link prospecting and outreach. You can buy the full suite or one app on its own.

Because the work runs on your computer, SEO PowerSuite doesn't sell by credits or report counts. Paid plans list unlimited projects, keywords and crawled pages. Cloud storage for projects has a cap: 20 projects on Professional, and 400 projects with 4,000 reports on Enterprise and Max. The same team also makes RankDots, a separate AI tool that groups keywords into topic clusters and writes content. The company says its team has over 50 people, and the suite averages about 10,000 weekly active users.

### SEO PowerSuite history

Aleh Barysevich and Viktar Khamianok started the company in 2004. Their first app was LinkAssistant, a tool for link building and outreach. Rank Tracker came in 2005, and the company calls it one of the first keyword tools of its kind. SEO SpyGlass followed in 2006 for backlink checks, and work on WebSite Auditor began in 2008.

In 2009 the team put the four apps together as one bundle and called it SEO PowerSuite. By 2016 the company said its users had passed 2 million people.

The suite grew one feature at a time. SEO SpyGlass got backlink history in 2019, the same year WebSite Auditor added its Content Editor and teams got cloud storage for shared projects. Core Web Vitals checks came in 2021 through Google's PageSpeed API. Rank Tracker added a keyword grouping tool in 2022 and one year of ranking history in 2024. Most recently, Rank Tracker added Google AI Overviews tracking. It shows which keywords trigger an AI Overview and whether your site appears in it.

### What Semrush does today

[Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/) is a cloud marketing suite built around SEO. Its main plans combine the SEO Toolkit and the AI Visibility Toolkit. The SEO plan covers SEO alone. Starter, Pro+ and Advanced add AI search tracking, and Semrush sells them under the Semrush One name.

Semrush also sells add-on toolkits for traffic and market data, local SEO, content, social, ads and AI PR. Each plan includes one user, and extra users start at $45 a month.

### Semrush history

Oleg Shchegolev and Dmitri Melnikov founded Semrush in 2008. Its roots go back to Seodigger and SeoQuake, a Firefox add-on that showed SEO data in the browser. The name Semrush comes from SEM, short for search engine marketing. In December 2020 the company changed its styling from "SEMrush" to "Semrush."

Semrush raised $40 million in 2018 in a round co-led by Greycroft, E.ventures and Siguler Guff. It went public on the New York Stock Exchange in March 2021 under the ticker SEMR. It then bought other marketing companies, including Backlinko in 2022 and Exploding Topics and Third Door Media in 2024.

AI search came next. Semrush launched its AI Toolkit in March 2025 to track brands in ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity. In October 2025 it launched Semrush One, which joins the SEO Toolkit and the AI Visibility Toolkit in one plan. Adobe agreed to buy Semrush in November 2025 for $1.9 billion and closed the deal on April 28, 2026. Semrush now runs as part of Adobe.

## SEO PowerSuite vs Semrush keyword research

| Feature | SEO PowerSuite | Semrush |

| Keyword database | 216 million keywords | 28.8 billion keywords |
| Research methods | 20+ methods in Rank Tracker | Keyword research tools, full set on Starter and up |
| Competitor keywords | Competitor lookups and a keyword gap tool | Any domain's ranking keywords |
| Metrics per keyword | Volume, competition, difficulty, PPC data | Volume, difficulty and more |
| Daily limits | None on paid plans | Set by plan |

Semrush has the much bigger keyword database. It collects Google results for hundreds of millions of popular keywords through data partners. You can type any domain and see the keywords it ranks for. The SEO plan includes basic keyword research. Starter adds the full keyword research and optimization tools.

SEO PowerSuite lists 216 million keywords in its own database. Rank Tracker adds more than 20 keyword research methods, competitor keyword lookups and a keyword gap tool. For each keyword it shows search volume, competition, difficulty and PPC data. If you also want to see which keywords pull AI Overviews and which sites those answers cite, [Arrowsterr's Organic Search](https://arrowsterr.com/platform/organic-search/) shows the Google top 10 with AI Overview sources for each keyword.

**Stronger:** Semrush. A bigger database finds more long-tail and niche keywords, and its competitor reports come straight from the cloud with no setup.

Rank tracking is where SEO PowerSuite stands out. Paid plans track unlimited keywords on unlimited sites. Rank Tracker checks Google, Bing, Yahoo and hundreds of other search engines, with local and mobile results. You can scan up to 1,000 results per keyword and run checks on your own schedule.

Rank Tracker runs checks from the app on your computer through scheduled tasks. Semrush runs every check in the cloud, so data shows up each day.

**Stronger:** SEO PowerSuite for volume and price. Semrush for daily data with no upkeep.

WebSite Auditor crawls entire sites on every paid plan. The free version stops at 500 URLs. It checks technical and on-page issues, and its content editor gives as-you-type SEO tips and a TF-IDF view of top-ranking pages. It also builds sitemaps and robots.txt files.

Semrush includes Site Audit on every paid plan. Starter and up add an AI-ready Site Audit that checks how well AI crawlers can read your site. The prices page doesn't list a page limit per plan. To see in seconds whether GPTBot, ClaudeBot or PerplexityBot can reach your pages, run the free [AI crawler checker](https://arrowsterr.com/free-tools/ai-crawler-checker/).

**Stronger:** SEO PowerSuite for large crawls on a budget, since crawl size has no cap on paid plans. Semrush if you want audits that run in the cloud and AI crawler checks.

Semrush runs its own backlink crawler. It says the crawler reads about 10 billion pages a day and lists 43 trillion backlinks and 808 million domains.

**Pick SEO PowerSuite if:**

## SEO PowerSuite vs Semrush FAQ

### Is SEO PowerSuite cheaper than Semrush?

SEO PowerSuite Professional is $349 a year, and Semrush's SEO plan is $139 a month. SEO PowerSuite's paid plans also list unlimited keywords and crawls. Semrush gives you a larger database and cloud access for its higher price.

### Is SEO PowerSuite free?

Yes, there is a free version with no time limit. It can't save projects, and site crawls stop at 500 URLs. It works as a trial before you buy a license.

### Does SEO PowerSuite track ChatGPT?

SEO PowerSuite lists Google AI Overviews tracking on every plan. We found no ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity tracking on its own pages. Semrush tracks those AIs on its Starter plan and up.

### Can SEO PowerSuite replace Semrush?

For rank tracking and site audits, many users can switch. For competitor research, its keyword and link databases are smaller than Semrush's. It also doesn't cover AI answer tracking beyond Google AI Overviews.

### Does SEO PowerSuite work on a Mac?

Yes. SEO PowerSuite lists versions for Windows, Mac and Linux. Semrush runs in a web browser, so it works on any device.

### Who makes SEO PowerSuite?

Link-Assistant.Com makes SEO PowerSuite. Aleh Barysevich and Viktar Khamianok founded it in 2004, and it lists its office in Nicosia, Cyprus.

## Summary: SEO PowerSuite vs Semrush

SEO PowerSuite fits people who want unlimited rank tracking and site crawls for a yearly fee, and who don't mind desktop software. Its link building tools are a plus for agencies that do outreach.

Semrush fits teams that want large cloud databases and AI search tracking in one account. Try SEO PowerSuite's free version and Semrush's 7-day trial on the same site, then compare the reports you use most.
