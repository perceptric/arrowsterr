# SEO PowerSuite vs Semrush: Features, AI Tracking and Pricing Compared in 2026

SEO PowerSuite and Semrush cover the same core SEO jobs. Both do keyword research, rank tracking, site audits and backlink analysis. They work in very different ways. SEO PowerSuite is desktop software you install on Windows, Mac or Linux, sold as a yearly license. Semrush runs in your browser, bills monthly or yearly, and bundles AI search tracking into its higher plans.

Here is the quick answer. Pick SEO PowerSuite if you track a lot of keywords or crawl big sites on a small budget, and you are fine running the work from your own computer. Pick Semrush if you want large cloud databases for competitor research and daily rankings with no setup. Semrush is also the pick if you need to track how ChatGPT and other AIs mention your brand.

## SEO PowerSuite vs Semrush: main points

- **How it runs:** SEO PowerSuite is four desktop apps: Rank Tracker, WebSite Auditor, SEO SpyGlass and LinkAssistant. Semrush is a cloud suite you use in the browser.
- **Price model:** SEO PowerSuite's paid plans are billed yearly, from $349 a year. Semrush's SEO plan is $139 a month, or $117.33 a month billed yearly.
- **Limits:** SEO PowerSuite's paid plans list unlimited projects, keywords, site crawls and backlinks. Semrush's SEO plan covers 5 websites and 500 keywords tracked daily.
- **AI search tracking:** SEO PowerSuite tracks Google AI Overviews on every plan. Semrush tracks prompts in ChatGPT, Google AI, Gemini and Perplexity on its Starter plan and up, or through its AI Visibility Toolkit.
- **Data size:** Semrush lists 28.8 billion keywords and 43 trillion backlinks. SEO PowerSuite lists 216 million keywords and 6.5 trillion backlinks.
- **Free use:** SEO PowerSuite has a free version with no time limit, but it can't save projects. Semrush has a free account with 10 reports a day and a 7-day trial of its paid plans.

## What is SEO PowerSuite?

SEO PowerSuite is a set of four desktop apps from Link-Assistant.Com. Rank Tracker handles rankings and keyword research. WebSite Auditor crawls sites and checks on-page SEO. SEO SpyGlass analyzes backlinks, and LinkAssistant runs link prospecting and outreach. You can buy the full suite or one app on its own.

Because the work runs on your computer, SEO PowerSuite doesn't sell by credits or report counts. Paid plans list unlimited projects, keywords and crawled pages. Cloud storage for projects has a cap: 20 projects on Professional, and 400 projects with 4,000 reports on Enterprise and Max. The same team also makes RankDots, a separate AI tool that groups keywords into topic clusters and writes content.

## What is Semrush?

Semrush is a cloud marketing suite built around SEO. Its main plans combine the SEO Toolkit and the AI Visibility Toolkit. The SEO plan covers SEO alone. Starter, Pro+ and Advanced add AI search tracking, and Semrush sells them under the Semrush One name.

Semrush also sells add-on toolkits for traffic and market data, local SEO, content, social, ads and AI PR. Each plan includes one user, and extra users start at $45 a month.

## SEO PowerSuite vs Semrush keyword research

Semrush has the much bigger keyword database. It lists 28.8 billion keywords and collects Google results for hundreds of millions of popular keywords through data partners. You can type any domain and see the keywords it ranks for. The SEO plan includes basic keyword research. Starter adds the full keyword research and optimization tools.

SEO PowerSuite lists 216 million keywords in its own database. Rank Tracker adds more than 20 keyword research methods, competitor keyword lookups and a keyword gap tool. For each keyword it shows search volume, competition, difficulty and PPC data. There are no daily report limits on paid plans.

**Stronger:** Semrush. A bigger database finds more long-tail and niche keywords, and its competitor reports come straight from the cloud with no setup.

## SEO PowerSuite vs Semrush rank tracking

Rank tracking is where SEO PowerSuite stands out. Paid plans track unlimited keywords on unlimited sites. Rank Tracker checks Google, Bing, Yahoo and hundreds of other search engines, with local and mobile results. You can scan up to 1,000 results per keyword and run checks on your own schedule.

| | SEO PowerSuite | Semrush |
|---|---|---|
| Keywords tracked | Unlimited on paid plans | 500 (SEO, Starter), 1,500 (Pro+), 5,000+ (Advanced) |
| Update schedule | You set it: daily, weekly or monthly | Daily |
| Competitors per project | 5 (Professional), 40 (Enterprise, Max) | Not listed on the prices page |
| History | 1 year listed on Professional | Historical SEO data on Pro+ and up |

Rank Tracker runs checks from the app on your computer through scheduled tasks. Semrush runs every check in the cloud, so data shows up each day.

**Stronger:** SEO PowerSuite for volume and price. Semrush for daily data with no upkeep.

## SEO PowerSuite vs Semrush site audit

WebSite Auditor crawls entire sites on every paid plan. The free version stops at 500 URLs. It checks technical and on-page issues, and its content editor gives as-you-type SEO tips and a TF-IDF view of top-ranking pages. It also builds sitemaps and robots.txt files.

Semrush includes Site Audit on every paid plan. Starter and up add an AI-ready Site Audit that checks how well AI crawlers can read your site. The prices page doesn't list a page limit per plan.

**Stronger:** SEO PowerSuite for large crawls on a budget, since crawl size has no cap on paid plans. Semrush if you want audits that run in the cloud and AI crawler checks.

## SEO PowerSuite vs Semrush backlink data

Semrush runs its own backlink crawler. It says the crawler reads about 10 billion pages a day and lists 43 trillion backlinks and 808 million domains.

SEO SpyGlass uses SEO PowerSuite's own Link Explorer index, which lists 6.5 trillion backlinks. It can also pull links from your Google Search Console and Google Analytics. It includes a link penalty risk audit and a disavow file maker. Enterprise compares link profiles for up to 11 sites. LinkAssistant adds 10 ways to find link prospects, plus a built-in email client for outreach.

Each vendor counts links its own way, so the totals don't compare one to one.

**Stronger:** Semrush for the size of its link index. SEO PowerSuite for link building work, since outreach sits in the same suite.

## SEO PowerSuite vs Semrush AI visibility tracking

This is the clearest gap between the two.

SEO PowerSuite tracks Google AI Overviews on every plan, the free version included. On the pages we checked, we found no tracking for ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or Perplexity. RankDots is a content tool and doesn't track AI answers.

Semrush has two routes for AI tracking:

- **Semrush One plans:** Starter tracks 50 prompts daily, and Pro+ tracks 100. Its help page says they cover Google Search, ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini. Semrush's pages list different prompt limits for Advanced, so confirm with sales.
- **AI Visibility Toolkit:** $99 a month on its own for 25 prompts and 1 domain. It has no free trial.

The SEO plan includes AI visibility reports for any domain and AI sentiment, with no prompt tracking quota.

**Stronger:** Semrush, by a wide margin. If you need to know how ChatGPT talks about your brand, SEO PowerSuite doesn't cover it.

## Which should you choose: SEO PowerSuite or Semrush?

**Pick SEO PowerSuite if:**

- You track thousands of keywords across many sites.
- You crawl large sites and don't want a page cap.
- You prefer one yearly payment to a monthly bill.
- You do link building and want outreach in the same suite.

**Pick Semrush if:**

- You need deep competitor data from a large keyword and link database.
- You want daily rankings that run in the cloud.
- You need to track ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity next to your SEO data.
- Your team works from many devices and wants the data in the cloud.

## SEO PowerSuite vs Semrush FAQ

### Is SEO PowerSuite cheaper than Semrush?

SEO PowerSuite Professional is $349 a year, and Semrush's SEO plan is $139 a month. SEO PowerSuite's paid plans also list unlimited keywords and crawls. Semrush gives you a larger database and cloud access for its higher price.

### Is SEO PowerSuite free?

Yes, there is a free version with no time limit. It can't save projects, and site crawls stop at 500 URLs. It works as a trial before you buy a license.

### Does SEO PowerSuite track ChatGPT?

SEO PowerSuite lists Google AI Overviews tracking on every plan. We found no ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity tracking on its own pages. Semrush tracks those AIs on its Starter plan and up.

### Can SEO PowerSuite replace Semrush?

For rank tracking and site audits, many users can switch. For competitor research, its keyword and link databases are smaller than Semrush's. It also doesn't cover AI answer tracking beyond Google AI Overviews.

### Does SEO PowerSuite work on a Mac?

Yes. SEO PowerSuite lists versions for Windows, Mac and Linux. Semrush runs in a web browser, so it works on any device.

## Summary: SEO PowerSuite vs Semrush

SEO PowerSuite fits people who want unlimited rank tracking and site crawls for a yearly fee, and who don't mind desktop software. Its link building tools are a plus for agencies that do outreach.

Semrush fits teams that want large cloud databases and AI search tracking in one account. Try SEO PowerSuite's free version and Semrush's 7-day trial on the same site, then compare the reports you use most.
