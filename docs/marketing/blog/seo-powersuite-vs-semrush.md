# SEO PowerSuite vs Semrush: Features, AI Tracking and Pricing Compared in 2026

SEO PowerSuite runs on your computer. Semrush runs in your browser. SEO PowerSuite is desktop software for Windows, Mac or Linux, sold as a yearly license. Semrush bills monthly or yearly and bundles AI search tracking into its higher plans. The SEO jobs are the same: keyword research, rank tracking, site audits and backlink analysis.

Pick SEO PowerSuite if you track a lot of keywords or crawl big sites on a small budget, and you're fine running the work from your own machine. Pick Semrush if you want large cloud databases for competitor research and daily rankings with no setup. Semrush is also your pick if you need to track how ChatGPT and other AIs mention your brand.

### What SEO PowerSuite does today

[SEO PowerSuite](https://www.link-assistant.com/) is a set of four desktop apps from Link-Assistant.Com. Rank Tracker handles rankings and keyword research. WebSite Auditor crawls sites and checks on page SEO. SEO SpyGlass analyzes backlinks, and LinkAssistant runs link prospecting and outreach. You can buy the full suite or one app on its own.

The work runs on your computer, so SEO PowerSuite doesn't sell by credits or report counts. Paid plans put no cap on projects or keywords, and crawls have no page limit. Cloud storage for projects does have a cap: 20 projects on Professional, and 400 projects with 4,000 reports on Enterprise and Max. The same team makes RankDots, a separate AI tool that groups keywords into topic clusters and writes content. The team has over 50 people, and the suite averages about 10,000 weekly active users.

### SEO PowerSuite history

Aleh Barysevich and Viktar Khamianok started the company in 2004. Their first app was LinkAssistant, built for link building and outreach. Rank Tracker came in 2005 as one of the first keyword tools of its kind. SEO SpyGlass followed in 2006 for backlink checks, and work on WebSite Auditor began in 2008.

In 2009 the team bundled the four apps and named the set SEO PowerSuite. By 2016 it had passed 2 million users.

After that the suite grew one feature at a time. In 2019 SEO SpyGlass got backlink history, WebSite Auditor added its Content Editor, and teams got cloud storage for shared projects. Core Web Vitals checks came in 2021 through Google's PageSpeed API. Rank Tracker added a keyword grouping tool in 2022 and one year of ranking history in 2024. Its newest addition is Google AI Overviews tracking, which shows which keywords trigger an AI Overview and whether your site appears in it.

### What Semrush does today

[Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/) is a cloud marketing suite built around SEO. The SEO plan covers SEO alone. Starter, Pro+ and Advanced combine the SEO Toolkit with the AI Visibility Toolkit, so they add AI search tracking. Semrush sells those three under the Semrush One name.

Extra toolkits cover traffic and market data, local SEO, content, social, ads and AI PR. Each plan includes one user, and extra users start at $45 a month.

### Semrush history

Oleg Shchegolev and Dmitri Melnikov founded Semrush in 2008. It grew out of Seodigger and SeoQuake, a Firefox extension that showed SEO data in the browser. The name comes from SEM, short for search engine marketing. In December 2020 the styling changed from "SEMrush" to "Semrush."

In 2018 Semrush raised $40 million in a round led jointly by Greycroft, E.ventures and Siguler Guff. It went public on the New York Stock Exchange in March 2021 under the ticker SEMR. Then it bought other marketing companies, including Backlinko in 2022 and Exploding Topics and Third Door Media in 2024.

AI search came next. The AI Toolkit launched in March 2025 to track brands in ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity. Semrush One followed in October 2025 and put the SEO Toolkit and the AI Visibility Toolkit in one plan. Adobe agreed to buy Semrush in November 2025 for $1.9 billion and closed the deal on April 28, 2026. Semrush now runs as part of Adobe.

## SEO PowerSuite vs Semrush FAQ

### Is SEO PowerSuite cheaper than Semrush?

Yes. SEO PowerSuite Professional is $349 a year, and Semrush's SEO plan is $139 a month. SEO PowerSuite's paid plans also have no cap on keywords or crawls. For its higher price, Semrush gives you a larger database and cloud access.

### Is SEO PowerSuite free?

Yes, there is a free version with no time limit. It can't save projects, and site crawls stop at 500 URLs. Treat it as your trial before you buy a license.

### Does SEO PowerSuite track ChatGPT?

No. SEO PowerSuite tracks Google AI Overviews on every plan. It doesn't track ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity. Semrush tracks those AIs on its Starter plan and up.

### Can SEO PowerSuite replace Semrush?

For rank tracking and site audits, many users can switch. Competitor research is harder, since its keyword and link databases are smaller than Semrush's. It also doesn't track AI answers beyond Google AI Overviews.

### Does SEO PowerSuite work on a Mac?

Yes. SEO PowerSuite has versions for Windows, Mac and Linux. Semrush runs in a web browser, so it works on any device.

### Who makes SEO PowerSuite?

Link-Assistant.Com makes SEO PowerSuite. Aleh Barysevich and Viktar Khamianok founded it in 2004, and its office is in Nicosia, Cyprus.

## Summary: SEO PowerSuite vs Semrush

Count your keywords first. If you track thousands across many client sites, the $349 Professional license covers all of them, and the outreach tools help if your agency builds links. If you need ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity in the same account as your rankings, Semrush is the one of these two that tracks them.

Run SEO PowerSuite's free version and Semrush's 7 day trial on the same site, then compare the reports you use most.
