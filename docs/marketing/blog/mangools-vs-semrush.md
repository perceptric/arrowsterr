# Mangools vs Semrush: Features, AI Tracking and Pricing Compared in 2026

Mangools starts at $37.70 a month billed yearly. Semrush starts at $117.33. Each covers keyword research, backlink checks, rank tracking and AI answer tracking, but they're built for different people.

Mangools is a small bundle of tools with KWFinder at the center, and every plan includes its AI Search Watcher for tracking brand mentions in AI answers. Semrush is a large suite with far more data. It adds site audits, daily rank tracking and extra toolkits for most of marketing.

Pick Mangools if you run a blog or small business site and want easy keyword research and AI answer tracking at a low price. Pick Semrush if you run SEO for a growing business or agency and need site audits and deep competitor data.

### What Mangools does today

[Mangools](https://mangools.com/) is a set of SEO tools sold as one bundle. KWFinder finds keywords and shows their difficulty. SERPChecker breaks down the Google results for a keyword, and SERPWatcher tracks your rankings. LinkMiner checks backlinks, and SiteProfiler shows the main SEO metrics for any domain. AI Search Watcher tracks how AI answers mention your brand.

You can't buy one tool alone, because the tools depend on each other. KWFinder's difficulty score, for one, comes from SERPChecker. Limits reset every 24 hours.

### Mangools history

Peter Hrbáčik launched the first version of KWFinder in August 2014, and he still runs the company as CEO. KWFinder 2.0 came out in March 2016, and work on SERPChecker began a month later. In May 2016 the tools came together under one brand, Mangools. That September the team opened an office in Bratislava.

New tools followed each year. SERPWatcher arrived in July 2017 for rank tracking. LinkMiner came in January 2018 for backlinks, and SiteProfiler joined in September 2018 as the fifth tool. An SEO browser extension came out in October 2018, then KWFinder 3.0 with a new design in May 2019.

Mangools has grown without outside investors. By 2024 it had 1.1 million users and 25,000 paying customers. Its newest tool, AI Search Watcher, tracks brand mentions in ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and six other AIs. Every plan now includes it, along with API and MCP access.

### What Semrush does today

[Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/) is a cloud marketing suite built around SEO. Its SEO plan covers SEO alone. The Semrush One plans (Starter, Pro+ and Advanced) combine the SEO Toolkit and the AI Visibility Toolkit, so they add AI search tracking.

Each plan includes one user. Traffic and market data, local SEO, content, social, ads and AI PR come as separate toolkits you can add.

### Semrush history

Semrush started as SEO data inside a browser. Its roots go back to Seodigger and SeoQuake, a Firefox extension, and the name comes from SEM, short for search engine marketing. Oleg Shchegolev and Dmitri Melnikov founded the company in 2008. In December 2020 it changed its styling from "SEMrush" to "Semrush."

In 2018 Semrush raised $40 million in a round led jointly by Greycroft, E.ventures and Siguler Guff. It went public on the New York Stock Exchange in March 2021 under the ticker SEMR. Purchases of other marketing companies followed, including Backlinko in 2022 and Exploding Topics and Third Door Media in 2024.

In March 2025 Semrush launched its AI Toolkit to track brands in ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity. Semrush One came in October 2025 and put the SEO Toolkit and the AI Visibility Toolkit in one plan. That November, Adobe agreed to buy Semrush for $1.9 billion. The deal closed on April 28, 2026, and Semrush now runs as part of Adobe.

Mangools has no crawler for technical SEO. Its free SEO Content Optimizer is a separate mini tool, and nothing in the bundle checks a whole site for broken links or redirects. Semrush includes Site Audit on every paid plan. To see whether AI crawlers can reach your pages, run the free [AI crawler checker](https://arrowsterr.com/free-tools/ai-crawler-checker/).

Rankings are closer. SERPWatcher is easy to set up and tracks any number of domains within your keyword limit. Weekly updates work for small sites that publish now and then. If a ranking drop costs you money, you'll want daily data, and Mangools only gives you that on Agency.

**Stronger:** Semrush, on both counts: daily rankings on every plan, plus a site audit Mangools doesn't have.

## Mangools vs Semrush FAQ

### Is Mangools cheaper than Semrush?

Yes. Mangools Basic is $61 a month, or $37.70 a month billed yearly. Semrush's SEO plan is $139 a month, or $117.33 a month billed yearly. Semrush does include site audits and daily rankings, and Mangools Basic has neither.

### Is Mangools good for beginners?

Yes. The design is easy to learn, and each tool does one job. KWFinder shows keyword ideas and difficulty on one screen. A free account lets you try it with 5 lookups a day.

### Does Mangools have a site audit?

No. Mangools has no site crawler for technical SEO. If you need to find broken links or crawl errors across a site, use Semrush or a separate audit tool.

### Does Mangools track ChatGPT?

Yes. Every paid plan includes AI Search Watcher PRO, which tracks ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, AI Mode, Gemini, Claude, Grok, Mistral and Llama. It monitors up to 500 prompts and checks them once a week.

### Can Mangools replace Semrush?

For keyword research and AI answer tracking, many small sites can switch. You lose site audits and Semrush's larger databases, and rankings update weekly below the Agency plan. Agencies and technical SEOs usually need more than Mangools has.

### Who makes Mangools?

Mangools is a Slovak company led by its founder and CEO, Peter Hrbáčik. He launched KWFinder in 2014, and the tools took the Mangools name in 2016.

## Summary: Mangools vs Semrush

If your week is keyword research plus a look at how AI answers mention you, Mangools covers it from $37.70 a month billed yearly, with 8 AIs on every plan. Once you need technical audits, or daily rankings without paying for Agency, Semrush is the next step.
