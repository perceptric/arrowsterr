# Mangools vs Semrush: Features, AI Tracking and Pricing Compared in 2026

Mangools and Semrush both cover keyword research, backlink checks, rank tracking and AI answer tracking. They are built for different people. Mangools is a small, simple bundle of tools with KWFinder at the center. Every plan also includes its AI Search Watcher for tracking brand mentions in AI answers. Semrush is a large suite with far more data, site audits, daily rank tracking and add-on toolkits for most of marketing.

Here is the quick answer. Pick Mangools if you run a blog or small business site and want easy keyword research and AI answer tracking at a low price. Pick Semrush if you run SEO for a growing business or agency and need site audits and deep competitor data.

## Mangools vs Semrush: main points

- **Entry price:** Mangools Basic is $61 a month, or $37.70 a month billed yearly. Semrush's SEO plan is $139 a month, or $117.33 a month billed yearly.
- **Ease of use:** Mangools keeps each job in one simple app: KWFinder, SERPChecker, SERPWatcher, LinkMiner and SiteProfiler. Semrush has many more tools and takes longer to learn.
- **Site audit:** Semrush includes Site Audit on every paid plan. Mangools has no site audit tool.
- **Rank tracking:** Semrush updates rankings daily on every plan. Mangools updates weekly, or daily on its Agency plan.
- **AI search tracking:** Every Mangools plan includes AI Search Watcher PRO with 500 prompts across 8 AIs. Semrush's Starter plan ($199) tracks 50 prompts daily across 4.
- **Data size:** Semrush lists 28.8 billion keywords and its own index of 43 trillion backlinks. Mangools lists 2.5 billion related keywords and uses Majestic's backlink index.

## What is Mangools?

Mangools is a set of SEO tools sold as one bundle. KWFinder finds keywords and shows their difficulty. SERPChecker breaks down the Google results for a keyword, and SERPWatcher tracks your rankings. LinkMiner checks backlinks, and SiteProfiler shows the main SEO metrics for any domain. AI Search Watcher tracks how AI answers mention your brand.

You can't buy one tool alone. Mangools says its tools depend on each other, such as KWFinder's difficulty score coming from SERPChecker. Limits reset every 24 hours.

## What is Semrush?

Semrush is a cloud marketing suite built around SEO. Its main plans combine the SEO Toolkit and the AI Visibility Toolkit. The SEO plan covers SEO alone. Starter, Pro+ and Advanced add AI search tracking, and Semrush sells them under the Semrush One name.

Semrush also sells add-on toolkits for traffic and market data, local SEO, content, social, ads and AI PR. Each plan includes one user.

## Mangools vs Semrush keyword research

KWFinder is the reason most people pick Mangools. You type a keyword and get related ideas, questions and a difficulty score in one clean screen. You can also type a competitor's domain to see the keywords it ranks for. Mangools lists 2.5 billion related keywords and about 65,000 locations, down to cities.

Limits are per 24 hours. Basic gives 100 keyword lookups a day, Premium 500 and Agency 1,200. Basic shows 200 keyword suggestions per lookup, and Premium and Agency show unlimited suggestions. Mangools says search volumes usually run about a month behind.

Semrush lists 28.8 billion keywords. Its SEO plan includes basic keyword research and competitor analysis. Starter adds the full keyword research and optimization tools, and Pro+ adds keyword cannibalization analysis.

**Stronger:** Semrush for data depth, since it has a much larger database for niche and long-tail keywords. Mangools for ease of use, which matters a lot if you only do keyword research a few times a week.

## Mangools vs Semrush backlink data

LinkMiner uses Majestic's backlink index. Mangools lists 9.5 trillion backlinks across Majestic's fresh and historic index. Basic allows 100,000 backlink rows a month, Premium 500,000 and Agency 1.2 million.

Semrush runs its own backlink crawler. It says the crawler reads about 10 billion pages a day and lists 43 trillion backlinks and 808 million domains. Each vendor counts links its own way, so the totals don't compare one to one.

**Stronger:** Semrush. It runs its own crawler, and its link data sits next to its keyword and audit data. LinkMiner is fine for quick checks of a competitor's top links.

SERPWatcher is simple to set up and tracks any number of domains within your keyword limit. Weekly updates work for small sites that publish now and then. If a ranking drop costs you money, you'll want daily data.

Mangools has no crawler for technical SEO. It offers a free SEO Content Optimizer as a separate mini tool, but nothing that checks a whole site for broken links or redirects. Semrush includes Site Audit on every paid plan.

**Stronger:** Semrush, on both counts. Daily data catches drops sooner, and a site audit is a core SEO tool that Mangools doesn't offer.

## Mangools vs Semrush AI visibility tracking

This is the one area where Mangools gives more on its entry plan.

Every Mangools plan includes AI Search Watcher PRO with 500 monitored prompts and unlimited monitors. It runs each prompt twice to smooth out changes in AI answers. It covers ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Gemini, Claude, Grok, Mistral and Llama. It shows your average position and citations next to your competitors. The pricing page doesn't say how often prompts are checked (check).

Semrush's SEO plan includes AI visibility reports for any domain and AI sentiment, with no prompt tracking quota. Prompt tracking starts on Starter:

- **Starter ($199/mo):** 50 prompts daily. The help page lists Google Search, ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini.
- **Pro+ ($299/mo):** 100 prompts daily.
- **Advanced:** Unlimited prompts on the prices page, 200 on the help page (check).
- **AI Visibility Toolkit:** $99 a month on its own, with 25 prompts and 1 domain and no free trial.

Semrush adds prompt research, which shows topics and prompts people ask AI. It also has an AI-ready Site Audit.

**Stronger:** Mangools for AI coverage on a small budget, with more AIs and a longer prompt list on every plan. Semrush for a daily update schedule and Perplexity coverage, with AI data tied to its SEO research.

## Which should you choose: Mangools or Semrush?

**Pick Mangools if:**

- You want keyword research that is quick to learn.

**Pick Semrush if:**

## Mangools vs Semrush FAQ

### Is Mangools cheaper than Semrush?

Yes. Mangools Basic is $61 a month, or $37.70 a month billed yearly. Semrush's SEO plan is $139 a month, or $117.33 a month billed yearly. Semrush includes site audits and daily rankings, which Mangools Basic doesn't.

### Is Mangools good for beginners?

Yes. Mangools keeps a simple design, and each tool does one job. KWFinder shows keyword ideas and difficulty on one screen. A free account lets you try it with 5 lookups a day.

### Does Mangools have a site audit?

No. Mangools has no site crawler for technical SEO. If you need to find broken links or crawl errors across a site, use Semrush or a separate audit tool.

### Does Mangools track ChatGPT?

Yes. Every paid plan includes AI Search Watcher PRO, which tracks ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, AI Mode, Gemini, Claude, Grok, Mistral and Llama. It monitors up to 500 prompts.

### Can Mangools replace Semrush?

For keyword research and AI answer tracking, many small sites can switch. You lose site audits and Semrush's larger databases, and rankings update weekly below the Agency plan. Agencies and technical SEOs usually need more than Mangools offers.

## Summary: Mangools vs Semrush

Mangools is the easier and cheaper choice for keyword research, and its plans now include AI answer tracking across 8 AIs. It fits bloggers and small businesses that don't need site audits or daily rankings.

Semrush is the stronger suite for teams that do technical SEO and research competitors in depth. Start with Mangools' free account and Semrush's 7-day trial, then compare them on the keywords you care about.
