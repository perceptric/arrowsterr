# Mangools vs Semrush: Features, AI Tracking and Pricing Compared in 2026

Mangools and Semrush both cover keyword research, backlink checks, rank tracking and AI answer tracking. They are built for different people. Mangools is a small, simple bundle of tools with KWFinder at the center. Every plan also includes its AI Search Watcher for tracking brand mentions in AI answers. Semrush is a large suite with far more data, site audits, daily rank tracking and add-on toolkits for most of marketing.

Here is the quick answer. Pick Mangools if you run a blog or small business site and want easy keyword research and AI answer tracking at a low price. Pick Semrush if you run SEO for a growing business or agency and need site audits and deep competitor data.

### What Mangools does today

[Mangools](https://mangools.com/) is a set of SEO tools sold as one bundle. KWFinder finds keywords and shows their difficulty. SERPChecker breaks down the Google results for a keyword, and SERPWatcher tracks your rankings. LinkMiner checks backlinks, and SiteProfiler shows the main SEO metrics for any domain. AI Search Watcher tracks how AI answers mention your brand.

You can't buy one tool alone. Mangools says its tools depend on each other, such as KWFinder's difficulty score coming from SERPChecker. Limits reset every 24 hours.

### Mangools history

Peter Hrbáčik launched the first version of KWFinder in August 2014. He still runs the company as CEO. KWFinder 2.0 came out in March 2016, and work on SERPChecker began a month later. In May 2016 the tools became one brand under the name Mangools. That September the team opened an office in Bratislava.

New tools followed each year. SERPWatcher arrived in July 2017 for rank tracking. LinkMiner came in January 2018 for backlinks, and SiteProfiler joined in September 2018 as the fifth tool. Mangools also released an SEO browser extension in October 2018, then KWFinder 3.0 with a new design in May 2019.

Mangools has grown without outside investors. By 2024 it had 1.1 million users and 25,000 paying customers. More recently it added AI Search Watcher, which tracks brand mentions in ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and six other AIs. Every plan now includes it, along with API and MCP access.

### What Semrush does today

[Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/) is a cloud marketing suite built around SEO. Its main plans combine the SEO Toolkit and the AI Visibility Toolkit. The SEO plan covers SEO alone. Starter, Pro+ and Advanced add AI search tracking, and Semrush sells them under the Semrush One name.

Semrush also sells add-on toolkits for traffic and market data, local SEO, content, social, ads and AI PR. Each plan includes one user.

### Semrush history

Oleg Shchegolev and Dmitri Melnikov founded Semrush in 2008. Its roots go back to Seodigger and SeoQuake, a Firefox add-on that showed SEO data in the browser. The name comes from SEM, short for search engine marketing. In December 2020 the company changed its styling from "SEMrush" to "Semrush."

Semrush raised $40 million in 2018 in a round co-led by Greycroft, E.ventures and Siguler Guff. It went public on the New York Stock Exchange in March 2021 under the ticker SEMR. It then bought other marketing companies, including Backlinko in 2022 and Exploding Topics and Third Door Media in 2024.

AI search came next. Semrush launched its AI Toolkit in March 2025 to track brands in ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity. In October 2025 it launched Semrush One, which joins the SEO Toolkit and the AI Visibility Toolkit in one plan. Adobe agreed to buy Semrush in November 2025 for $1.9 billion and closed the deal on April 28, 2026. Semrush now runs as part of Adobe.

## Mangools vs Semrush FAQ

### Is Mangools cheaper than Semrush?

Yes. Mangools Basic is $61 a month, or $37.70 a month billed yearly. Semrush's SEO plan is $139 a month, or $117.33 a month billed yearly. Semrush includes site audits and daily rankings, which Mangools Basic doesn't.

### Is Mangools good for beginners?

Yes. Mangools keeps a simple design, and each tool does one job. KWFinder shows keyword ideas and difficulty on one screen. A free account lets you try it with 5 lookups a day.

### Does Mangools have a site audit?

No. Mangools has no site crawler for technical SEO. If you need to find broken links or crawl errors across a site, use Semrush or a separate audit tool.

### Does Mangools track ChatGPT?

Yes. Every paid plan includes AI Search Watcher PRO, which tracks ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, AI Mode, Gemini, Claude, Grok, Mistral and Llama. It monitors up to 500 prompts and checks them once a week.

### Can Mangools replace Semrush?

For keyword research and AI answer tracking, many small sites can switch. You lose site audits and Semrush's larger databases, and rankings update weekly below the Agency plan. Agencies and technical SEOs usually need more than Mangools offers.

### Who makes Mangools?

Mangools is a Slovak company led by its founder and CEO, Peter Hrbáčik. He launched KWFinder in 2014, and the tools took the Mangools name in 2016.

## Summary: Mangools vs Semrush

Mangools is the easier and cheaper choice for keyword research, and its plans now include AI answer tracking across 8 AIs. It fits bloggers and small businesses that don't need site audits or daily rankings.

Semrush is the stronger suite for teams that do technical SEO and research competitors in depth. Start with Mangools' free account and Semrush's 7-day trial, then compare them on the keywords you care about.
