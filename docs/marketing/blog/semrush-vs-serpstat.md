# Semrush vs Serpstat: SEO Data, AI Tracking and Pricing Compared in 2026

Semrush and Serpstat are both all-in-one SEO platforms. Each covers keyword research, competitor analysis, backlinks, site audits and rank tracking. Semrush has the larger data set and more tools. Serpstat costs less to start and includes more seats and API access on its team plans.

The quick answer: pick Semrush if you want the biggest keyword and backlink data and a free plan to start. Pick Serpstat if you want a lower entry price, several users on one plan, and API access without a big contract.

## Semrush vs Serpstat: main points

- **Semrush has much bigger published data.** It lists 28.8 billion keywords and over 43 trillion backlinks. Serpstat lists 8.62 billion keywords and 529 billion backlinks.
- **Serpstat starts lower.** Its pricing page lists the Individual plan at $50 a month. Semrush's SEO plan is $139 a month.
- **Serpstat includes more seats.** Team includes 3 users and Agency 30. Semrush charges from $45 a month for each extra user.
- **Both track AI answers.** Semrush tracks 50 prompts daily from its Starter plan. Serpstat includes 1,500 AI Visibility checks a month from its Team plan.
- **Semrush has a free plan.** Serpstat has a 7-day trial and no free plan.

## What is Semrush?

<!-- shot: semrush -->

[Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/) is one of the largest SEO suites. It covers keyword research, backlink analysis, technical site audits, rank tracking and content tools. Its current plans bundle SEO and AI search tracking under the name Semrush One. Traffic, local, content, social and ads tools are sold as extra toolkits. Adobe now owns it, and Adobe says it has over 28 million users.

### How Semrush started

Oleg Shchegolev and Dmitri Melnikov founded Semrush in 2008. Their first product was SeoDigger, which became SeoQuake, a Firefox add-on for SEO checks. The name comes from SEM, short for search engine marketing. The founders moved to the US in 2017, and the company is based in Boston.

### Funding, IPO and Adobe

Semrush raised $40 million in April 2018. It went public on the New York Stock Exchange in March 2021 under the ticker SEMR. It also grew by buying companies, such as Backlinko in 2022 and Brand24 in 2024.

Adobe agreed to buy Semrush in November 2025 for about $1.9 billion. The deal closed on April 28, 2026, and Semrush now runs as "Semrush, an Adobe company."

### AI search features

Semrush launched its AI Toolkit in March 2025 to track how ChatGPT and Gemini describe brands. In May 2025 it split the product into seven toolkits you can buy one at a time. In October 2025 it launched Semrush One, which puts SEO and AI visibility on one plan.

## What is Serpstat?

<!-- shot: serpstat -->

[Serpstat](https://serpstat.com/) is an all-in-one SEO platform. It covers keyword research, competitor analysis, backlinks, site audit and rank tracking. Its team plans add AI tools, AI Visibility checks, API access and an MCP connection for ChatGPT and Claude. It sells four self-serve plans, from Individual to Agency. Serpstat says more than 1.1 million people use it worldwide.

### How Serpstat started

Serpstat began in 2013 as an in-house keyword tool at the Netpeak agency. Oleg Salamakha created it, and Artem Borodatyuk, an IT entrepreneur and investor, co-founded it. The first team had five people and worked in Odesa, Ukraine.

In 2015 the tool became a separate SaaS product under the name Serpstat. It has run on its own since then, as part of the FRACTAL group. By 2016 it was a full SEO platform with five core tools.

### How the product grew

Serpstat added API access in 2017. In 2018 it added keyword clustering and text analytics. In 2019 it added local Google databases for more than 100 regions. It launched its own backlink index in 2023. In 2024 it released Rank Tracking 2.0, and its user count passed 1.1 million.

Its newer plans add AI features. Team and up now include AI Visibility checks, an AI Overview Brand Opportunities report and an MCP connection for AI assistants.

## Semrush vs Serpstat FAQ

### Is Serpstat cheaper than Semrush?

Yes. Serpstat lists its Individual plan at $50 a month and Team at $100. Semrush's SEO plan is $139 a month. Serpstat also includes more users on its team plans.

### Is Serpstat as accurate as Semrush?

Both tools estimate volumes and traffic, so neither matches your own analytics exactly. Semrush has the larger data set, with 28.8 billion keywords against Serpstat's 8.62 billion. Test both on a site whose real numbers you know.

### Does Serpstat have a free plan?

No. Serpstat offers a 7-day free trial on its Individual and Team plans, with a $1 card check that is refunded. Semrush has a free plan with 10 reports a day.

### Does Serpstat track AI answers like Semrush?

Yes. Serpstat's Team plan and up include 1,500 AI Visibility checks a month across ChatGPT, Grok, Gemini and other models. Semrush tracks 50 prompts daily on its Starter plan, across ChatGPT, Gemini and Google's AI answers.

### Which is better for agencies, Semrush or Serpstat?

Serpstat's Agency plan includes 30 users, white label reports and API access. Semrush offers more data and tools, and charges for each extra user. Serpstat suits small agencies on a budget. Semrush suits agencies that need the deepest data.

## Summary: Semrush vs Serpstat

Semrush is the stronger tool for data. It has far bigger keyword and backlink databases, daily AI prompt tracking and a free plan. Serpstat is the stronger value for teams. It starts lower, includes more users and API access, and sets clear audit and tracking limits.

If your SEO work leans on links and deep research, choose Semrush. If you need a capable suite for a small team on a tight budget, choose Serpstat.
