# Semrush vs Serpstat: SEO Data, AI Tracking and Pricing Compared in 2026

Semrush and Serpstat are both all-in-one SEO platforms. Each covers keyword research, competitor analysis, backlinks, site audits and rank tracking. Semrush has the larger data set and more tools. Serpstat costs less to start and includes more seats and API access on its team plans.

The quick answer: pick Semrush if you want the biggest keyword and backlink data and a free plan to start. Pick Serpstat if you want a lower entry price, several users on one plan, and API access without a big contract.

## Semrush vs Serpstat: main points

- **Semrush has much bigger published data.** It lists 28.8 billion keywords and over 43 trillion backlinks. Serpstat lists 8.62 billion keywords and 529 billion backlinks.
- **Serpstat starts lower.** Its Individual plan is listed at $50 a month (check billing). Semrush's SEO plan is $139 a month.
- **Serpstat includes more seats.** Team includes 3 users and Agency 30. Semrush charges from $45 a month for each extra user.
- **Both track AI answers.** Semrush tracks 50 prompts daily from its Starter plan. Serpstat includes 1,500 AI Visibility checks a month from its Team plan.
- **Semrush has a free plan.** Serpstat has a 7-day trial and no free plan.

## What is Semrush?

[Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/) is one of the largest SEO suites. It covers keyword research, backlink analysis, technical site audits, rank tracking and content tools. Its current plans bundle SEO and AI search tracking under the name Semrush One. Traffic, local, content, social and ads tools are sold as extra toolkits.

## What is Serpstat?

[Serpstat](https://serpstat.com/) is an all-in-one SEO platform. It covers keyword research, competitor analysis, backlinks, site audit and rank tracking. Its team plans add AI tools, AI Visibility checks, API access and an MCP connection for ChatGPT and Claude. It sells four self-serve plans, from Individual to Agency.

## Semrush vs Serpstat keyword research

Semrush lists 28.8 billion keywords across 142 country databases. Its keyword tools show volume, difficulty, intent and SERP features. You also get keyword gaps and topic research.

Serpstat lists 8.62 billion keywords and 5.6 billion keyword suggestions. It covers 230 countries and 2.21 billion Google SERPs. Its plans limit how many searches you run a day: 100 on Individual, 500 on Team and 5,000 on Agency. Results per report run from 2,000 on Individual to 100,000 on Agency.

**Stronger: Semrush.** Its keyword database is more than three times the size of Serpstat's, which matters most in smaller markets and long-tail research. Serpstat covers more countries by count and is enough for many sites.

## Semrush vs Serpstat backlink data

Semrush's backlink index holds over 43 trillion links and updates daily. Backlink analysis, gaps and audits are part of every SEO plan.

Serpstat lists 529 billion backlinks and 1.5 billion domains. Backlink reports show up to 2,000 results on Individual and 10,000 on the other plans.

**Stronger: Semrush.** Its link index is far larger, so it finds more of the links pointing to you and your competitors. If links are a big part of your SEO work, this is the main reason to pick Semrush.

## Semrush vs Serpstat site audit and rank tracking

Serpstat sets clear audit limits. Individual audits 30,000 pages a month, Team 150,000 and Agency 1.5 million. JavaScript rendering comes with Team and up. For rank tracking, Individual includes 10,000 keyword position checks a month and Team includes 50,000.

Semrush's Site Audit crawls your site and lists technical issues. Its Starter plan adds an AI-ready site audit. For rank tracking, the SEO plan tracks 500 keywords daily across 5 websites, and Pro+ tracks 1,500 across 15. Rankings update daily across Google, Bing and Baidu.

**Stronger: close.** Serpstat gives larger audit and tracking limits for the price, and states them clearly. Semrush tracks more search engines and has more reports built on its rank data.

## Semrush vs Serpstat AI visibility tracking

Semrush's AI tracking comes with its Starter plan and up. Starter tracks 50 prompts daily, Pro+ tracks 100, and Advanced raises the limit further. Semrush names ChatGPT and Gemini, plus Google AI Overviews and AI Mode. Its pricing page also shows Perplexity (check). You can also buy the AI Visibility Toolkit alone for $99 a month, with 25 prompts and one user.

Serpstat's AI Visibility comes with Team, Team x2 and Agency. Each includes 1,500 AI Visibility checks a month. It tracks your brand across ChatGPT, Grok, Gemini and other AI models. Extra checks cost $20 per 1,000, with a $40 minimum top-up. Team and up also add an AI Overview Brand Opportunities report.

**Stronger: Semrush** for AI tracking that runs every day next to your SEO data. Serpstat is a good fit for teams that want to spot-check AI answers as part of a lower-priced plan.

## Semrush vs Serpstat team seats and API

Semrush plans with the SEO Toolkit charge from $45 a month for each extra user. API access comes with the Advanced plan.

Serpstat includes 1 user on Individual, 3 on Team, 6 on Team x2 and 30 on Agency. API access starts on Team, with 200,000 API credits a month. Agency adds white label reports and 2 million API credits. Team and up also connect to ChatGPT and Claude through MCP.

**Stronger: Serpstat.** It includes more people and API access on its lower plans. This matters for small agencies and for teams that pull SEO data into their own reports.

## Semrush vs Serpstat FAQ

### Is Serpstat cheaper than Semrush?

Yes. Serpstat lists its Individual plan at $50 a month and Team at $100. Semrush's SEO plan is $139 a month. Serpstat also includes more users on its team plans.

### Is Serpstat as accurate as Semrush?

Both tools estimate volumes and traffic, so neither matches your own analytics exactly. Semrush has the larger data set, with 28.8 billion keywords against Serpstat's 8.62 billion. Test both on a site whose real numbers you know.

### Does Serpstat have a free plan?

No. Serpstat offers a 7-day free trial on its Individual and Team plans, with a $1 card check that is refunded. Semrush has a free plan with 10 reports a day.

### Does Serpstat track AI answers like Semrush?

Yes. Serpstat's Team plan and up include 1,500 AI Visibility checks a month across ChatGPT, Grok, Gemini and other models. Semrush tracks 50 prompts daily on its Starter plan, across ChatGPT, Gemini and Google's AI answers.

### Which is better for agencies, Semrush or Serpstat?

Serpstat's Agency plan includes 30 users, white label reports and API access. Semrush offers more data and tools, and charges for each extra user. Serpstat suits small agencies on a budget. Semrush suits agencies that need the deepest data.

## Summary: Semrush vs Serpstat

Semrush is the stronger tool for data. It has far bigger keyword and backlink databases, daily AI prompt tracking and a free plan. Serpstat is the stronger value for teams. It starts lower, includes more users and API access, and sets clear audit and tracking limits.

If your SEO work leans on links and deep research, choose Semrush. If you need a capable suite for a small team on a tight budget, choose Serpstat.
