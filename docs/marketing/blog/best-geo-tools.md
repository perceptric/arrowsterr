# Best GEO Tools for Generative Engine Optimization

Generative engine optimization, or GEO, is the work of getting AI assistants to name and cite your brand. ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews now answer many questions that once sent people to a list of links. If your brand is in the answer, you get the click and the trust.

GEO is more than one task. You need to know where you stand. You need a site AI can read. You need pages that answer buyer questions, and you need other sites to mention you. Few tools do all of it, so most teams mix 2 or 3.

This guide groups the best GEO tools by the job each one does, with prices from October 2026. We make Arrowsterr, so it's on this list. Every tool here has a trial or free plan you can test before you pay.

## What GEO tools do: 4 jobs

### Job 1: Track AI answers

You can't fix what you can't see. Tracking tools run buyer prompts on AI assistants and record which brands get named, in what order and with what tone. They also show which pages AI cites. This is the base of every GEO plan.

### Job 2: Make your site readable to AI

AI crawlers like GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot and others need access to your pages. A firewall rule or a line in robots.txt can block them without anyone noticing. Clear structure and schema markup help AI read what each page is about. An llms.txt file helps too.

### Job 3: Create pages that answer the prompts

AI cites pages that answer the question asked. If buyers ask "best [category] for [team]", you need a page that answers it. GEO tools help you find these questions, plan the pages and write them.

### Job 4: Earn mentions on the sites AI cites

Much of what AI says comes from other sites: review sites, listicles, forums and news. Getting listed on the pages AI already cites is often the fastest way into an answer. Good GEO tools show you which sites those are.

## A GEO workflow you can run every month

Tools only help if you use them on a rhythm. Here is a monthly loop that fits most teams.

### Week 1: measure

Pull your mention rate and average position for each AI. Compare them with last month and with your top 3 rivals. Mark the 3 topics where you lost the most ground.

### Week 2: check the site

Run a crawler check after any site change. Look at the pages AI cites for your weak topics. Are they yours? If yes, make sure they carry schema and answer the question in the first paragraph.

### Week 3: publish

Write or update 2 to 4 pages aimed at your weakest prompts. Lead with a direct answer. Add real numbers, a comparison table and an FAQ. Link each page from 2 related pages on your site.

### Week 4: earn mentions

Open the list of third-party sites AI cites most. Reach out to the 5 that don't list you yet. Ask for a correction or offer fresh data. Update your own review profiles too. Then let the checks run. Changes in AIs that search the web can show within weeks.

## FAQ

### What is GEO?

GEO, or generative engine optimization, is the work of getting AI assistants to name and cite your brand in their answers. It covers tracking, site readiness, content and outside mentions.

### Is GEO different from SEO?

They overlap a lot. Both need crawlable pages and helpful content. GEO adds tracking of AI answers and a bigger focus on the third-party pages AI cites.

### Do I need a separate GEO tool if I have an SEO suite?

Many SEO suites now include some AI tracking. Dedicated GEO tools usually track more prompts on more AIs, and check them more often. Pick based on how much of your traffic and pipeline comes from AI.

### What does llms.txt do?

llms.txt is a plain text file at the root of your site. It lists your key pages with short notes, so AI tools can find what matters. It is easy to add, and support for it is still growing.

### How long does GEO take to work?

AIs that search the web can pick up a new page within days or weeks. Changes on review sites and in press take 1 to 3 months to show up broadly.

### Which AI should GEO focus on first?

ChatGPT, then Google AI Overviews and AI Mode. Together they reach most buyers. Add the rest after, starting with Gemini.

### Can I do GEO for free?

You can start. Free crawler checks, an llms.txt file, schema markup and Arrowsterr's free plan with 10 weekly prompts cover the basics. Paid tools add more prompts on more AIs, checked daily.
