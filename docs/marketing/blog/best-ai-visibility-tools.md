## What is an AI visibility tool?

An AI visibility tool tracks how often AI assistants mention your brand when people ask questions about your category. You may also see them called AI rank trackers, LLM trackers or GEO tools, and they all do roughly the same job.

You give the tool a list of prompts, like “best CRM for real estate agents.” The tool asks those prompts in ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity and Google’s AI Overviews, then records:

- whether your brand was named,
- where it showed up in the list,
- how warmly the AI described it,
- which competitors were named instead,
- which websites the AI cited as sources.

It works like rank tracking for answers. Google rankings show where your page sits on a results page, and AI visibility shows whether your brand appears in the answer.

## Why AI visibility is hard to measure

AI answers work differently from a page of search results in four ways.

### AI answers change between runs

If you ask ChatGPT the same question twice, you might get 2 different lists. The models vary their answers a little each time and get updated often, so the pattern only shows up across many checks.

### AI assistants don’t share query data

Google Search Console shows which queries showed your site and how often people clicked. AI assistants don’t share that data, so you have to work out which questions your buyers ask and test them yourself.

### Small wording changes can change the answer

“Best CRM for agencies” and “which CRM should a small agency use” ask the same thing, and they can still get different answers with different brands. The most useful tools track groups of related prompts, called topics, and report on the whole group.

### AI answers depend on the sources they cite

When AI assistants search the web before answering, they pull from a handful of pages: review sites, Reddit threads, listicles and brand websites. When those pages mention you, the answer is more likely to mention you too. A good tool shows you those sources so you know where to get mentioned.

## How AI visibility tools work

Almost every tool follows the same 4 steps.

1.  It sends your prompts to each AI, through its API or through a browser that acts like a real user. Daily runs give the clearest view, since answers move often.
2.  It reads every answer and pulls out each brand named, in order. It matches spelling variations, so “HubSpot CRM” and “HubSpot” count as one brand.
3.  It scores the results. Most tools roll the answers into visibility (the share of answers that name you), position, sentiment and citations.
4.  It tracks the trend. Because answers move around, the trend over 7, 30 and 90 days tells you more than any single day.

To find prompts worth tracking, we use this method with every client. Export your queries from Google Search Console, paste them into a Google Sheet, and filter for questions with this regex:

    \b(who|what|where|when|why|how|which|can|do|does|is|are|should|best|top|vs)\b

Long questions that already bring you Google traffic are often the same questions people now ask AI. You’ll end up with prompts like “Which CRM do most realtors use?” or “Is HubSpot or Pipedrive better for a small sales team?”

## What to look for in an AI visibility tool

Compare these points before you pick a tool.

- A free trial or free plan lets you test the tool on your own prompts.
- Prompt suggestions save you hours, since choosing what to track takes the most thought. Look for tools that research your brand and suggest prompts.
- Coverage should match the models your buyers use. Start with ChatGPT, add Google AI Overviews and AI Mode if you get Google traffic, and then add Perplexity, Gemini and Claude. Check whether any of these cost extra.
- A sources report shows which sites to work on next, while visibility numbers show where you stand.
- Some tools turn gaps into a content plan or a list of sites to get mentioned on, which gives your team clear next steps.

## How to get your brand recommended by AI

Tracking comes first. These are the steps we take once the data comes in.

### How query fan-out shapes AI answers

When you ask an AI assistant a question, it often splits it into several smaller searches before it answers. This is called [query fan-out](https://www.semrush.com/blog/query-fan-out/). Ask “what’s the best CRM for a small agency?” and the model might also search for “CRM pricing for small teams,” “CRM with client portals” and “HubSpot alternatives.”

It then combines the most useful parts of each search into one answer, which is why pages with almost no Google traffic can still get cited. Ahrefs’ Ryan Law looked at the 1,000 pages ChatGPT cited most and found that [28.3% of them had zero organic traffic](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/thinkingslow_5-reasons-content-can-rank-in-chatgpt-activity-7389639341452713985-xEBD). If your page answers one of those smaller questions clearly, it can earn the citation.

### Get mentioned on the sites AI assistants cite

Open the sources report in your tool and list the sites AI cites most for your topics. In most B2B categories, you’ll see the same mix: G2 and Capterra, a few Reddit threads, 2 or 3 “best tools” listicles and some brand sites.

Ask customers for reviews on G2 and Capterra, take part in the Reddit conversations and reach out to the authors of those listicles. A new mention on a source AI trusts can lift your visibility within weeks.

### Write the pages buyers ask AI about

Look at the prompts where competitors get named and you don’t. They usually point to a handful of missing pages:

- “Best \[category\] for \[audience\]” pages
- “\[Your brand\] vs \[competitor\]” comparisons
- “\[Competitor\] alternatives” pages
- Clear pricing and feature pages that AI can quote

Write them with plain facts AI can quote, such as who the product is for, what it costs, what it does better and real numbers. AI quotes specific facts more often than general marketing copy.

### Refresh pages ranking 4 to 20 on Google

Pages at positions 4 to 20 on Google are often the quickest to improve. AI assistants often search the web before they answer, so climbing a few spots there can also lift you in AI answers. Update those pages with fresh data, clearer headings and direct answers to the questions in your prompts.

### Track your AI visibility after each change

Give every change 2 to 4 weeks, then check your visibility trend for that topic and do more of what works.

## AI visibility tools FAQ

### Is AI visibility the same as SEO?

They overlap a lot. Strong SEO pages often get cited by AI too. AI visibility also depends on what other sites say about you, and on how clearly your pages answer specific questions.

### How many prompts should you track?

Start with 25 to 50 prompts spread across 5 or so topics. That is enough to see patterns while keeping the data easy to review. Add more once you know which topics matter most.

### How often should you check?

Daily checks are best, because answers shift so often. Judge your progress on trends over 30 days, since single days vary a lot.

### Which AI assistants matter most?

ChatGPT has the most users, so start there. If you get traffic from Google, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode matter just as much. Then add Perplexity, Gemini and Claude.
