# Semrush vs Similarweb: SEO, Traffic Data and AI Search Compared in 2026

Semrush is an SEO suite that also sells traffic data, as an extra toolkit. Similarweb is a traffic and market intelligence platform that adds SEO tools on its larger plans. Each one estimates how other websites perform, from a different starting point.

Pick Semrush if most of your week goes to keyword research, backlinks, site audits and rank tracking. Pick Similarweb if it goes to sizing competitors and studying their traffic sources and ads. Either one can now track how AI answers mention your brand.

## Semrush vs Similarweb: main points

- Semrush is the better SEO tool. It has 28.8 billion keywords and over 43 trillion backlinks, and its SEO plans add site audit and daily rank tracking.
- Similarweb is the better traffic tool. Its estimates come from four sources, including first party analytics from websites that connect it.
- Semrush sells traffic data as its own toolkit. Traffic & Market Pro costs $289 a month for one seat.
- Similarweb's AI search tracking starts at $99. That plan tracks 150 prompts for one user, with AI traffic data and citation analysis.
- Semrush has a free plan with 10 reports a day. Similarweb's free site shows a high level sample, and each tool has a free trial.

## What is Semrush?

[Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/) is one of the largest SEO suites, with over 28 million users. You get keyword research, backlink analysis, technical site audits, rank tracking and content tools. ### How Semrush started

Oleg Shchegolev and Dmitri Melnikov founded Semrush in 2008. The product began as SeoDigger and then became SeoQuake, a Firefox extension for SEO checks. The name Semrush comes from SEM, short for search engine marketing. The founders moved to the US in 2017, and the company is based in Boston.

### Funding, IPO and Adobe

Semrush raised $40 million in April 2018. It went public on the New York Stock Exchange in March 2021 under the ticker SEMR. Bill Wagner took over as CEO in March 2025, and Shchegolev became CTO.

Adobe agreed to buy Semrush in November 2025 for about $1.9 billion. The deal closed on April 28, 2026. Semrush now runs as "Semrush, an Adobe company."

### From keyword tool to AI search

Semrush grew from a keyword tool into a full marketing suite, partly by buying other companies. It bought Backlinko in 2022. In 2024 it bought Third Door Media, the owner of Search Engine Land, along with Brand24.

The AI work started in March 2025, when Semrush launched its AI Toolkit to track how ChatGPT and Gemini describe brands. In May 2025 it split the product into seven toolkits you can buy one at a time. Semrush One followed in October 2025 and put SEO and AI visibility on one plan.

## What is Similarweb?

<!-- shot: similarweb -->

[Similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com/) is a digital market intelligence platform. It estimates traffic and engagement for websites and apps, and shows where that traffic comes from. Its plans group tools into suites for competitive intel, SEO, ads and generative AI. Larger companies buy a custom package through sales. Similarweb has over 1,000 employees and offices on six continents.

### How Similarweb started

Or Offer founded Similarweb in Tel Aviv in 2007. He first built a web analytics tool to study competitors while he tried to grow a jewelry business. That tool became Similarweb. In 2009 it won the first Israeli Seedcamp, which brought it early attention from investors and the press.

### Funding, IPO and acquisitions

Naspers led an $18 million round in Similarweb in February 2014. The company listed on the New York Stock Exchange in May 2021 under the ticker SMWB.

Similarweb grew its product by buying smaller firms. Quettra brought mobile analytics in 2015. Rank Ranger, a rank tracking and SEO tool bought in 2022, helped build out the SEO suite. In 2025 Similarweb bought The Search Monitor, a paid search and affiliate tool.

### AI search features

In July 2025, Similarweb launched its GenAI Intelligence Toolkit. It has two parts. AI Brand Visibility shows where your brand appears in AI answers. AI Traffic shows how many visits AI platforms send to any site. In November 2025 it added more AI brand reports.

Traffic is the main reason people compare these two, and Similarweb wins it. Traffic data is its core product, and it is the only one of the two that uses direct measurement. Keep in mind that neither tool can show you a competitor's real analytics. Every number here is an estimate.

Similarweb builds its estimates from four sources. The first is direct measurement from the first party analytics of websites and apps that connect it. The second is anonymous data from its own products installed on devices. It adds public data and data from partners such as ISPs and measurement firms, then models it all into traffic estimates. You also get customer demographics and app data.

Semrush's traffic data comes from a panel of over 200 million anonymized internet users in more than 190 countries and regions, built with clickstream partners. A neural network combines that panel with Semrush's backlink and ranking data. The toolkit covers the main reports, and it makes the most sense if you already use Semrush for SEO.

## Semrush vs Similarweb FAQ

### Is Similarweb more accurate than Semrush for traffic?

Neither matches a site's own analytics exactly, since each one estimates traffic. Similarweb adds direct measurement from sites that connect their analytics to its panel and partner data. Semrush uses a clickstream panel and its own ranking data. To test them, compare both against a site whose real numbers you know.

### Does Semrush have traffic data like Similarweb?

Yes. Semrush sells the Traffic & Market toolkit, with traffic analytics, channel breakdowns, top pages and market views. It starts at $289 a month for one user and is sold apart from the standard SEO plans.

### Does Similarweb have SEO tools?

Yes. Similarweb has keyword research, keyword gaps, rank tracking, site audit and backlink analysis. On its AI search plans, these start with the $333 plan. Its keyword database is smaller than Semrush's.

### Which is better for AI search tracking, Semrush or Similarweb?

Similarweb includes 150 tracked prompts on every AI search plan and adds AI referral traffic. Semrush includes 50 prompts on its Starter plan and shows them next to your SEO data. Pick the one whose other tools you will use more.

### Does Semrush or Similarweb have a free plan?

Semrush has a free plan with 10 reports a day, plus a 7 day trial on most paid plans. Similarweb shows free, high level data on its website and has a free trial.

### Who owns Semrush and Similarweb?

Adobe bought Semrush, and the deal closed in April 2026. Similarweb is a public company listed on the New York Stock Exchange.

## Summary: Semrush vs Similarweb

If you spend your week on keywords and pages, choose Semrush. Its keyword and backlink data are bigger, and AI tracking sits on the same plan as your rankings.

If you spend it sizing markets and competitors, choose Similarweb. Its AI search plans start at $99 with 150 prompts and AI referral traffic, and traffic data is its core product.
