# Semrush vs Similarweb: SEO, Traffic Data and AI Search Compared in 2026

Semrush and Similarweb both estimate how other websites perform. They start from different places. Semrush is an SEO suite first, with traffic data sold as an extra toolkit. Similarweb is a market and traffic intelligence platform first, with SEO tools added on its larger plans.

The quick answer: pick Semrush if your main job is SEO, such as keyword research, backlinks, site audits and rank tracking. Pick Similarweb if your main job is market research, such as sizing competitors, studying their traffic sources and watching their ads. Both now track how AI answers mention your brand.

## Semrush vs Similarweb: main points

- **Semrush is stronger for SEO work.** Its pages list 28.8 billion keywords and over 43 trillion backlinks, plus site audit and daily rank tracking on its SEO plans.
- **Similarweb is stronger for traffic and market research.** It builds its estimates from four named sources, including first-party analytics from websites that connect it.
- **Semrush sells traffic data as a separate toolkit.** Traffic & Market Pro is $289 a month for one seat.
- **Similarweb sells AI search tracking from $99.** That plan includes 150 tracked prompts, AI traffic data and citation analysis for one user.
- **Semrush has a free plan.** It gives 10 reports a day. Similarweb's free site shows a high-level sample, and both offer free trials.

## What is Semrush?

[Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/) is one of the largest SEO suites. It covers keyword research, backlink analysis, technical site audits, rank tracking and content tools. Its current plans bundle SEO with AI search tracking under the name Semrush One. Traffic, local, content, social and ads tools are sold as separate toolkits.

## What is Similarweb?

[Similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com/) is a digital market intelligence platform. It estimates traffic and engagement for websites and apps, and shows where that traffic comes from. Its plans group tools into suites for competitive intel, SEO, ads and generative AI. Larger companies buy a custom package through sales.

## Semrush vs Similarweb traffic data

This is the main reason people compare the two tools. Both give you traffic estimates for any domain. Neither can show a competitor's real analytics.

Similarweb names four data sources. The first is direct measurement from the first-party analytics of websites and apps that connect it. The second is anonymous data from its own products installed on devices. It also uses public data and data from partners such as ISPs and measurement firms. It then models these inputs into traffic estimates.

Semrush says its traffic data comes from a panel of over 200 million anonymized internet users in more than 190 countries and regions. It builds this panel with clickstream partners. A neural network then combines the panel with Semrush's backlink and ranking data.

What you get in each tool:

| | Semrush Traffic & Market | Similarweb |
|---|---|---|
| Traffic and engagement | Traffic Analytics | Traffic & Engagement |
| Channel breakdown | Traffic Distribution, incl. AI traffic | Marketing Channels Overview |
| Top pages | Top Pages | Segment and page-level insights |
| Market view | Market Overview | Market research analysis |
| Competitor alerts | Competitor Monitoring | Competitor Alerts |
| History | Back to 2017 | Up to 37 months as an add-on |

**Stronger: Similarweb.** Traffic data is its core product, and it is the only one of the two that names direct measurement as a source. It also adds customer demographics and app data. Semrush's toolkit covers the main reports and is a good fit if you already use Semrush for SEO.

## Semrush vs Similarweb SEO and keyword research

Semrush lists 28.8 billion keywords across 142 country databases. Its SEO plans include keyword research, backlink analysis, site audit and daily rank tracking. The SEO plan tracks 500 keywords daily across 5 websites. Its backlink index holds over 43 trillion links and updates daily.

Similarweb lists over 6 billion keywords and 100 million websites. Its Strategic SEO Suite covers keyword research, keyword gap analysis, rank tracking and SERP analysis. Rank tracking also watches AI Overviews. On the AI search plans, SEO tools start with the $333 plan, which adds keyword research, rank tracking, and site audit and backlinks.

**Stronger: Semrush.** It has the bigger keyword and backlink data and more SEO tools on its entry plan. Similarweb's SEO tools are useful for spotting which keywords send competitors traffic. Most SEO teams will still want a dedicated SEO suite.

## Semrush vs Similarweb AI visibility tracking

Both tools now track how AI assistants answer questions about your market.

Semrush's AI tracking comes with the Starter plan and up. Starter tracks 50 prompts daily, Pro+ tracks 100, and Advanced raises the limit further. Semrush names ChatGPT and Gemini, plus Google AI Overviews and AI Mode. You can also buy the AI Visibility Toolkit alone for $99 a month. It includes 25 prompts and has no free trial, and each user needs their own subscription.

Similarweb's AI Brand Visibility tracker covers ChatGPT, Google AI Mode, Gemini and Perplexity. Its AI traffic tools also measure visits from Claude, Grok and Microsoft Copilot. Every AI search plan includes 150 tracked prompts, along with sentiment and citation analysis. Its AI Traffic report shows how many visits AI chats send to a site.

**Stronger: close.** Similarweb includes more prompts on its entry plan and pairs AI answers with AI referral traffic. Semrush puts AI tracking next to its SEO data, so you see Google rankings and AI answers in one place.

## Semrush vs Similarweb ads and competitor research

Semrush sells an Advertising Toolkit. Its Base plan covers advertising research and PLA research. Pro adds AdClarity, with data across 30+ regions.

Similarweb's Ads Intel Suite covers paid search, display and social ads, plus publisher analysis. On the AI search plans, ads data comes with the $542 plan. That plan adds ad spend, creative and campaign insights.

**Stronger: Similarweb** for broad market and ad research across channels. Semrush is a good fit when you mainly study competitors' Google Ads.

Similarweb also sells self-service packages for individuals and small teams on its main pricing page. See the pricing page for their current prices.

## Which should you choose: Semrush or Similarweb?

**Pick Semrush if:**

- SEO is your main channel and you need keyword research, backlinks and site audits.
- You want AI tracking next to your Google rankings.
- You want a free plan to start, or several people on one account.
- You only need traffic data now and then, and can add the toolkit later.

**Pick Similarweb if:**

- Your main job is market research or competitor benchmarking.
- You need traffic estimates for many sites, apps or whole industries.
- You want AI brand tracking with AI referral traffic from $99.
- You study competitors' ads across search, display and social.

Some larger teams use both: Semrush for SEO work and Similarweb for market research.

## Semrush vs Similarweb FAQ

### Is Similarweb more accurate than Semrush for traffic?

Both tools estimate traffic, so neither matches a site's own analytics exactly. Similarweb uses direct measurement from sites that connect their analytics, plus panel and partner data. Semrush uses a clickstream panel and its own ranking data. Compare both against a site whose real numbers you know.

### Does Semrush have traffic data like Similarweb?

Yes. Semrush sells the Traffic & Market toolkit, with traffic analytics, channel breakdowns, top pages and market views. It starts at $289 a month for one user. It is not part of the standard SEO plans.

### Does Similarweb have SEO tools?

Yes. Similarweb offers keyword research, keyword gaps, rank tracking, site audit and backlink analysis. On its AI search plans, these start with the $333 plan. Its keyword database is smaller than Semrush's.

### Which is better for AI search tracking, Semrush or Similarweb?

Similarweb includes 150 tracked prompts on every AI search plan and adds AI referral traffic. Semrush includes 50 prompts on its Starter plan and shows them next to your SEO data. Pick the one whose other tools you will use more.

### Does Semrush or Similarweb have a free plan?

Semrush has a free plan with 10 reports a day. Similarweb shows free, high-level data on its website and offers a free trial. Semrush also offers a 7-day trial on most paid plans.

## Summary: Semrush vs Similarweb

Semrush is the better pick for SEO. It has bigger keyword and backlink data, site audits and rank tracking, and AI tracking on the same plan. Similarweb is the better pick for traffic and market research. It also has an AI search plan with more prompts at a lower entry price.

Start from your main job. If you spend your week on keywords and pages, choose Semrush. If you spend it sizing markets and competitors, choose Similarweb.
