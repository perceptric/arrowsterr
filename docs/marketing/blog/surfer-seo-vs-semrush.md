# Surfer SEO vs Semrush: Content Tools, AI Tracking and Pricing in 2026

Surfer is a content tool. It scores each draft against the pages that rank, and it now tracks how AI answers mention your brand. Semrush is a full SEO suite with keyword data, backlinks, site audits and rank tracking, with AI visibility tools on top.

If your team writes and updates a lot of content and wants a score for every page, pick Surfer. If you need SEO research and link data in one login, pick Semrush. Many teams run both: Semrush for research, Surfer for writing.

## Surfer SEO vs Semrush: main points

- Surfer is built for writers. Every plan centers on the Content Editor and its Content Score, with an AI writing assistant beside them.
- Semrush's SEO plan holds the research tools: keyword research, backlink analysis, Site Audit and daily position tracking.
- AI tracking comes with each. Surfer Pro runs 50 prompts daily across 5 AIs. Semrush One Starter runs 50 daily, and the standalone AI Visibility Toolkit runs 25.
- Surfer starts lower. Surfer Discovery is $49 a month billed yearly. Semrush's SEO plan is $139 a month, or $117.33 billed yearly.
- You can start free on either. Surfer has a 7 day trial of Pro. Semrush has a Free plan and a 7 day trial of its paid plans.

### What Surfer does today

[Surfer](https://surfer.ai/) is a content optimization platform. It moved from surferseo.com to surfer.ai, where it now leads with AI visibility. You write in its Content Editor, and Surfer scores the draft on the terms and headings that top pages use.

Its plans also include an AI writing assistant called Surfy, an AI detector and humanizer, a plagiarism check and one click internal linking. The AI Tracker shows how often ChatGPT and other AIs mention your brand. More than 150,000 marketers and SEOs use Surfer, agencies among them.

### Surfer's history

Surfer began in Poland in 2017. Lucjan Suski built the first version as a tool for KS, a marketing agency run by Sławek Czajkowski. His brother Michał worked there as an SEO specialist. The agency took up the prototype within weeks, and the team turned it into a product.

In 2018 Surfer launched data backed content optimization and its first Content Score. That score became the heart of the product. In 2022 it added AI writing with Surfer AI. By 2026 it had moved to the surfer.ai domain and rebuilt its pitch around AI search visibility, with prompt tracking on most plans.

In October 2025, Positive Group bought Surfer. Positive, a French software group with about 400 staff, did not disclose the price. Surfer had about 12,000 customers at the time. Lucjan Suski stayed on as CEO, and Surfer now runs as Positive's AI search unit.

### What Semrush does today

[Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/) is a large SEO and marketing suite. Its SEO plan covers keyword research, competitor analysis, Position Tracking and Site Audit. Semrush sells AI tracking as the AI Visibility Toolkit, or bundled with SEO as Semrush One.

Semrush also sells toolkits for content, local, traffic and market data, social, ads and PR. Each one is priced on its own. More than 28 million people use Semrush.

### Semrush's history

Oleg Shchegolev and Dmitri Melnikov founded Semrush in 2008. It grew from a keyword tool into a full marketing suite. In March 2021 it went public on the New York Stock Exchange under the ticker SEMR.

Semrush then bought its way into new areas. It added Backlinko in 2022. In 2024 it bought Brand24 and Exploding Topics. That same year it bought Third Door Media, the publisher of Search Engine Land.

AI search came next. Semrush launched its AI Toolkit in March 2025. In October 2025 it launched Semrush One, which bundles SEO and AI visibility in one plan. In November 2025, Adobe agreed to buy Semrush for about $1.9 billion. The deal closed on April 28, 2026, and Semrush now sits inside Adobe's customer experience business.

## Which should you choose: Surfer SEO or Semrush?

Pick Surfer if:

- Your team publishes and refreshes articles every week.
- You want writers to hit a content score before a page goes live.
- You want AI tracking next to the editor, so you can act on a mention gap right away.
- Your budget starts under $100 a month.

Pick Semrush if:

- You need keyword research on any domain and competitor gaps.

## Surfer SEO vs Semrush FAQ

### Is Surfer better than Semrush for content?

Yes, for writing and scoring drafts. Surfer's Content Editor and Content Score are the core of the product. Semrush has a Content Toolkit and content optimization on Pro+, but its strength is research.

### Can Surfer replace Semrush?

Only for content work. Surfer has no backlink tools and no technical site crawler. If you need link data or a technical site audit, you still need a suite like Semrush.

### Which is cheaper, Surfer or Semrush?

Surfer's entry plan is lower. Discovery is $49 a month billed yearly, and Semrush's SEO plan is $139 a month or $117.33 billed yearly. Semrush also has a Free plan with 10 reports a day.

### Do Surfer and Semrush track ChatGPT?

Yes. Surfer tracks ChatGPT on Standard, and adds Perplexity, Gemini, AI Overviews and AI Mode on Pro. Semrush tracks ChatGPT in its AI Visibility Toolkit and Semrush One plans.

### Can you use Surfer and Semrush together?

Yes, and many teams do. They use Semrush to find keywords and check competitors, then write and score each page in Surfer.

## Summary: Surfer SEO vs Semrush

If you only write content, start with a Surfer trial. Put a reminder in for day 6, since the trial turns into a paid plan on day 7. If you run the whole SEO program, start with Semrush's Free plan or its 7 day trial, and add Surfer later if your writers want it.
