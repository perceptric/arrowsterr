# Surfer SEO vs Semrush: Content Tools, AI Tracking and Pricing in 2026

Surfer SEO and Semrush both help you win search traffic, but they start from different jobs. Surfer is a content tool. It scores each draft against the pages that rank and now tracks how AI answers mention your brand. Semrush is a full SEO suite with keyword data, backlinks, site audits and rank tracking, plus AI visibility tools on top.

The quick answer: pick Surfer if your team writes and updates a lot of content and wants a clear score for every page. Pick Semrush if you need SEO research and link data in one login. Many teams use both, with Semrush for research and Surfer for writing.

## Surfer SEO vs Semrush: main points

- **Surfer is built for writers.** Its Content Editor, Content Score and AI writing assistant sit at the center of every plan.
- **Semrush is built for SEO research.** Keyword research, backlink analysis, Site Audit and daily position tracking come with its SEO plan.
- **Both track AI answers.** Surfer Pro tracks 50 prompts daily across 5 AIs. Semrush One Starter tracks 50 prompts daily, and the standalone AI Visibility Toolkit tracks 25.
- **Surfer starts lower.** Surfer Discovery is $49 a month billed yearly. Semrush's SEO plan is $139 a month, or $117.33 billed yearly.
- **Both have a free way in.** Surfer offers a 7-day trial of Pro. Semrush has a Free plan and a 7-day trial of its paid plans.

### What Surfer does today

[Surfer](https://surfer.ai/) is a content optimization platform. It moved from surferseo.com to surfer.ai and now calls itself an AI visibility platform. You write in its Content Editor, and Surfer scores the draft on the terms and headings that top pages use.

Its plans also include an AI writing assistant called Surfy, an AI detector and humanizer, a plagiarism check and one-click internal linking. The AI Tracker shows how often ChatGPT and other AIs mention your brand. Surfer says more than 150,000 marketers, agencies and SEOs use it.

### Surfer's history

Surfer began in Poland in 2017. Lucjan Suski built the first version as a tool for KS, a marketing agency run by Sławek Czajkowski. His brother Michał worked there as an SEO specialist. The agency took up the prototype within weeks, and the team turned it into a product.

In 2018 Surfer launched its data-backed content optimization and its first Content Score. That score became the heart of the product. In 2022 it added AI writing with Surfer AI. By 2026 it had moved to the surfer.ai domain and rebuilt its pitch around AI search visibility, with prompt tracking on most plans.

In October 2025, Positive Group bought Surfer. Positive is a French software group with about 400 staff, and the price was not disclosed. At the time, Surfer had about 12,000 customers. Lucjan Suski stayed on as CEO, and Surfer now runs as Positive's AI search unit.

### What Semrush does today

[Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/) is a large SEO and marketing suite. Its SEO plan covers keyword research, competitor analysis, Position Tracking and Site Audit. Semrush sells AI tracking as the AI Visibility Toolkit, or bundled with SEO as Semrush One.

Semrush also sells toolkits for content, local, traffic and market data, social, ads and PR. Each one is priced on its own. Semrush says more than 28 million people use it.

### Semrush's history

Oleg Shchegolev and Dmitri Melnikov founded Semrush in 2008. It grew from a keyword tool into a full marketing suite. In March 2021 it went public on the New York Stock Exchange under the ticker SEMR.

Semrush then bought its way into new areas. It added Backlinko in 2022. In 2024 it bought Brand24 and Exploding Topics. That same year it bought Third Door Media, the publisher of Search Engine Land.

AI search came next. Semrush launched its AI Toolkit in March 2025. In October 2025 it launched Semrush One, which bundles SEO and AI visibility in one plan. In November 2025, Adobe agreed to buy Semrush for about $1.9 billion. The deal closed on April 28, 2026, and Semrush now sits inside Adobe's customer experience business.

## Which should you choose: Surfer SEO or Semrush?

Pick Surfer if:

- Your team publishes and refreshes articles every week.
- You want writers to hit a content score before a page goes live.
- You want AI tracking next to the editor, so you can act on a mention gap right away.
- Your budget starts under $100 a month.

Pick Semrush if:

- You need keyword research on any domain and competitor gaps.
- You need backlink data or a technical site audit.
- You report daily keyword positions to a boss or a client.
- You want SEO and AI visibility on one Semrush One plan.

## Surfer SEO vs Semrush FAQ

### Is Surfer better than Semrush for content?

Yes, for writing and scoring drafts. Surfer's Content Editor and Content Score are the core of the product. Semrush has a Content Toolkit and content optimization on Pro+, but its strength is research.

### Can Surfer replace Semrush?

Only for content work. Surfer's pricing page lists no backlink tools and no technical site crawler. If you need link data or a technical site audit, you still need a suite like Semrush.

### Which is cheaper, Surfer or Semrush?

Surfer's entry plan is lower. Discovery is $49 a month billed yearly, and Semrush's SEO plan is $139 a month or $117.33 billed yearly. Semrush also has a Free plan with 10 reports a day.

### Do Surfer and Semrush track ChatGPT?

Both do. Surfer tracks ChatGPT on Standard, and adds Perplexity, Gemini, AI Overviews and AI Mode on Pro. Semrush tracks ChatGPT in its AI Visibility Toolkit and Semrush One plans.

### Can you use Surfer and Semrush together?

Yes, and many teams do. They use Semrush to find keywords and check competitors, then write and score each page in Surfer.

## Summary: Surfer SEO vs Semrush

Surfer is the better pick for content teams. It gives writers a score to hit and AI tracking tied to the pages they write. Semrush is the better pick for SEO teams that need keyword and backlink data, with AI visibility in the Semrush One bundle.

If you only write content, start with a Surfer trial. If you run the whole SEO program, start with Semrush's Free plan or its 7-day trial and add Surfer later if your writers want it.
