# Surfer SEO vs Semrush: Content Tools, AI Tracking and Pricing in 2026

Surfer SEO and Semrush both help you win search traffic, but they start from different jobs. Surfer is a content tool. It scores each draft against the pages that rank and now tracks how AI answers mention your brand. Semrush is a full SEO suite with keyword data, backlinks, site audits and rank tracking, plus AI visibility tools on top.

The quick answer: pick Surfer if your team writes and updates a lot of content and wants a clear score for every page. Pick Semrush if you need SEO research and link data in one login. Many teams use both, with Semrush for research and Surfer for writing.

## Surfer SEO vs Semrush: main points

- **Surfer is built for writers.** Its Content Editor, Content Score and AI writing assistant sit at the center of every plan.
- **Semrush is built for SEO research.** Keyword research, backlink analysis, Site Audit and daily position tracking come with its SEO plan.
- **Both track AI answers.** Surfer Pro tracks 50 prompts daily across 5 AIs. Semrush One Starter tracks 50 prompts daily, and the standalone AI Visibility Toolkit tracks 25.
- **Surfer starts lower.** Surfer Discovery is $49 a month billed yearly. Semrush's SEO plan is $139 a month, or $117.33 billed yearly.
- **Both have a free way in.** Surfer offers a 7-day trial of Pro. Semrush has a Free plan and a 7-day trial of its paid plans.

## What is Surfer SEO?

[Surfer](https://surfer.ai/) is a content optimization platform. It moved from surferseo.com to surfer.ai and now calls itself an AI visibility platform. You write in its Content Editor, and Surfer scores the draft on the terms and headings that top pages use.

Its plans also include an AI writing assistant called Surfy, an AI detector and humanizer, a plagiarism check and one-click internal linking. The AI Tracker shows how often ChatGPT and other AIs mention your brand.

## What is Semrush?

[Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/) is a large SEO and marketing suite. Its SEO plan covers keyword research, competitor analysis, Position Tracking and Site Audit. Semrush sells AI tracking as the AI Visibility Toolkit, or bundled with SEO as Semrush One.

Semrush also sells toolkits for content, local, traffic and market data, social, ads and PR. Each one is priced on its own.

## Surfer SEO vs Semrush content tools

Surfer is stronger here. Content is the product, so every plan opens with the Content Editor and a document limit.

- **Content Editor and Content Score:** Surfer grades your draft live against the pages that rank. Writers get a number to aim for.
- **Surfy and humanizer:** The AI writing assistant drafts and rewrites inside the editor. The humanizer and AI detector check the tone.
- **Brand Knowledge and Custom Voices:** You can feed Surfer facts about your brand and set a writing voice.
- **Integrations:** Standard connects to WordPress and Google Docs. Pro adds Contentful, and Peace of Mind adds Zapier.

Semrush has content tools too. Semrush One Pro+ adds content optimization. The separate Content Toolkit is $60 a month billed yearly, with Topic Finder, an SEO Brief Generator, 5 SEO content boosts a month and AI writing in your brand voice.

Pick Surfer if your writers live in a content editor every day. Semrush's content tools are fine for briefs and quick checks, but they are one part of a bigger suite.

## Surfer SEO vs Semrush keyword research

Semrush is stronger here. Keyword research is one of its core tools, and the SEO plan includes it next to competitor analysis. You can look up any domain to see the keywords it ranks for, then compare it with rivals.

Surfer's plan grid lists Keyword Research, a Topical Map and a SERP Analyzer. These help you plan clusters of articles and see what top pages cover. They work well for content planning. They are lighter than Semrush for market research, such as checking a competitor's whole keyword set.

## Surfer SEO vs Semrush backlink data and site audit

Semrush wins this one. Surfer's pricing page lists no backlink tools at all.

| | Surfer | Semrush |
|---|---|---|
| Backlink analysis | Not listed | Yes, in the SEO suite |
| Technical site audit | Not listed as a site crawler | Site Audit on the SEO plan |
| AI crawler checks | Not listed | AI Search Site Audit flags issues that block AI bots |
| Content checks | Content Audit, Cannibalization Report (Pro) | Keyword cannibalization analysis (Pro+) |

Surfer's Content Audit looks at how your existing pages perform and which ones to refresh. That is useful work. It does not replace a technical crawl that finds broken links and blocked pages.

## Surfer SEO vs Semrush rank tracking

Semrush is stronger for classic rank tracking. Its SEO plan and Semrush One Starter track 500 keywords daily on 5 websites. Pro+ tracks 1,500 keywords on 15 sites, with multi-location and device tracking. Advanced starts at 5,000 keywords.

Surfer tracks pages. Discovery covers 10 pages, Standard 50, Pro 200 and Peace of Mind 500. Its Ranking Drop Alerts tell you when a tracked page loses ground. That fits a content team that wants to know which article needs a refresh. It is a weaker fit for an SEO team that reports keyword positions to clients each week.

## Surfer SEO vs Semrush AI visibility tracking

This is the closest contest. Both tools track prompts and show how AI answers mention your brand.

| | Surfer | Semrush |
|---|---|---|
| Entry plan with AI tracking | Standard: 25 prompts, weekly, ChatGPT only | AI Visibility Toolkit: 25 prompts, daily |
| Mid plan | Pro: 50 prompts daily | Semrush One Starter: 50 prompts daily |
| Higher plan | Peace of Mind: 100 prompts daily | Semrush One Pro+: 100 prompts daily |
| AI-only plan | AI Search Analytics: 50 prompts daily, 5 brand spaces | AI Visibility Toolkit |
| AIs named | ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, AI Overviews, Gemini | ChatGPT, Google AI, Gemini, Perplexity |
| Other AI reports | Mention Gap, Brand Sentiment | Brand Performance, Competitor Research, Prompt Research |

Semrush's knowledge base says Prompt Tracking covers ChatGPT and Google AI Mode, so check which AIs each report uses before you buy. Semrush also lets you run AI visibility reports for any domain, which helps with competitor research.

Surfer's edge is the link to writing. You see a mention gap, then fix the page in the same tool. Semrush's edge is the wider data around the prompts, such as Prompt Research, which shows volume and difficulty for AI topics.

## Which should you choose: Surfer SEO or Semrush?

Pick Surfer if:

- Your team publishes and refreshes articles every week.
- You want writers to hit a content score before a page goes live.
- You want AI tracking next to the editor, so you can act on a mention gap right away.
- Your budget starts under $100 a month.

Pick Semrush if:

- You need keyword research on any domain and competitor gaps.
- You need backlink data or a technical site audit.
- You report daily keyword positions to a boss or a client.
- You want SEO and AI visibility on one Semrush One plan.

## Surfer SEO vs Semrush FAQ

### Is Surfer better than Semrush for content?

Yes, for writing and scoring drafts. Surfer's Content Editor and Content Score are the core of the product. Semrush has a Content Toolkit and content optimization on Pro+, but its strength is research.

### Can Surfer replace Semrush?

Only for content work. Surfer's pricing page lists no backlink tools and no technical site crawler. If you need link data or a technical site audit, you still need a suite like Semrush.

### Which is cheaper, Surfer or Semrush?

Surfer's entry plan is lower. Discovery is $49 a month billed yearly, and Semrush's SEO plan is $139 a month or $117.33 billed yearly. Semrush also has a Free plan with 10 reports a day.

### Do Surfer and Semrush track ChatGPT?

Both do. Surfer tracks ChatGPT on Standard, and adds Perplexity, Gemini, AI Overviews and AI Mode on Pro. Semrush tracks ChatGPT in its AI Visibility Toolkit and Semrush One plans.

### Can you use Surfer and Semrush together?

Yes, and many teams do. They use Semrush to find keywords and check competitors, then write and score each page in Surfer.

## Summary: Surfer SEO vs Semrush

Surfer is the better pick for content teams. It gives writers a score to hit and AI tracking tied to the pages they write. Semrush is the better pick for SEO teams that need keyword and backlink data, with AI visibility in the Semrush One bundle.

If you only write content, start with a Surfer trial. If you run the whole SEO program, start with Semrush's Free plan or its 7-day trial and add Surfer later if your writers want it.
