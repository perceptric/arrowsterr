# AirOps vs Profound: AI Search Tracking and Content Tools Compared in 2026

AirOps and Profound both help brands show up in AI answers. They start from different ends. AirOps puts content work at the center: workflows that research, write, review and refresh pages, with AI search tracking on top. Profound puts AI search data at the center: up to 9 answer engines, prompt volumes and AI crawler data, with an AI Marketer that drafts work for you to approve.

Here is the quick answer. Pick AirOps if your team ships a lot of content and wants workflows, brand rules and human review in one place. Pick Profound if you are a large brand that needs deep AI search data, SSO and SOC 2, and you are fine with a sales-led contract. Neither vendor shows a dollar price on its pricing page, so plan on a call with either one.

## AirOps vs Profound: main points

- **Focus.** AirOps is a content platform with AI search insights. Profound is an AI search platform with content agents.
- **AI engines.** AirOps Pro lists insights across "10+ answer engines." Profound Enterprise covers up to 9, including DeepSeek and Exa Search.
- **Content tools.** AirOps has deeper content tools: tasks, Brand Kits, Knowledge Bases, Grids, playbooks and human review steps.
- **AI crawler data.** Profound's Agent Analytics connects to your CDN or host and is included on the trial and Enterprise.
- **Trial.** Profound has a free 7-day trial with fixed prompts. AirOps shows "Start for free" on its Solo and Pro plans.
- **Price.** Neither lists a dollar price. Profound's ongoing use is Enterprise only. AirOps sells Solo, Pro and Enterprise.

## What is AirOps?

AirOps (airops.com) calls itself "the partner behind your brand's AI search strategy." It sells a platform plus expert help. The platform runs content workflows that use AI agents to research, create, publish, review and refresh pages. It also tracks how AI answers name your brand, through a part of the product called Insights.

AirOps bills by tasks. A task is one action in a workflow, like generating content or pulling data. Solo includes 35,000 tasks and Pro includes 100,000. AirOps also offers strategy services from its own team.

## What is Profound?

Profound (tryprofound.com) calls itself "the AI marketing platform." It tracks how AI answers describe your category and brand across up to 9 engines. It adds Prompt Volumes, which shows what people ask AI, along with ChatGPT Shopping data and Agent Analytics for AI bots on your site.

Its AI Marketer finds work worth doing, drafts it and asks you to approve it. That covers pages, briefs, social posts and corrections to wrong claims about your brand. Profound sells ongoing use as Enterprise only, after a 7-day free trial.

## AirOps vs Profound AI visibility tracking

Profound is stronger here. AI search data is its core product, and it shows.

On Enterprise, Profound covers these engines: ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, DeepSeek, Claude, Google AI Overviews and Exa Search. Prompts run daily. You get all-time history, custom regions and languages, and Prompt Volumes based on what it calls "billions of real user conversations." ChatGPT Shopping tracking comes with Enterprise too.

AirOps covers a wide set of engines on Pro. Its docs list ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Google AI Overviews, Claude, Microsoft Copilot, Grok and Muse Spark. By default, it collects one answer per prompt per engine each day. Enterprise customers can ask for more runs per day. The docs say competitor tracking is capped at 10 competitors. Solo tracks ChatGPT only.

## AirOps vs Profound content tools

AirOps is stronger here. Content work is what it was built around.

AirOps gives you tasks to run workflows, Brand Kits for your voice and style, and Knowledge Bases for facts about your company. You get playbooks, workflows and Grids for work in bulk. Its pricing page also lists content review with quality scores, human review and approvals, version history and an inbox. Integrations cover your CMS and SEO tools on Solo. Pro adds AEO research, social and project tools.

Profound's AI Marketer works in a different way. It looks at your AI search data, picks the work worth doing and drafts it for you to approve. Its home page names these jobs:

- Pages built for AI answers
- Briefs turned into content and channel cutdowns
- Social posts, with images sized per platform
- Fixes for wrong claims about your brand, traced to their source
- Launch kits with messaging, one-pagers and FAQs

Profound also has a Context Manager that keeps your company, audience and competitor facts up to date, plus named agents such as a FactCheck agent.

Pick AirOps if you want to design your own content system and run it at volume. Pick Profound if you want an assistant that turns AI search data into drafts for you.

## AirOps vs Profound AI crawler analytics

Profound is clearer here. Its Agent Analytics shows how AI bots view, cite and refer to your pages. It is on both the trial and Enterprise, with unlimited domains. It connects to Akamai, AWS, Cloudflare, Fastly, Google Analytics, Google Cloud Platform, Netlify, Vercel and WordPress.

AirOps lists "Agent Analytics" in its pricing table. The page does not make clear which plans include it. If AI crawler data matters to you, ask AirOps to show it on your plan before you buy.

## AirOps vs Profound offsite and paid AI search

Both tools now look past your own site.

AirOps lists "Offsite Insights" and "AI Ads Insights" on its pricing page, along with an Ads Library. Its pricing table shows the Opportunities row growing by plan, from onsite and offsite on Solo to social and community on Pro and Enterprise.

Profound says its AI Marketer can run sponsored AI search campaigns on prompts where your brand is not cited. Ask each vendor which ad platforms they support today, since this area changes fast.

## AirOps vs Profound support and setup

AirOps Solo and Pro come with community and live chat support. Enterprise adds a dedicated account manager, training and 1:1 onboarding. A forward deployed engineer is listed as an add-on.

Profound's trial comes with no support. Enterprise support is set per contract: email, a dedicated Slack channel, or a dedicated specialist with a 24-hour SLA. Profound lists SSO/SAML and SOC 2 on Enterprise.

For a security review, Profound gives you more on its pricing page. AirOps lists SSO in its table without saying which plan.

## AirOps vs Profound pricing

Neither vendor shows a dollar price on its pricing page. Here is what each plan includes, as listed.

**AirOps**

- **Solo:** price not shown. One user, ChatGPT insights only, 35,000 tasks, 1 Brand Kit, 3 Knowledge Bases, basic CMS and SEO integrations, community and live chat support. Extra tasks cost $0.025 each.
- **Pro:** price not shown. Insights across 10+ answer engines, 100,000 tasks, 1 Brand Kit, 5 Knowledge Bases, CMS, SEO, AEO, social and project integrations, unlimited seats.
- **Enterprise:** custom. Custom prompt, page and task limits, custom agent builds, unlimited Brand Kits and Knowledge Bases, a dedicated account manager and 1:1 onboarding.
- **Free trial:** the FAQ describes a 14-day free trial with no payment details until the end. It names a "Scale" plan that is not in the plan list, so confirm the trial terms with AirOps.

**Profound**

- **Trial:** free for 7 days. 50 prompts run daily on ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews, in 1 language and 1 region. You can't change the prompts. Limited credits, no exports, no API.
- **Enterprise:** custom. Up to 9 engines, custom prompts, regions and languages, all-time history, CSV and JSON exports, API, SSO/SAML, SOC 2 and custom support.
- **Agency Growth:** the FAQ mentions a self-serve agency plan with 400 credits a month for each client workspace. The pricing page shows no price for it.

## Which should you choose: AirOps or Profound?

**Pick AirOps if:**

- Your team publishes and refreshes a lot of content.
- You want to build your own workflows with brand rules and review steps.
- You want unlimited seats on a plan below Enterprise.
- AI search tracking is a check on your content work, and you don't need every engine.

**Pick Profound if:**

- AI search data is the main job, and you need up to 9 engines.
- You need DeepSeek, Exa Search or ChatGPT Shopping data.
- You want AI crawler data tied to your CDN or host.
- You need SSO, SOC 2 and a support SLA on a custom contract.

## AirOps vs Profound FAQ

You can't tell from their sites. Neither AirOps nor Profound shows a dollar price on its pricing page. AirOps sells Solo and Pro below Enterprise, while Profound sells ongoing use as Enterprise only. Ask both for a quote on the same prompt count and engine list.

Yes. AirOps Insights tracks how AI answers name your brand. Solo covers ChatGPT only. Pro covers 10+ answer engines, and the docs name ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, AI Mode, AI Overviews, Claude, Copilot, Grok and Muse Spark.

### Can Profound write content?

Yes. Profound's AI Marketer drafts pages, briefs, social posts and launch kits, then asks you to approve them. It also drafts fixes for wrong claims about your brand. AirOps gives you more control over how content workflows are built and reviewed.

No. Profound has a free 7-day trial with 50 fixed prompts on ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews. After that, you need an Enterprise contract or the Agency Growth plan.
