# AirOps vs Profound: AI Search Tracking and Content Tools Compared in 2026

AirOps and Profound chase the same prize from opposite ends: getting your brand named in AI answers. AirOps puts content work at the center. Its workflows research, write, review and refresh pages, and AI search tracking sits on top. Profound starts from the data. It covers up to 9 answer engines with prompt volumes and AI crawler data, and its AI Marketer drafts work for you to approve.

Pick AirOps if your team ships a lot of content and wants workflows and human review in one place. Pick Profound if you are a large brand that needs deep AI search data with SSO and SOC 2, and you are fine buying through sales. Neither has a public price, so plan on a call with either one.

## AirOps vs Profound: main points

- AirOps is a content platform with AI search insights. Profound is an AI search platform with content agents.
- AirOps Pro tracks 10+ answer engines. Profound Enterprise tracks up to 9, with DeepSeek and Exa Search among them.
- AirOps goes deeper on content, with tasks, Brand Kits, Knowledge Bases, Grids, playbooks and human review steps.
- Profound's Agent Analytics connects to your CDN or host for AI crawler data. It comes with the trial and Enterprise.
- Profound has a free 7 day trial with fixed prompts. AirOps Solo and Pro have a "Start for free" option.
- Neither has a public price. Profound sells ongoing use as Enterprise only. AirOps sells Solo, Pro and Enterprise.

### What AirOps does today

[AirOps](https://www.airops.com/) sells a platform plus expert help for your brand's AI search strategy. The platform runs content workflows where AI agents research, create, publish, review and refresh pages. A part of the product called Insights tracks how AI answers name your brand. Ramp, Webflow, Kayak, Klaviyo and Chime all use AirOps.

AirOps bills by tasks. A task is one action in a workflow, like generating content or pulling data. Solo includes 35,000 tasks and Pro includes 100,000. Its own team also sells strategy services.

### AirOps history

Alex Halliday, Berna Gonzalez and Matt Hammel founded AirOps. Before that, the three built products at MasterClass and Bungalow. The company started out in Miami. Its first product, AirOps Apps, went into private beta in late 2022. Apps let teams drop AI tools built for single tasks into the work they already do, from writing content to writing SQL queries.

In April 2023, AirOps raised a $7 million seed round led by Wing VC. A $15.5 million Series A led by Unusual Ventures followed in October 2024. By then, content marketing was the main focus.

In November 2025, AirOps raised a $40 million Series B led by Greylock, which brought its total funding to $60 million. With the round came an end to end content engineering platform built for AI search. It added Insights for AI visibility across ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Mode and AI Overviews. It also added Page360, one view of each page's SEO and AI search data. AirOps Actions then turns those findings into on brand content.

### What Profound does today

[Profound](https://www.tryprofound.com/) is an AI marketing platform. It tracks how AI answers describe your category and brand across up to 9 engines. Prompt Volumes shows what people ask AI. ChatGPT Shopping data comes with it, along with Agent Analytics for AI bots on your site.

Its AI Marketer finds work worth doing, drafts it and asks you to approve it. That covers pages, briefs, social posts and corrections to wrong claims about your brand. Ongoing use is Enterprise only, after a 7 day free trial. By September 2026, more than 2,500 brands used Profound, Walmart, Zoom, Stripe and Figma among them.

### Profound history

James Cadwallader and Dylan Babbs started Profound in 2024. They met at South Park Commons, a startup community and early stage fund. Cadwallader had founded the influencer marketing firm Kyra. Babbs was a software engineer at Uber.

Funding came fast. Profound closed a $3.5 million seed round in August 2024, with Khosla Ventures and South Park Commons among its backers. A $20 million Series A led by Kleiner Perkins followed in June 2025. That same month, Profound earned SOC 2 Type 2. In August 2025, Sequoia led a $35 million Series B.

In February 2026, Lightspeed Venture Partners led a $96 million Series C that valued Profound at $1 billion. Profound had more than 700 enterprise customers by then. The same month brought Profound Agents, which write marketing copy in a brand's voice. An Agent Template Marketplace came in June 2026.

In September 2026, Sequoia Capital and Kleiner Perkins led a $180 million Series D at a $1.8 billion valuation. AI Marketer, also called Aim, is now the heart of the product. A new generation of Agents followed in October 2026.

## AirOps vs Profound content tools

AirOps was built around content work, and this is where it pulls ahead.

On AirOps, tasks run your workflows. Brand Kits hold your voice and style, and Knowledge Bases hold facts about your company. There is also an inbox for content work.

A Context Manager keeps your company and competitor facts current, along with your audience.

AirOps suits a team that wants to design its own content system and run it at volume. If you would rather have an assistant turn AI search data into drafts, that is what Profound does.

## Which should you choose: AirOps or Profound?

**Pick AirOps if:**

- Your team publishes and refreshes a lot of content.
- You want to build your own workflows with brand rules and review steps.
- You want unlimited seats on a plan below Enterprise.
- AI search tracking is a check on your content work, and you don't need every engine.

**Pick Profound if:**

- AI search data is the main job, and you need up to 9 engines.
- You need DeepSeek, Exa Search or ChatGPT Shopping data.
- You want AI crawler data tied to your CDN or host.
- You need SSO, SOC 2 and a support SLA on a custom contract.

## AirOps vs Profound FAQ

### Is AirOps cheaper than Profound?

There is no way to compare yet, since neither AirOps nor Profound has a public price. AirOps sells Solo and Pro below Enterprise, while Profound sells ongoing use as Enterprise only. Ask both for a quote on the same prompt count and engine list.

### Does AirOps track AI search visibility?

Yes, through AirOps Insights. Solo covers ChatGPT only. Pro covers 10+ answer engines, including ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, AI Mode, AI Overviews, Claude, Copilot, Grok and Muse Spark.

### Can Profound write content?

Yes. Profound's AI Marketer drafts pages, briefs, social posts and launch kits, then asks you to approve them. It also drafts fixes for wrong claims about your brand. AirOps gives you more control over how content workflows are built and reviewed.

### Does Profound have a free plan?

No. Profound has a free 7 day trial with 50 fixed prompts on ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews. After that, you need an Enterprise contract or the Agency Growth plan.

### Which tool tracks more AI engines?

It depends on the engines you need. Profound Enterprise covers 9, including DeepSeek and Exa Search. AirOps Pro covers 10+, including Grok and Muse Spark. Match the list to the AI tools your buyers use.
