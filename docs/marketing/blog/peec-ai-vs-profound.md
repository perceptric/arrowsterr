# Peec AI vs Profound: AI Visibility Tracking, Models and Pricing Compared in 2026

Peec AI and Profound both track how AI answers talk about your brand. They run your prompts every day, show who gets named and list the sources AI cites. The big split is how you buy them. Peec AI lists its prices and sells three plans you can start on your own. Profound sells ongoing use as Enterprise only, at a price set on a sales call.

Here is the quick answer. Pick Peec AI if you want daily tracking on 3 models at a listed price, with unlimited users and API access from Pro. Pick Profound if you are a large brand that needs up to 9 engines on one contract, with an AI Marketer that drafts work for you. Most small and mid-size teams will find Peec AI easier to start with.

## Peec AI vs Profound: main points

- **Price.** Peec AI starts at $95/mo. Profound shows no price and sells ongoing use on custom Enterprise plans.
- **Models.** Peec AI's self-serve plans track 3 models you choose. Profound Enterprise covers up to 9 engines in one plan.
- **Claude and Perplexity.** Peec AI lists Claude on Enterprise only, with Perplexity as an add-on. Profound includes both on Enterprise.
- **Content.** Profound's AI Marketer drafts pages, posts and fixes for you to approve. Peec AI focuses on data, gap analysis and agent actions.
- **Data access.** Peec AI includes MCP and CSV exports on every plan and the API from Pro. Profound's exports and API are Enterprise only.
- **Trial.** Profound has a free 7-day trial with fixed prompts. Peec AI offers a free trial from its site but doesn't list its length.

### What Peec AI does today

[Peec AI](https://peec.ai/) is an AI search analytics tool for marketing teams and SEO agencies. You add prompts, pick models and add competitors. Peec AI then checks those prompts daily and reports your visibility, position, sentiment and share of voice.

It also shows the sources AI cites, finds gaps where rivals get named and you don't, and ranks actions to fix them. Every plan includes unlimited users. A built-in Peec agent runs reports and other jobs with a monthly pool of agent credits.

### Peec AI history

Marius Meiners, Daniel Drabo and Tobias Siwonia met in Antler's Winter 2024 cohort in Berlin. The three co-founded Peec AI, with Meiners as CEO. Before that, Meiners was an esports player who ranked among the top 100 League of Legends players. The product went live in February 2025.

Growth came quickly. In July 2025, Peec AI announced €7 million in early funding, with a seed round led by 20VC. Antler and Foreword VC also backed it. In November 2025, it raised a $21 million Series A led by Singular, which brought total funding to $29 million. At that point, Peec AI had onboarded more than 1,300 brands and agencies. It said it was adding over 300 customers a month and had passed $4 million in annual recurring revenue.

The Series A post also set the next goals. Peec AI planned to open offices in New York and to grow from analytics into a full marketing software stack for the AI era. By May 2026, TechCrunch reported that Peec AI had opened its New York office and crossed $10 million in annualized revenue. At the Series A, Meiners said its value had passed $100 million.

### What Profound does today

[Profound](https://www.tryprofound.com/) calls itself "the AI marketing platform." It tracks how AI answers describe your category and brand across up to 9 engines. It adds Prompt Volumes and ChatGPT Shopping data. Agent Analytics shows how AI bots use your site.

Its AI Marketer looks at your data, picks work worth doing and drafts it for you to approve. Profound sells ongoing use as Enterprise only, after a 7-day free trial. In September 2026, it said more than 2,500 brands use it, including Walmart, Zoom, Stripe and Figma.

### Profound history

James Cadwallader and Dylan Babbs started Profound in 2024. They met at South Park Commons, a startup community and early-stage fund. Cadwallader had founded the influencer marketing firm Kyra. Babbs was a software engineer at Uber.

Funding came fast. Profound closed a $3.5 million seed round in August 2024, with Khosla Ventures and South Park Commons among its backers. A $20 million Series A led by Kleiner Perkins followed in June 2025. That same month, Profound earned SOC 2 Type 2. In August 2025, Sequoia led a $35 million Series B.

In February 2026, a $96 million Series C led by Lightspeed Venture Partners valued Profound at $1 billion. Fortune reported more than 700 enterprise customers at the time, along with the launch of Profound Agents, which write marketing copy in a brand's voice. An Agent Template Marketplace came in June 2026.

In September 2026, Profound raised a $180 million Series D at a $1.8 billion valuation, co-led by Sequoia Capital and Kleiner Perkins. Its Series D post puts AI Marketer, also called Aim, at the heart of the product. A new generation of Agents followed in October 2026.

## Peec AI vs Profound FAQ

### Is Peec AI cheaper than Profound?

Peec AI lists its prices, starting at $95/mo for 50 prompts on 3 models. Profound shows no prices and sells ongoing use on custom Enterprise plans. For most teams, Peec AI will cost less. Ask Profound for a quote if you need its wider engine list.

### Does Peec AI track Claude?

Only on Enterprise. Peec AI lists Claude Sonnet 4 (API) among its Enterprise models. Its self-serve plans let you pick 3 from ChatGPT, AI Mode, AI Overviews, Copilot, Gemini and Naver AI. Profound includes Claude on Enterprise.

### Does Profound have a free plan?

No. Profound has a free 7-day trial with 50 fixed prompts on ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews. After that, you need an Enterprise contract or the Agency Growth plan.

### Which tool tracks more AI models?

On the top plans, Peec AI Enterprise lists up to 13 models and Profound Enterprise lists up to 9. On plans you can buy without a call, Peec AI tracks 3 models you pick. Check that the models you need are on the plan you plan to buy.
