# Peec AI vs Profound: AI Visibility Tracking, Models and Pricing Compared in 2026

Peec AI has public prices and three plans you can start on your own. Profound sells ongoing use as Enterprise only, at a price set on a sales call. Under that, the job is the same. Each tool runs your prompts every day and shows which brands get named, along with the sources AI cites.

Pick Peec AI if you want daily tracking on 3 models at a public price, with unlimited users and API access from Pro. Pick Profound if you are a large brand that needs up to 9 engines on one contract, with an AI Marketer that drafts work for you. Most small and midsize teams will find Peec AI easier to start with.

## Peec AI vs Profound: main points

- Peec AI starts at $95/mo. Profound has no public price and sells ongoing use on custom Enterprise plans.
- On self serve plans, Peec AI tracks 3 models you choose. Profound Enterprise covers up to 9 engines in one plan.
- Claude is Enterprise only on Peec AI, and Perplexity costs extra. Profound includes both on Enterprise.
- Profound's AI Marketer drafts pages, posts and fixes for you to approve. Peec AI sticks to data, gap analysis and agent actions.
- Every Peec AI plan has MCP and CSV exports, and Pro adds the API. Profound keeps exports and the API on Enterprise.
- Profound has a free 7 day trial with fixed prompts. Peec AI has a free trial too.

### What Peec AI does today

[Peec AI](https://peec.ai/) is an AI search analytics tool for marketing teams and SEO agencies. You add prompts, pick models and add competitors. Peec AI then checks those prompts daily and reports your visibility, position, sentiment and share of voice.

It also shows the sources AI cites, finds gaps where rivals get named and you don't, and ranks actions to fix them. Every plan includes unlimited users. Peec AI's own agent runs reports and other jobs from a monthly pool of agent credits.

### Peec AI history

Marius Meiners, Daniel Drabo and Tobias Siwonia met in Antler's Winter 2024 cohort in Berlin. The three started Peec AI together, with Meiners as CEO. Before that, Meiners was an esports player who ranked among the top 100 League of Legends players. The product went live in February 2025.

Growth came quickly. In July 2025, Peec AI raised €7 million in early funding, with a seed round led by 20VC. Antler and Foreword VC also backed it. In November 2025, a $21 million Series A led by Singular brought total funding to $29 million. By then, Peec AI had onboarded more than 1,300 brands and agencies. It was adding over 300 customers a month and had passed $4 million in annual recurring revenue.

The Series A came with two goals: offices in New York, and a move from analytics into a full marketing software stack for the AI era. At that round, Peec AI's value had passed $100 million. By May 2026, it had opened the New York office and crossed $10 million in annualized revenue.

### What Profound does today

[Profound](https://www.tryprofound.com/) is an AI marketing platform. It tracks how AI answers describe your category and brand across up to 9 engines, with Prompt Volumes and ChatGPT Shopping data on top. Agent Analytics shows how AI bots use your site.

Its AI Marketer looks at your data, picks work worth doing and drafts it for you to approve. After a 7 day free trial, ongoing use is Enterprise only. More than 2,500 brands used Profound by September 2026, including Walmart, Zoom, Stripe and Figma.

### Profound history

James Cadwallader and Dylan Babbs started Profound in 2024. They met at South Park Commons, a startup community and early stage fund. Cadwallader had founded the influencer marketing firm Kyra. Babbs was a software engineer at Uber.

Funding came fast. Profound closed a $3.5 million seed round in August 2024, with Khosla Ventures and South Park Commons among its backers. A $20 million Series A led by Kleiner Perkins followed in June 2025. That same month, Profound earned SOC 2 Type 2. In August 2025, Sequoia led a $35 million Series B.

In February 2026, a $96 million Series C led by Lightspeed Venture Partners valued Profound at $1 billion. It had more than 700 enterprise customers at that point and launched Profound Agents, which write marketing copy in a brand's voice. An Agent Template Marketplace came in June 2026.

In September 2026, Profound raised a $180 million Series D at a $1.8 billion valuation, led by Sequoia Capital and Kleiner Perkins together. AI Marketer, also called Aim, now sits at the heart of the product. A new generation of Agents followed in October 2026.

## Peec AI vs Profound FAQ

### Is Peec AI cheaper than Profound?

Peec AI has public prices, starting at $95/mo for 50 prompts on 3 models. Profound's prices are not public, and ongoing use runs on custom Enterprise plans. For most teams, Peec AI will cost less. Ask Profound for a quote if you need its wider engine list.

### Does Peec AI track Claude?

Only on Enterprise, where Peec AI tracks Claude Sonnet 4 (API). Its self serve plans let you pick 3 from ChatGPT, AI Mode, AI Overviews, Copilot, Gemini and Naver AI. Profound includes Claude on Enterprise.

### Does Profound have a free plan?

No. Profound has a free 7 day trial with 50 fixed prompts on ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews. After that, you need an Enterprise contract or the Agency Growth plan.

### Which tool tracks more AI models?

On the top plans, Peec AI Enterprise tracks up to 13 models and Profound Enterprise covers up to 9. On plans you can buy without a call, Peec AI tracks 3 models you pick. Make sure the models you need are on the plan you buy.
