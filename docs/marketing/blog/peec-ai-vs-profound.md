# Peec AI vs Profound: AI Visibility Tracking, Models and Pricing Compared in 2026

Peec AI and Profound both track how AI answers talk about your brand. They run your prompts every day, show who gets named and list the sources AI cites. The big split is how you buy them. Peec AI lists its prices and sells three plans you can start on your own. Profound sells ongoing use as Enterprise only, at a price set on a sales call.

Here is the quick answer. Pick Peec AI if you want daily tracking on 3 models at a listed price, with unlimited users and API access from Pro. Pick Profound if you are a large brand that needs up to 9 engines on one contract, an AI Marketer that drafts work for you, and a support SLA. Most small and mid-size teams will find Peec AI easier to start with.

## Peec AI vs Profound: main points

- **Price.** Peec AI starts at $95/mo. Profound shows no price and sells ongoing use on custom Enterprise plans.
- **Models.** Peec AI's self-serve plans track 3 models you choose. Profound Enterprise covers up to 9 engines in one plan.
- **Claude and Perplexity.** Peec AI lists Claude on Enterprise only, with Perplexity as an add-on. Profound includes both on Enterprise.
- **Content.** Profound's AI Marketer drafts pages, posts and fixes for you to approve. Peec AI focuses on data, gap analysis and agent actions.
- **Data access.** Peec AI includes MCP and CSV exports on every plan and the API from Pro. Profound's exports and API are Enterprise only.
- **Trial.** Profound has a free 7-day trial with fixed prompts. Peec AI offers a free trial from its site (length: check).

## What is Peec AI?

Peec AI (peec.ai) is an AI search analytics tool for marketing teams and SEO agencies. You add prompts, pick models and add competitors. Peec AI then checks those prompts daily and reports your visibility, position, sentiment and share of voice.

It also shows the sources AI cites, finds gaps where rivals get named and you don't, and ranks actions to fix them. Every plan includes unlimited users. A built-in Peec agent runs reports and other jobs with a monthly pool of agent credits.

## What is Profound?

Profound (tryprofound.com) calls itself "the AI marketing platform." It tracks how AI answers describe your category and brand across up to 9 engines. It adds Prompt Volumes, ChatGPT Shopping data and Agent Analytics for AI bots on your site.

Its AI Marketer looks at your data, picks work worth doing and drafts it for you to approve. Profound sells ongoing use as Enterprise only, after a 7-day free trial.

- **Self-serve models:** ChatGPT, AI Mode, AI Overviews, Microsoft Copilot, Gemini and Naver AI.

- **Enterprise only:** Perplexity, Claude Sonnet 4 (API), GPT 5 Search (API), Grok (API), DeepSeek (API), Qwen (API), Mistral (API) and Meta Spark (API). Enterprise tracks up to 13 models.

Profound's trial runs on ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews. Enterprise covers up to 9 engines: ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, DeepSeek, Claude, Google AI Overviews and Exa Search.

## Peec AI vs Profound sources and gap analysis

Both tools are strong here, and Peec AI shows more of it on a listed plan.

Every Peec AI plan includes source analytics with domain and URL detail, citation share and fanout views. Each source is sorted into a type such as competitor, editorial, reference or UGC. Gap analysis finds sources that name competitors but not you. Recommended actions rank what to fix, split into earned and owned.

Peec AI also adds a brand perception layer. It scores what AI says you are known for and fact-checks AI answers against facts you set. Starter checks 5 prompts against 5 facts per brand, and the limits rise by plan. Objections, the recurring arguments AI raises against your brand, come monthly on Pro and weekly on Advanced.

Profound's Answer Engine Insights shows how AI describes your category and brand. It has FactCheck, sentiment and benchmarking tools. Suggestions are limited on the trial and unlimited on Enterprise.

## Peec AI vs Profound prompt research

Profound is stronger here. Prompt Volumes is one of its core products. Profound says it is built on "billions of real user conversations," so you can see what people ask AI and why. The trial includes 5 Prompt Volumes searches. Enterprise limits are custom.

Peec AI gives each tracked prompt a prompt volume score from 1 to 5, based on demand for the topic behind it. It also suggests prompts, topics and competitors from your brand context. It can turn keywords into prompts and classify each prompt by intent. It gives less depth than Profound's volume data.

## Peec AI vs Profound AI crawler analytics

Both tools show how AI bots use your site.

Peec AI's agent analytics comes on every plan. A crawlability audit checks which of 40+ AI bots your robots.txt allows or blocks. AI referrals show visits from AI assistants. Crawl insights show which bots hit which pages, with a cap on bot visits by plan: 4.5M on Starter, 13.5M on Pro and 31.5M on Advanced.

Profound's Agent Analytics shows how bots view, cite and refer to your pages. It comes on the trial and on Enterprise, with unlimited domains.

Both connect to common CDNs and hosts, such as Cloudflare, Vercel, Akamai, AWS and WordPress. This one is close. Peec AI gives you more on a listed plan. Profound has no domain cap.

## Peec AI vs Profound agents and content

Profound is stronger here. Its AI Marketer drafts real work: pages built for AI answers, social posts, launch kits and fixes for wrong claims about your brand. It pings you to approve each draft. A Context Manager keeps your company and competitor facts current for its agents.

Peec AI's agent is closer to an analyst. It uses your data and your company context to run skills, such as reports and diagnostics, and you can write custom skills. Agent credits come with each plan: 500 on Starter, 750 on Pro and 1,000 on Advanced. "Agent actions" turn your data into next steps. Writing the pages themselves happens in other tools.

## Peec AI vs Profound reporting and data access

Peec AI is stronger here on any plan below Enterprise. Every plan includes shareable dashboards, custom CSV exports, MCP access and role-based permissions. Pro adds the API. Advanced adds a Looker Studio connector. SOC 2 is listed on all plans.

Profound's trial has no exports, no API and no data history. Enterprise adds all-time history, CSV and JSON exports, the API and SOC 2.

## Peec AI vs Profound FAQ

### Is Peec AI cheaper than Profound?

Peec AI lists its prices, starting at $95/mo for 50 prompts on 3 models. Profound shows no prices and sells ongoing use on custom Enterprise plans. For most teams, Peec AI will cost less. Ask Profound for a quote if you need its wider engine list.

### Does Peec AI track Claude?

Only on Enterprise. Peec AI lists Claude Sonnet 4 (API) among its Enterprise models. Its self-serve plans let you pick 3 from ChatGPT, AI Mode, AI Overviews, Copilot, Gemini and Naver AI. Profound includes Claude on Enterprise.

### Does Profound have a free plan?

No. Profound has a free 7-day trial with 50 fixed prompts on ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews. After that, you need an Enterprise contract or the Agency Growth plan.

### Which tool tracks more AI models?

On the top plans, Peec AI Enterprise lists up to 13 models and Profound Enterprise lists up to 9. On plans you can buy without a call, Peec AI tracks 3 models you pick. Check that the models you need are on the plan you plan to buy.
