# BrightEdge vs Semrush: Enterprise SEO and AI Search Compared in 2026

BrightEdge and Semrush are both SEO platforms with AI search tracking. They are sold in very different ways. BrightEdge is an enterprise platform you buy through a demo and a custom contract. Semrush is a self-serve suite with public prices, a free plan and a 7-day trial.

The quick answer: pick BrightEdge if you run SEO for a large brand with many sites, markets or teams, and you want a managed platform with automation and reporting. Pick Semrush if you want to start today, see the price up front, and get broad keyword and backlink data on one plan.

## BrightEdge vs Semrush: main points

- **BrightEdge has no public pricing.** Its pricing page is a demo request. Semrush lists its plans, starting at $139 a month, plus a free plan.
- **Semrush has the bigger published data.** It lists 28.8 billion keywords and over 43 trillion backlinks. BrightEdge does not publish its database sizes.
- **BrightEdge is built around enterprise workflows.** It adds automatic on-site fixes (Autopilot), dashboards (StoryBuilder) and page-level reporting.
- **Both track AI answers.** BrightEdge's AI Catalyst names ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode. Semrush tracks prompts from its Starter plan up.
- **Semrush is easier to try.** You can sign up and run reports the same day. BrightEdge starts with a sales call.

## What is BrightEdge?

<!-- shot: brightedge -->

[BrightEdge](https://www.brightedge.com/) is an enterprise SEO and content platform. It says it is used by more than 1,700 enterprise brands, and its About page counts 57 of the Fortune 100 as customers. Its main parts are Data Cube X for research, ContentIQ for site audits, Autopilot for automatic site fixes and AI Catalyst for AI search. It targets midsize, enterprise and global multi-market businesses. It has more than 430 employees.

### How BrightEdge started

Jim Yu founded BrightEdge in 2007 with Lemuel Park, who became its CTO. Before that, Yu led platform product teams at Salesforce. The two wrote the first code at Yu's kitchen table on a budget of $5,000. In 2008 the team moved into its first office in San Mateo and raised a Series A led by Altos Ventures.

BrightEdge built its product in stealth with early customers. In June 2010 it made its SEO platform generally available and called it an enterprise SEO platform. In March 2012 it raised a $12.6 million Series C led by Intel Capital. That year it opened offices in New York and London.

### From reporting to automation and AI

BrightEdge has used its own deep learning engine, DataMind, since 2015. In September 2019 it launched Autopilot at its Share19 event. It called Autopilot the first self-driving SEO tool. Autopilot was built partly on technology BrightEdge bought from Trilibis.

Generative AI came next. In August 2023 BrightEdge launched Copilot, an AI assistant that suggests page titles and descriptions. In December 2023 it added Copilot for Content Advisor, which writes outlines and drafts. In April 2025 it launched AI Catalyst to track brands in AI search. In March 2026 it added AI Hyper Cube, which shows how brands appear in AI search.

## What is Semrush?

<!-- shot: semrush -->

[Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/) is one of the largest self-serve SEO suites. It covers keyword research, backlink analysis, technical audits, rank tracking and content tools. Its current plans bundle SEO and AI search tracking under the name Semrush One. Traffic, local, content, social and ads tools are sold as extra toolkits. Adobe now owns it, and Adobe says it has over 28 million users.

### How Semrush started

Oleg Shchegolev and Dmitri Melnikov founded Semrush in 2008. Their first product was SeoDigger, which became SeoQuake, a Firefox add-on for SEO checks. The name comes from SEM, short for search engine marketing. The founders moved to the US in 2017, and the company is based in Boston.

### Funding, IPO and Adobe

Semrush raised $40 million in April 2018. It went public on the New York Stock Exchange in March 2021 under the ticker SEMR. It grew by buying companies too, such as Backlinko in 2022 and Third Door Media, the owner of Search Engine Land, in 2024.

Adobe agreed to buy Semrush in November 2025 for about $1.9 billion. The deal closed on April 28, 2026, and Semrush now runs as "Semrush, an Adobe company."

### AI search features

Semrush launched its AI Toolkit in March 2025 to track how ChatGPT and Gemini describe brands. In May 2025 it split the product into seven toolkits you can buy one at a time. In October 2025 it launched Semrush One, which puts SEO and AI visibility on one plan. It also sells a custom Enterprise plan for large companies, which puts it closer to BrightEdge's market.

## BrightEdge vs Semrush FAQ

### Is BrightEdge better than Semrush?

It depends on your team. BrightEdge is built for large enterprises that want automation, dashboards and a managed contract. Semrush is better for teams that want broad keyword and backlink data with public prices.

### How much does BrightEdge cost?

BrightEdge does not publish its prices. You request a demo, and the price is set in a custom contract. Semrush, by contrast, lists plans from $139 a month and has a free plan.

### Does BrightEdge track ChatGPT and AI Overviews?

Yes. BrightEdge AI Catalyst names ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode. It tracks mentions, citations and sentiment against your competitors.

### Does Semrush have an enterprise plan?

Yes. Semrush lists an Enterprise plan with custom pricing, unlimited projects, SSO and an account manager. Its Advanced plan also adds API access and team users.

### Does BrightEdge have a free trial?

BrightEdge's pages offer a demo and do not list a free trial or free plan. Semrush has a free plan and a 7-day trial on most paid plans.

### How long have BrightEdge and Semrush been around?

BrightEdge was founded in 2007 in the San Francisco Bay Area. Semrush was founded in 2008 and is now part of Adobe.

## Summary: BrightEdge vs Semrush

BrightEdge is the stronger choice for large brands that want enterprise reporting and automatic site fixes, with AI search tracking across five engines. You pay a custom price set through sales. Semrush is the stronger choice for most other teams. It has bigger published keyword and backlink data, public prices and a free way to start.

If you can't tell which side you are on, start a Semrush trial. If you outgrow it or need automation across many sites, book a BrightEdge demo.
