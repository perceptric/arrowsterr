# BrightEdge vs Semrush: Enterprise SEO and AI Search Compared in 2026

You can't buy BrightEdge without a demo. Its price is set in a custom contract. Semrush is self serve: its prices are public, and you can start on a free plan or a 7 day trial. Each one tracks AI search on top of its SEO tools.

Choose BrightEdge if you run SEO for a large brand with many sites in many markets, and you want a managed platform with automation and reporting. Choose Semrush if you want to start today at a known price, with broad keyword and backlink data on one plan.

## BrightEdge vs Semrush: main points

- BrightEdge prices are not public. You get one through a demo request. Semrush plans start at $139 a month, and there is a free plan.
- Semrush has 28.8 billion keywords and over 43 trillion backlinks. BrightEdge's database sizes are not public.
- BrightEdge is built around enterprise workflows. Autopilot makes fixes on your site for you, while StoryBuilder dashboards and page level reports cover the reporting side.
- BrightEdge's AI Catalyst tracks ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode. Semrush tracks prompts from its Starter plan up.
- You can sign up for Semrush and run reports the same day. BrightEdge starts with a sales call.

## What is BrightEdge?

<!-- shot: brightedge -->

[BrightEdge](https://www.brightedge.com/) is an enterprise SEO and content platform. More than 1,700 enterprise brands use it, including 57 of the Fortune 100. Its main parts are Data Cube X for research, ContentIQ for site audits, Autopilot for automatic site fixes and AI Catalyst for AI search. It sells to midsize and enterprise businesses, including global brands that work across many markets. BrightEdge has more than 430 employees.

### How BrightEdge started

Jim Yu founded BrightEdge in 2007 with Lemuel Park, who became its CTO. Before that, Yu led platform product teams at Salesforce. The two wrote the first code at Yu's kitchen table on a budget of $5,000. In 2008 the team moved into its first office in San Mateo and raised a Series A led by Altos Ventures.

BrightEdge built its product in stealth with early customers. Its enterprise SEO platform became generally available in June 2010. In March 2012 it raised a $12.6 million Series C led by Intel Capital, and that year it opened offices in New York and London.

### From reporting to automation and AI

BrightEdge has used its own deep learning engine, DataMind, since 2015. In September 2019 it launched Autopilot at its Share19 event as the first "self-driving" SEO tool. Autopilot was built partly on technology BrightEdge bought from Trilibis.

Generative AI arrived in August 2023 with Copilot, an AI assistant that suggests page titles and descriptions. In December 2023 BrightEdge added Copilot for Content Advisor, which writes outlines and drafts. AI Catalyst followed in April 2025 to track brands in AI search. In March 2026 it added AI Hyper Cube, which shows how brands appear in AI search.

## What is Semrush?

[Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/) is one of the largest self serve SEO suites, with over 28 million users. Adobe now owns it. It covers keyword research, backlink analysis, technical audits, rank tracking and content tools. Its current plans, sold as Semrush One, bundle SEO and AI search tracking. Traffic, local, content, social and ads tools come as extra toolkits.

### How Semrush started

Oleg Shchegolev and Dmitri Melnikov founded Semrush in 2008. Their first product was SeoDigger, which became SeoQuake, a Firefox extension for SEO checks. The name comes from SEM, short for search engine marketing. The founders moved to the US in 2017, and the company is based in Boston.

### Funding, IPO and Adobe

Semrush raised $40 million in April 2018. It went public on the New York Stock Exchange in March 2021 under the ticker SEMR. It grew by buying companies too, such as Backlinko in 2022 and Third Door Media, the owner of Search Engine Land, in 2024.

Adobe agreed to buy Semrush in November 2025 for about $1.9 billion. The deal closed on April 28, 2026, and Semrush now runs as "Semrush, an Adobe company."

### AI search features

Semrush launched its AI Toolkit in March 2025 to track how ChatGPT and Gemini describe brands. In May 2025 it split the product into seven toolkits you can buy one at a time. In October 2025 it launched Semrush One, which puts SEO and AI visibility on one plan. A custom Enterprise plan for large companies puts Semrush closer to BrightEdge's market.

## BrightEdge vs Semrush site audit and automation

The real difference here is who makes the fixes. On BrightEdge, Autopilot makes website fixes automatically once it is set up on your site. ContentIQ audits and monitors the site and ranks errors before they hurt performance. Intent Signal flags the content most likely to rank above the fold.

On Semrush, your team does the work. Site Audit crawls your site and flags technical issues for you or your developers to fix. The Starter plan adds an AI ready site audit that checks for issues that affect AI crawlers. You can run a quick check of your own site with our free [AI crawler checker](https://arrowsterr.com/free-tools/ai-crawler-checker/).

Large sites that want fixes applied for them should look at BrightEdge. Semrush's audit is easier to start and suits teams that fix issues by hand.

## BrightEdge vs Semrush FAQ

### Is BrightEdge better than Semrush?

For large enterprises that want automation and dashboards under a managed contract, BrightEdge is the better fit. Semrush is better for teams that want broad keyword and backlink data with public prices.

### How much does BrightEdge cost?

BrightEdge prices are not public. You request a demo, and the price is set in a custom contract. Semrush plans start at $139 a month, and it has a free plan.

### Does BrightEdge track ChatGPT and AI Overviews?

Yes. BrightEdge AI Catalyst tracks ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode. It measures mentions, citations and sentiment against your competitors.

### Does Semrush have an enterprise plan?

Yes. Semrush has an Enterprise plan with custom pricing, unlimited projects, SSO and an account manager. Its Advanced plan also adds API access and team users.

### Does BrightEdge have a free trial?

BrightEdge starts with a demo and has no public free trial or free plan. Semrush has a free plan and a 7 day trial on most paid plans.

### How long have BrightEdge and Semrush been around?

BrightEdge was founded in 2007 in the San Francisco Bay Area. Semrush was founded in 2008 and is now part of Adobe.

## Summary: BrightEdge vs Semrush

Start with the size of your SEO operation. A large brand that reports to leadership and wants Autopilot fixing pages is the buyer BrightEdge is built for, at a custom price set through sales. Most other teams will do more with Semrush's public plans and bigger published data.

Not sure which side you are on? Start a Semrush trial. If you outgrow it or need automation across many sites, book a BrightEdge demo.
