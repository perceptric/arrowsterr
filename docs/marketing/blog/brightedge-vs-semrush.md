# BrightEdge vs Semrush: Enterprise SEO and AI Search Compared in 2026

BrightEdge and Semrush are both SEO platforms with AI search tracking. They are sold in very different ways. BrightEdge is an enterprise platform you buy through a demo and a custom contract. Semrush is a self-serve suite with public prices, a free plan and a 7-day trial.

The quick answer: pick BrightEdge if you run SEO for a large brand with many sites, markets or teams, and you want a managed platform with automation and reporting. Pick Semrush if you want to start today, see the price up front, and get broad keyword and backlink data on one plan.

## BrightEdge vs Semrush: main points

- **BrightEdge has no public pricing.** Its pricing page is a demo request. Semrush lists its plans, starting at $139 a month, plus a free plan.
- **Semrush has the bigger published data.** It lists 28.8 billion keywords and over 43 trillion backlinks. BrightEdge does not publish its database sizes.
- **BrightEdge is built around enterprise workflows.** It adds automatic on-site fixes (Autopilot), dashboards (StoryBuilder) and page-level reporting.
- **Both track AI answers.** BrightEdge's AI Catalyst names ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode. Semrush tracks prompts from its Starter plan up.
- **Semrush is easier to try.** You can sign up and run reports the same day. BrightEdge starts with a sales call.

## What is BrightEdge?

[BrightEdge](https://www.brightedge.com/) is an enterprise SEO and content platform. It says it is used by more than 1,700 enterprise brands. Its main parts are Data Cube X for research, ContentIQ for site audits, Autopilot for automatic site fixes and AI Catalyst for AI search. It targets midsize, enterprise and global multi-market businesses.

## What is Semrush?

[Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/) is one of the largest self-serve SEO suites. It covers keyword research, backlink analysis, technical audits, rank tracking and content tools. Its current plans bundle SEO and AI search tracking under the name Semrush One. Traffic, local, content, social and ads tools are sold as extra toolkits.

## BrightEdge vs Semrush keyword research

BrightEdge's Data Cube X is its research engine. BrightEdge calls it "the broadest keyword universe in the industry." It does not give a keyword count on the pages we checked. Its FAQ says the Data Cube covers "billions of pieces of content" and local search data across 37,000 locations. BrightEdge also sells AI Hyper Cube, which shows how people search across ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews.

Semrush lists 28.8 billion keywords across 142 country databases. Its keyword tools show volume, difficulty, intent and SERP features. You also get competitor gaps and topic research.

**Stronger: Semrush** for open keyword research, since it publishes its data size and lets you research any keyword in any market on every paid plan. BrightEdge's research is tied to its reporting, which helps large teams that track thousands of pages.

## BrightEdge vs Semrush backlink data

Semrush's backlink index holds over 43 trillion links and updates daily. Backlink analysis, gaps and audits are part of its SEO plans.

BrightEdge's product pages we checked do not list a backlink index or backlink tool. Ask about link data in your demo if you need it.

**Stronger: Semrush.** Link data is a core part of its SEO plans, and it publishes the size of its index.

## BrightEdge vs Semrush site audit and automation

BrightEdge's ContentIQ audits and monitors your site, and ranks errors before they hurt performance. Autopilot goes further. BrightEdge says it makes automatic website optimizations once it is set up on your site. Intent Signal flags the content most likely to rank above the fold.

Semrush's Site Audit crawls your site and lists technical issues. Its Starter plan adds an AI-ready site audit that checks for issues that affect AI crawlers. Semrush gives you the fixes to make, and your team or developers make them.

**Stronger: BrightEdge** for large sites that want fixes applied for them. Semrush's audit is easier to start and fits teams that fix issues by hand.

## BrightEdge vs Semrush AI visibility tracking

BrightEdge AI Catalyst tracks how often your brand is mentioned and cited in AI answers. It names ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode and Gemini. It rates each answer as positive, neutral or negative, and compares you with competitors by engine. It also builds buyer prompts mapped to the buyer journey and flags when a competitor takes over an answer.

Semrush's AI tracking comes with its Starter plan and up. Starter tracks 50 prompts daily, Pro+ tracks 100, and Advanced raises the limit further. Semrush names ChatGPT and Gemini, plus Google AI Overviews and AI Mode. You can buy the AI Visibility Toolkit alone for $99 a month, with 25 prompts. It has no free trial, and each user needs their own subscription.

**Stronger: close.** BrightEdge lists more engines and adds prompt building for each buyer stage. Semrush shows you the prompt limits and price up front, so you know what you get before you buy.

## BrightEdge vs Semrush reporting and rank tracking

BrightEdge is built for reporting at scale. StoryBuilder makes SEO and content dashboards. Page Reporting and Keyword Reporting tie rankings and pages to business results. Share of Voice maps competitors at page level. Its FAQ also mentions real-time rank tracking.

Semrush tracks rankings daily across Google, Bing and Baidu. The SEO plan tracks 500 keywords across 5 websites, and Pro+ tracks 1,500 across 15. Its reports tool builds client and team reports.

**Stronger: BrightEdge** for large teams that report to leadership across many pages and markets. **Semrush** is the better fit for agencies and smaller teams that want rank tracking with clear limits.

## BrightEdge vs Semrush pricing

**BrightEdge**

BrightEdge does not publish prices. Its pricing page asks you to request a demo, and price is set in a custom contract. There is no public free plan or trial.

**Semrush (SEO and AI search plans)**

- Free: $0. 1 demo project, 10 reports a day, limited AI visibility features.
- SEO: $139/mo ($117.33/mo billed yearly). 5 websites, 500 keywords tracked daily. No prompt tracking.
- Starter: $199/mo ($165.17/mo billed yearly). 5 websites, 500 keywords, 50 prompts tracked daily.
- Pro+: $299/mo ($248.17/mo billed yearly). 15 websites, 1,500 keywords, 100 prompts daily, historical SEO data.
- Advanced: custom. 40+ websites, 5,000+ keywords, higher prompt limits, API access, team users.
- Enterprise: custom. Unlimited projects, custom limits, SSO and an account manager.
- Extra users: from $45/mo.
- AI Visibility Toolkit alone: $99/mo for 25 prompts and 1 user.

## Which should you choose: BrightEdge or Semrush?

**Pick BrightEdge if:**

- You run SEO for a large brand with many sites, markets or locations.
- You want automatic on-site fixes through Autopilot.
- Your team reports SEO and AI search results to leadership in dashboards.
- You prefer a managed contract and onboarding over a self-serve tool.

**Pick Semrush if:**

- You want public prices and a tool you can start using today.
- You need backlink data next to keyword research.
- You are an agency or a small in-house team.
- You want to try it first on a free plan or 7-day trial.

Semrush also sells a custom Enterprise plan for large companies. If you are in that range, ask both vendors for a demo.

## BrightEdge vs Semrush FAQ

### Is BrightEdge better than Semrush?

It depends on your team. BrightEdge is built for large enterprises that want automation, dashboards and a managed contract. Semrush is better for teams that want broad keyword and backlink data with public prices.

### How much does BrightEdge cost?

BrightEdge does not publish its prices. You request a demo, and the price is set in a custom contract. Semrush, by contrast, lists plans from $139 a month and has a free plan.

### Does BrightEdge track ChatGPT and AI Overviews?

Yes. BrightEdge AI Catalyst names ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode. It tracks mentions, citations and sentiment against your competitors.

### Does Semrush have an enterprise plan?

Yes. Semrush lists an Enterprise plan with custom pricing, unlimited projects, SSO and an account manager. Its Advanced plan also adds API access and team users.

### Does BrightEdge have a free trial?

BrightEdge's pages offer a demo and do not list a free trial or free plan. Semrush has a free plan and a 7-day trial on most paid plans.

## Summary: BrightEdge vs Semrush

BrightEdge is the stronger choice for large brands that want enterprise reporting and automatic site fixes, with AI search tracking across five engines. You pay a custom price set through sales. Semrush is the stronger choice for most other teams. It has bigger published keyword and backlink data, public prices and a free way to start.

If you can't tell which side you are on, start a Semrush trial. If you outgrow it or need automation across many sites, book a BrightEdge demo.
