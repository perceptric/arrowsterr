# Ubersuggest vs Semrush: Pricing and Features Compared in 2026

Ubersuggest and Semrush both cover keyword research, site audits, rank tracking and backlinks. Ubersuggest is a low-cost tool from Neil Patel's company, with plans from $29 a month and a lifetime option. Semrush is one of the largest SEO suites, with much bigger data and a higher price.

Here is the quick answer. Pick Ubersuggest if you run one or a few small sites and need to keep costs low. Pick Semrush if SEO is a main channel and you need deep keyword and backlink data with daily rank tracking.

## Ubersuggest vs Semrush: main points

- **Price:** Ubersuggest plans run from $29 to $99 a month. Semrush's SEO plan is $139 a month, and Semrush One starts at $199.
- **Lifetime plan:** Ubersuggest sells lifetime access for a one-time payment. Semrush sells monthly and yearly plans only.
- **Data:** Semrush lists more than 26 billion keywords and more than 43 trillion backlinks. Ubersuggest lists 2.2 billion+ tracked keywords and 11 billion+ indexed pages.
- **Rank tracking:** Ubersuggest tracks 125 to 300 keywords per project, by plan. Semrush's SEO plan tracks 500 keywords daily.
- **AI search:** Ubersuggest tracks 10 to 20 AI prompts per brand on ChatGPT and Gemini. Semrush's AI Visibility Toolkit tracks 25 prompts, and Semrush One tracks 50 or 100.
- **Trying it out:** Both have a free account and a 7-day trial.

[Ubersuggest](https://ubersuggest.com/) is an SEO tool from NP Digital, the marketing company Neil Patel co-founded. It covers keyword research, competitor research, site audits, rank tracking and backlinks. It also has AI Search Visibility and a Content Studio that drafts articles from your keywords. It is known for low prices and a lifetime plan. Ubersuggest says more than 250,000 marketers trust it.

### Ubersuggest history

Ubersuggest was already a keyword tool when Neil Patel bought it in February 2017 for $120,000. He wrote that his dream was an SEO tool that could compete with the big players, offered for free.

By 2020 it had grown past keywords, and Patel's post that year lists an SEO Analyzer and a Traffic Analyzer among its free features. In February 2020 he wrote that rising costs meant a change. Core features stayed free, and paid plans with a 7-day free trial unlocked more projects and data. Prices vary by country, which he called "the Netflix/Amazon approach."

In 2022 NP Digital bought AnswerThePublic, a keyword question tool based in Brighton, UK. Search Engine Land reported that it would be joined with Ubersuggest.

### Ubersuggest and AI search

Ubersuggest now tracks AI answers as well as Google. Its AI Search Visibility tool lets you read the full answers from ChatGPT and Gemini on paid plans, with Google AI Overviews on the Enterprise plan. Ubersuggest says it has indexed more than 20 million AI conversations. It also offers a ChatGPT app and an MCP connection for AI assistants such as Claude.

[Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/) is one of the largest SEO suites. It is split into toolkits, such as SEO, AI Visibility, Advertising and Local. You can buy each toolkit on its own. Semrush One bundles the SEO and AI Visibility toolkits on one plan. Adobe says Semrush has more than 28 million users, from startups to Fortune 500 companies.

### Semrush history

Semrush has its roots in SEOquake, a browser add-on for SEO data. Oleg Shchegolev and Dmitri Melnikov first released the product as Seodigger, then as a Firefox extension. The company took the SeoQuake name in 2007, and the two founders started Semrush in 2008. Its head office is in Boston.

Semrush raised $40 million in April 2018, with Greycroft among the lead investors. It listed on the New York Stock Exchange on March 25, 2021, under the ticker SEMR. It went on to buy other marketing brands. Backlinko joined in 2022, and Brand24 and Exploding Topics followed in 2024. In March 2025 Bill Wagner became CEO, and Shchegolev moved to CTO.

### Semrush and AI search

Semrush launched its AI Toolkit on March 4, 2025, to show how AI tools such as ChatGPT and Perplexity describe a brand. On October 29, 2025, it launched Semrush One, which joins SEO and AI search tracking on one plan. In November 2025 Adobe agreed to buy Semrush for about $1.9 billion. The deal closed on April 28, 2026, and the company now runs as "Semrush, an Adobe company."

## Ubersuggest vs Semrush FAQ

### Is Ubersuggest better than Semrush?

Ubersuggest is cheaper and simpler. Semrush has far more data and more tools. For a small site on a tight budget, Ubersuggest is often enough. For a team that relies on SEO, Semrush does more.

### Is Ubersuggest free?

Ubersuggest has a free account for light research, and paid plans start at $29 a month. Monthly plans include a one-time 7-day free trial. Semrush also has a free account with 10 reports a day.

### Does Ubersuggest have a lifetime plan?

Yes. Ubersuggest sells lifetime access for a one-time payment, and the purchase cannot be refunded. Semrush has no lifetime plan.

### Is Ubersuggest data as good as Semrush data?

Ubersuggest's database is smaller. It lists 2.2 billion+ keywords, and Semrush lists more than 26 billion. Semrush also lists more than 43 trillion backlinks, while Ubersuggest caps backlink reports at 10,000 rows on its top plan.

### Do Ubersuggest and Semrush track AI search?

Yes, both do. Ubersuggest tracks 10 to 20 prompts per brand on ChatGPT and Gemini, depending on the plan. Semrush tracks 25 prompts with its AI Visibility Toolkit and 50 or 100 with Semrush One, across Google's AI results, ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity.

### Who owns Ubersuggest?

NP Digital owns Ubersuggest. Neil Patel bought the tool in February 2017, and he co-founded NP Digital.

## Summary: Ubersuggest vs Semrush

Ubersuggest is a low-cost SEO tool with a lifetime option. It covers the SEO basics for one or a few small sites. Semrush is a full suite with far larger data and daily rank tracking. Its AI search tracking also goes further.

If you are starting out, try Ubersuggest's free account or its 7-day trial. If SEO already drives your business, start with Semrush's free account or its 7-day trial.
