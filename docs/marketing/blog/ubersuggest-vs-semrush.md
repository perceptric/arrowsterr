# Ubersuggest vs Semrush: Pricing and Features Compared in 2026

Running one small site on a tight budget? Ubersuggest starts at $29 a month and sells a lifetime plan. Semrush's SEO plan starts at $139 a month and brings far bigger keyword and backlink data.

Ubersuggest comes from NP Digital, Neil Patel's company. Semrush is one of the largest SEO suites. Each covers keyword research, site audits, rank tracking and backlinks.

Stick with Ubersuggest if you run one or a few small sites and need to keep costs low. Move to Semrush when SEO is a main channel and you need deep keyword and backlink data with daily rank tracking.

## Ubersuggest vs Semrush: main points

- Ubersuggest plans run from $29 to $99 a month. Semrush's SEO plan is $139 a month, and Semrush One starts at $199.
- Ubersuggest sells lifetime access for a one time payment. Semrush sells monthly and yearly plans only.
- Semrush has more than 26 billion keywords and more than 43 trillion backlinks. Ubersuggest has 2.2 billion+ tracked keywords and 11 billion+ indexed pages.
- Ubersuggest tracks 125 to 300 keywords per project, by plan. Semrush's SEO plan tracks 500 keywords daily.
- Ubersuggest tracks 10 to 20 AI prompts per brand on ChatGPT and Gemini. Semrush's AI Visibility Toolkit tracks 25 prompts, and Semrush One tracks 50 or 100.
- Each has a free account and a 7 day trial.

[Ubersuggest](https://ubersuggest.com/) is an SEO tool from NP Digital, the marketing company Neil Patel cofounded. It covers keyword research, competitor research, site audits, rank tracking and backlinks. It also has AI Search Visibility and a Content Studio that drafts articles from your keywords. It is known for low prices and a lifetime plan, and more than 250,000 marketers trust it.

### Ubersuggest history

Ubersuggest was already a keyword tool when Neil Patel bought it in February 2017 for $120,000. His goal was a free SEO tool that could compete with the big players.

By 2020 it had grown past keywords, with a free SEO Analyzer and a free Traffic Analyzer. In February 2020 rising costs forced a change. Core features stayed free, and paid plans with a 7 day free trial added more projects and data. Prices vary by country, the way Netflix and Amazon set theirs.

In 2022 NP Digital bought AnswerThePublic, a keyword question tool based in Brighton, UK, with a plan to join it with Ubersuggest.

### Ubersuggest and AI search

Ubersuggest now tracks AI answers as well as Google. Its AI Search Visibility tool lets you read the full answers from ChatGPT and Gemini on paid plans, with Google AI Overviews on the Enterprise plan. Ubersuggest has indexed more than 20 million AI conversations. It also has a ChatGPT app and an MCP connection for AI assistants such as Claude.

[Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/) is one of the largest SEO suites. It is split into toolkits, such as SEO, AI Visibility, Advertising and Local. You can buy each toolkit on its own. Semrush One bundles the SEO and AI Visibility toolkits on one plan. Semrush has more than 28 million users, from startups to Fortune 500 companies.

### Semrush history

Semrush has its roots in SEOquake, a browser addon for SEO data. Oleg Shchegolev and Dmitri Melnikov first released the product as Seodigger, then as a Firefox extension. The company took the SeoQuake name in 2007, and the two founders started Semrush in 2008. Its head office is in Boston.

Semrush raised $40 million in April 2018, with Greycroft among the lead investors. It listed on the New York Stock Exchange on March 25, 2021, under the ticker SEMR. It went on to buy other marketing brands. Backlinko joined in 2022, and Brand24 and Exploding Topics followed in 2024. In March 2025 Bill Wagner became CEO, and Shchegolev moved to CTO.

### Semrush and AI search

Semrush launched its AI Toolkit on March 4, 2025, to show how AI tools such as ChatGPT and Perplexity describe a brand. On October 29, 2025, it launched Semrush One, which joins SEO and AI search tracking on one plan. In November 2025 Adobe agreed to buy Semrush for about $1.9 billion. The deal closed on April 28, 2026, and the company now runs as "Semrush, an Adobe company."

## Ubersuggest vs Semrush FAQ

### Is Ubersuggest better than Semrush?

Ubersuggest costs less. Semrush has far more data and more tools. For a small site on a tight budget, Ubersuggest is often enough. For a team that relies on SEO, Semrush does more.

### Is Ubersuggest free?

Ubersuggest has a free account for light research, and paid plans start at $29 a month. Monthly plans include a one time 7 day free trial. Semrush also has a free account with 10 reports a day.

### Does Ubersuggest have a lifetime plan?

Yes. Ubersuggest sells lifetime access for a one time payment, and the purchase cannot be refunded. Semrush has no lifetime plan.

### Is Ubersuggest data as good as Semrush data?

Ubersuggest's database is smaller: 2.2 billion+ keywords against more than 26 billion in Semrush. Semrush also has more than 43 trillion backlinks, while Ubersuggest caps backlink reports at 10,000 rows on its top plan.

### Do Ubersuggest and Semrush track AI search?

Yes, both do. Ubersuggest tracks 10 to 20 prompts per brand on ChatGPT and Gemini, depending on the plan. Semrush tracks 25 prompts with its AI Visibility Toolkit and 50 or 100 with Semrush One, across Google's AI results, ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity.

### Who owns Ubersuggest?

NP Digital owns Ubersuggest. Neil Patel bought the tool in February 2017, and he cofounded NP Digital.

## Summary: Ubersuggest vs Semrush

Start where your site is today. A new blog or side project can run on Ubersuggest's free account, and the $29 plan adds 125 tracked keywords when you need them.

Once SEO drives your business, open a free Semrush account and run its 7 day trial. You will see quickly what 26 billion keywords and daily rankings add.
