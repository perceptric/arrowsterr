# Ubersuggest vs Semrush: Pricing and Features Compared in 2026

Ubersuggest and Semrush both cover keyword research, site audits, rank tracking and backlinks. Ubersuggest is a low-cost tool from Neil Patel's company, with plans from $29 a month and a lifetime option. Semrush is one of the largest SEO suites, with much bigger data and a higher price.

Here is the quick answer. Pick Ubersuggest if you run one or a few small sites and need to keep costs low. Pick Semrush if SEO is a main channel and you need deep keyword and backlink data with daily rank tracking.

## Ubersuggest vs Semrush: main points

- **Price:** Ubersuggest plans run from $29 to $99 a month. Semrush's SEO plan is $139 a month, and Semrush One starts at $199.
- **Lifetime plan:** Ubersuggest sells lifetime access for a one-time payment. Semrush sells monthly and yearly plans only.
- **Data:** Semrush lists more than 26 billion keywords and more than 43 trillion backlinks. Ubersuggest lists 2.2 billion+ tracked keywords and 11 billion+ indexed pages.
- **Rank tracking:** Ubersuggest tracks 125 to 300 keywords per project, by plan. Semrush's SEO plan tracks 500 keywords daily.
- **AI search:** Ubersuggest tracks 10 to 20 AI prompts per brand on ChatGPT and Gemini. Semrush's AI Visibility Toolkit tracks 25 prompts, and Semrush One tracks 50 or 100.
- **Trying it out:** Both have a free account and a 7-day trial.

## What is Ubersuggest?

Ubersuggest is an SEO tool from Neil Patel's company. It covers keyword research, competitor research, site audits, rank tracking and backlinks. It also has AI Search Visibility and a Content Studio that drafts articles from your keywords. It is known for low prices and a lifetime plan.

## What is Semrush?

Semrush is one of the largest SEO suites. It is split into toolkits, such as SEO, AI Visibility, Advertising and Local. You can buy each toolkit on its own. Semrush One bundles the SEO and AI Visibility toolkits on one plan.

## Ubersuggest vs Semrush keyword research

Both tools show search volume and keyword difficulty for each keyword. Ubersuggest also shows CPC and intent, and it finds ideas from autocomplete, related terms, questions and comparisons. Its plans include 20,000 to 100,000 keyword suggestions a month.

The gap is data size. Semrush's Keyword Magic Tool lists more than 26 billion keywords. Ubersuggest lists 2.2 billion+ tracked keywords across 230+ markets.

Semrush is stronger for keyword research. Its bigger database finds more long-tail ideas. Ubersuggest covers the basics well for a small site, and its daily report limits of 150 to 900 are enough for light research.

## Ubersuggest vs Semrush backlink data

Semrush is much stronger here. It lists more than 43 trillion backlinks and 390 million referring domains, and it says the database refreshes at least hourly.

Ubersuggest shows backlink profiles and the domains that link to rivals but not to you. Its plans cap backlink reports at 2,000, 5,000 or 10,000 rows. Link history goes back 3 years, and new and lost links cover 30 days. Ubersuggest does not list the size of its backlink index.

If link building is part of your plan, Semrush gives you far more to work with. Ubersuggest works for a quick look at your own links.

## Ubersuggest vs Semrush rank tracking

Semrush tracks keywords daily. Its SEO plan includes 500 tracked keywords across 5 websites. Semrush One Pro+ raises that to 1,500 keywords and adds tracking by location and device.

Ubersuggest's pricing page lists 125, 150 or 300 tracked keywords per project, updated weekly. Its home page describes daily tracking, so confirm the update speed with Ubersuggest. Plans include 1, 7 or 15 projects. Ubersuggest adds mobile rank tracking and pixel rank tracking.

Semrush is stronger if you need daily data on many keywords for one site. Ubersuggest spreads a smaller number of keywords across several projects.

## Ubersuggest vs Semrush site audit

Both tools crawl your site and flag technical SEO issues. Ubersuggest's pricing page lists 1,000, 5,000 or 10,000 pages a week, by plan.

Semrush includes Site Audit in its SEO Toolkit. Semrush One Starter adds an AI-ready Site Audit. It checks whether your site blocks eight AI crawlers, including bots from ChatGPT, Perplexity and Claude.

Semrush is stronger for larger sites and for AI crawler checks. Ubersuggest's audit covers a small site's needs.

## Ubersuggest vs Semrush AI visibility tracking

Ubersuggest includes AI Search Visibility on its paid plans. It tracks ChatGPT and Gemini. Reports show your visibility score and share of voice, plus sentiment. The limits are small and grow with the plan:

- Entry plan: 10 prompts per brand, updated monthly.
- Middle plan: 15 prompts per brand, updated every two weeks.
- Top plan: 20 prompts per brand, updated weekly.

Extra AI prompts are a $5 a month add-on.

Semrush's AI Visibility Toolkit is $99 a month on its own. It includes 1 project, 1 Brand Performance domain and 25 tracked prompts. Extra prompts cost $60 a month for each 50, and each extra user needs a $99 license. The toolkit has no free trial. Semrush One raises the limits to 50 prompts on Starter and 100 on Pro+. Semrush's AI reports cover Google AI Overviews, AI Mode, ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity, with coverage that varies by report.

Semrush is stronger for AI tracking. It covers more AIs and more prompts, and it updates them more often. Ubersuggest's AI tracking is a light add-on that suits a first look. Neither tool lists Claude among the AIs it tracks.

## Ubersuggest vs Semrush pricing

Semrush prices come from its pricing pages in November 2026. Ubersuggest's pricing page lists 3 plans from $29 to $99 a month, with the limits below.

**Ubersuggest**

- **Free:** A free account for light research. Ubersuggest does not list the daily limit on its pages.
- **Entry plan:** $29 per month. 150 reports a day, 1 project, 125 tracked keywords, 10 AI prompts per brand and 1,000 audit pages a week.
- **Middle plan:** price shown on the pricing page. 300 reports a day, 7 projects, 150 tracked keywords per project, 15 AI prompts per brand and 5,000 audit pages a week.
- **Top plan:** $99 per month. 900 reports a day, 15 projects, 300 tracked keywords per project, 20 AI prompts per brand and 10,000 audit pages a week.
- **Lifetime:** One-time payment. Lifetime purchases are final and cannot be refunded.
- **Add-ons:** $5 a month each for extra projects, users, daily searches, tracked keywords or AI prompts.
- **Trial:** 7 days, once, for monthly plans.

**Semrush**

- **Free:** $0. 1 demo project and 10 reports a day.
- **SEO:** $139 per month, or $117.33 per month billed yearly. 5 websites and 500 tracked keywords.
- **Semrush One Starter:** $199 per month, or $165.17 per month billed yearly. 5 websites, 500 keywords, 50 AI prompts daily, AI-ready Site Audit and MCP access.
- **Semrush One Pro+:** $299 per month, or $248.17 per month billed yearly. 15 websites, 1,500 keywords, 100 AI prompts, historical data and content optimization.
- **Semrush One Advanced:** Custom price.
- **AI Visibility Toolkit:** $99 per month for 25 prompts and 1 user. No trial.
- **Extras:** Extra users from $45 a month. 7-day trial.

## Which should you choose: Ubersuggest or Semrush?

**Pick Ubersuggest if:**

- You run one site or a few small ones.
- You want to keep your SEO tool under $100 a month.
- You would rather pay once with a lifetime plan.
- You need keyword ideas and a basic audit more than deep data.

**Pick Semrush if:**

- SEO is a main source of traffic or leads for you.
- You need large keyword and backlink databases.
- You want daily rank tracking on hundreds of keywords.
- You want AI search tracking with more prompts and more AIs.

## Ubersuggest vs Semrush FAQ

### Is Ubersuggest better than Semrush?

Ubersuggest is cheaper and simpler. Semrush has far more data and more tools. For a small site on a tight budget, Ubersuggest is often enough. For a team that relies on SEO, Semrush does more.

### Is Ubersuggest free?

Ubersuggest has a free account for light research, and paid plans start at $29 a month. Monthly plans include a one-time 7-day free trial. Semrush also has a free account with 10 reports a day.

### Does Ubersuggest have a lifetime plan?

Yes. Ubersuggest sells lifetime access for a one-time payment, and the purchase cannot be refunded. Semrush has no lifetime plan.

### Is Ubersuggest data as good as Semrush data?

Ubersuggest's database is smaller. It lists 2.2 billion+ keywords, and Semrush lists more than 26 billion. Semrush also lists more than 43 trillion backlinks, while Ubersuggest caps backlink reports at 10,000 rows on its top plan.

### Do Ubersuggest and Semrush track AI search?

Yes, both do. Ubersuggest tracks 10 to 20 prompts per brand on ChatGPT and Gemini, depending on the plan. Semrush tracks 25 prompts with its AI Visibility Toolkit and 50 or 100 with Semrush One, across Google's AI results, ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity.

## Summary: Ubersuggest vs Semrush

Ubersuggest is a low-cost SEO tool with a lifetime option. It covers the SEO basics for one or a few small sites. Semrush is a full suite with far larger data and daily rank tracking. Its AI search tracking also goes further.

If you are starting out, try Ubersuggest's free account or its 7-day trial. If SEO already drives your business, start with Semrush's free account or its 7-day trial.
