# Semrush vs Moz: SEO Tools, AI Tracking and Pricing Compared in 2026

Moz Pro starts at $49 a month. Semrush starts at $139. Moz also puts AI prompt tracking in every plan, while Semrush sells it through Semrush One or a separate toolkit. What Semrush gives you for the higher price is bigger databases and daily rank tracking.

Each suite covers keyword research, backlinks, rank tracking and site audits, and each now tracks how AI answers mention your brand. Choose Semrush for daily rankings and the largest keyword and link data. Choose Moz for a lower priced suite focused on core SEO, with AI prompt tracking included in every Moz Pro plan.

## Semrush vs Moz: main points

- Moz Starter is $49 a month and Moz Pro Standard is $99. Semrush's SEO plan is $139 a month.
- Every Moz Pro plan includes AI prompt tracking, from 150 prompts checked weekly. On Semrush, AI prompt tracking comes with Semrush One (from $199 a month) or the $99 AI Visibility Toolkit.
- Semrush checks rankings and AI prompts daily. Moz checks AI prompts weekly, and how often its campaigns update rankings isn't public.
- Semrush holds 28.8 billion keywords and 43 trillion backlinks. Moz has a 1.25 billion+ keyword index and over 40 trillion links.
- Moz created Domain Authority and adds Page Authority, Brand Authority and Spam Score. Semrush uses its own Authority Score.
- Semrush has a 7 day free trial on its paid plans. Moz has a free trial on its Medium plan, and its length isn't public.

### What Semrush does today

[Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/) is a marketing suite built around SEO. It sells toolkits for SEO, AI visibility, traffic and market research, local, content, social and ads. The SEO toolkit covers keyword research, competitor analysis, Position Tracking and Site Audit. Semrush One bundles the SEO and AI Visibility toolkits on one plan.

Semrush is now a brand visibility platform with over 28 million users, from startups to large enterprises. It became part of Adobe in April 2026.

### How Semrush grew

Semrush was founded in 2008 by Oleg Shchegolev and Dmitri Melnikov. Its roots are in Seodigger, an early SEO tool that later turned into a Firefox extension. "SEM" in the name means search engine marketing, and the company switched its spelling from "SEMrush" to "Semrush" in December 2020.

The company listed on the New York Stock Exchange in March 2021 as SEMR. Over the next few years it grew through deals. Backlinko and Kompyte joined in 2022, and Traffic Think Tank in 2023. In 2024 Semrush bought Third Door Media, Brand24, Ryte, Datos and Exploding Topics.

By 2025 the focus had moved to AI search. Semrush launched Semrush One on October 29, 2025, joining SEO and AI visibility in one subscription. In November 2025, Adobe agreed to buy the company for about $1.9 billion. Adobe completed the deal on April 28, 2026, and the product continues with no immediate changes for customers.

### What Moz does today

[Moz](https://moz.com/) is one of the oldest SEO software companies, and it is now part of Ziff Davis. Moz Pro is its SEO suite, with Keyword Explorer, Link Explorer, Site Crawl, rank tracking and AI visibility dashboards. Moz also sells Moz Local for listings, STAT for enterprise SERP tracking, the Moz API and Moz AI Explorer on its own.

Moz is known for Domain Authority, a score many SEOs use to judge sites. It also runs free learning resources, such as the Beginner's Guide to SEO and Moz Academy.

### Moz history

Rand Fishkin and Gillian Muessig founded the company in 2004 as SEOmoz. It started as a blog and online community where early SEO experts shared research. It published the Beginner's Guide to SEO and its first Search Ranking Factors study, then grew into a small consulting firm that built its first SEO tools.

Software soon took over. SEOmoz raised its first funding round in 2007 and had 5,000 subscribers by 2009, the year Whiteboard Friday videos began. In 2012 it raised an $18 million Series B led by Foundry Group. It also bought Followerwonk, a Twitter analytics tool, and GetListed, which became Moz Local.

In 2013 SEOmoz renamed itself Moz and launched a wider suite called Moz Analytics. In 2016 it pulled back to focus on search. Moz bought STAT Search Analytics in 2018 for daily rankings and SERP data. In 2021 Moz was bought by iContact, which is part of Ziff Davis. Today every Moz Pro plan includes AI Visibility Dashboards, and Moz AI Explorer tracks what AI assistants say about your brand.

## Which should you choose: Semrush or Moz?

**Pick Semrush if:**

- You need daily rank tracking and daily AI prompt checks.
- You do heavy keyword research and need a large database.
- You want content, local and ads tools from one vendor.
- You want a 7 day trial on any paid plan.

**Pick Moz if:**

- You want a lower entry price for a full SEO suite.
- You want AI prompt tracking included in your base plan.
- Your team uses Domain Authority and Spam Score for link work.
- You manage a few sites and weekly data is enough.

## Semrush vs Moz FAQ

### Is Moz cheaper than Semrush?

Yes, at the entry level. Moz Starter is $49 a month and Standard is $99. Semrush's SEO plan is $139 a month. Higher up, Moz Large and Semrush One Pro+ both cost $299 a month.

### Is Semrush better than Moz for keyword research?

For most teams, yes. Semrush has 28.8 billion keywords, and Moz has 1.25 billion+. Moz Standard also caps keyword queries at 150 a month.

### Does Moz track AI search?

Yes. Every Moz Pro plan includes AI Visibility Dashboards that track ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode weekly. Standard includes 150 prompts. Moz also sells AI Explorer on its own from $45 a month.

### Does Semrush use Domain Authority?

No. Domain Authority is Moz's metric. Semrush has its own score, called Authority Score. The two use different formulas, so don't compare one to the other directly.

### Does Semrush or Moz have a free plan?

Semrush has a free account with 1 demo project and 10 reports a day. Moz has a free Moz Community account with limited access to MozBar, Keyword Explorer and Link Explorer. Both also have free trials on paid plans.

### Who owns Semrush and Moz?

Adobe owns Semrush, after closing its purchase on April 28, 2026. Moz is part of Ziff Davis, through iContact, which bought it in 2021.

## Summary: Semrush vs Moz

Moz Pro is the pick if you track a few sites and weekly data is enough. You pay less and get AI prompts in every plan, and Domain Authority is still a standard for link work.

Semrush is the pick if you need daily data and depth. It has 28.8 billion keywords and daily rankings. AI prompt tracking comes through Semrush One or the AI Visibility Toolkit, and other toolkits cover most marketing jobs.
