# Search Atlas vs Semrush: Features, AI Tracking and Pricing Compared in 2026

Should your SEO tool change your site for you? Search Atlas does. It's built around OTTO SEO, an agent that finds on page and technical issues and pushes fixes to your live site. Semrush is built around research data. Its keyword and backlink database is far larger, and its reports leave the changes to you. Each is an all in one SEO platform with AI search tracking.

Pick Search Atlas if you want software that makes SEO changes for you, with content and local SEO tools on the same plan. Pick Semrush if research is your main job and you want the bigger data set, or if you want a free account before you pay.

### What Search Atlas does today

[Search Atlas](https://searchatlas.com/) is an SEO platform that does the work as well as reporting on it. The core is OTTO SEO. You connect it through a pixel, your CMS, Cloudflare or your code pipeline, and it deploys changes such as page titles, meta descriptions, headings, schema and internal links. Atlas Agent takes requests in your own words and suggests next steps.

Around OTTO sit research tools (Site Explorer, Keyword Research, Backlink Analyzer, Site Auditor), content tools (Topical Maps, Content Genius) and local tools (GBP Galactic, Local Citations). LLM Visibility tracks AI answers. Every plan runs on a monthly pool of Universal Credits that all tools draw from. More than 5,000 agencies use OTTO.

### Search Atlas history

Search Atlas comes from the team behind LinkGraph, an SEO company Manick Bhan founded in the United States. Bhan is the founder, CEO and CTO of Search Atlas, and Sophia Deluz-Bhan is its cofounder and COO. The company is based in New York City.

It started as an SEO research platform. OTTO arrived in 2023 and turned Search Atlas from a reporting tool into one that makes changes on your site. In August 2024 Search Atlas bought Signal Genesys, a press release distribution service. The team behind LinkGraph and Signal Genesys now builds the platform.

LLM Visibility came as AI search grew. It tracks brand mentions, sentiment, share of voice and your rank inside AI answers. The higher plans now cover six AIs, Claude and Copilot among them.

### What Semrush does today

[Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/) is a cloud marketing suite built around SEO. Its main plans pair the SEO Toolkit with the AI Visibility Toolkit. The SEO plan covers SEO alone, while Starter, Pro+ and Advanced add AI search tracking under the Semrush One name.

Traffic and market data, local SEO, content, social, ads and AI PR each come as a separate toolkit. You add only the ones you need.

### Semrush history

Semrush is part of Adobe now. Adobe agreed to buy it in November 2025 for $1.9 billion and closed the deal on April 28, 2026.

The company goes back to 2008, when Oleg Shchegolev and Dmitri Melnikov founded it. Its roots are in Seodigger and SeoQuake, a Firefox extension that showed SEO data as you browsed. The name comes from SEM, short for search engine marketing. In December 2020 the styling changed from "SEMrush" to "Semrush."

In 2018 Semrush raised $40 million in a round led jointly by Greycroft, E.ventures and Siguler Guff. It went public on the New York Stock Exchange in March 2021 under the ticker SEMR. It then bought other marketing companies, including Backlinko in 2022 and Exploding Topics and Third Door Media in 2024. The AI Toolkit launched in March 2025 to track brands in ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity. Semrush One followed in October 2025 and joined the SEO Toolkit and the AI Visibility Toolkit in one plan.

Site Explorer shows any domain's organic and paid keywords, traffic, backlinks and competitors. Next to its own Domain Power score, it shows Domain Rating, Domain Authority, Trust Flow and Citation Flow. Backlink Analyzer covers referring domains, anchors, new and lost links and spam signals. The size of its backlink index is not public.

## Search Atlas vs Semrush FAQ

### Is Search Atlas better than Semrush?

For SEO changes made for you, yes. Search Atlas wins when you want an agent to make changes and more tools on one plan. For research, Semrush wins with a larger keyword and backlink database.

### Is Search Atlas cheaper than Semrush?

At the entry level, yes. Search Atlas Starter is $99 a month against Semrush's $139 SEO plan. At $199, each vendor's second plan adds more AI tracking. Higher up it flips: Search Atlas Pro is $399, and Semrush Pro+ is $299.

### What is OTTO SEO?

OTTO SEO is the Search Atlas agent that deploys SEO fixes to your live site. It handles items such as titles, meta descriptions, schema and internal links. It connects through a pixel, your CMS, Cloudflare or your code pipeline. Search Atlas launched it in 2023.

### Does Search Atlas track ChatGPT?

Yes. ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode tracking starts on the Growth plan. Pro and Agency add Copilot, Perplexity and Claude. Starter covers 1 AI.

### Does Search Atlas have a free plan?

No. Search Atlas has a 7 day trial with a free onboarding call. Semrush has both a free account and a 7 day trial.

### Who owns Search Atlas?

Manick Bhan founded Search Atlas and runs it as CEO and CTO. It comes from the team behind LinkGraph and Signal Genesys, and it is based in New York City.
