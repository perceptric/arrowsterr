# Search Atlas vs Semrush: Features, AI Tracking and Pricing Compared in 2026

Search Atlas and Semrush are both all-in-one SEO platforms with AI search tracking. They differ in what they do for you. Search Atlas is built around OTTO SEO, an agent that finds on-page and technical issues and pushes fixes to your live site. Semrush is built around research data. It has a very large keyword and backlink database, and its reports leave the changes to you.

Here is the quick answer. Pick Search Atlas if you want software that makes SEO changes for you, with content and local SEO tools on the same plan. Pick Semrush if research is your main job and you want the bigger data set, or if you want a free account before you pay.

## Search Atlas vs Semrush: main points

- **Approach:** Search Atlas deploys fixes through OTTO SEO on every plan. Semrush finds issues and leaves the fix to you.
- **Entry price:** Search Atlas Starter is $99 a month. Semrush's SEO plan is $139 a month, or $117.33 a month billed yearly.
- **AI search tracking:** Search Atlas Growth ($199) tracks ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode, and Pro adds Copilot, Perplexity and Claude. Semrush Starter ($199) tracks 50 prompts daily across ChatGPT, Google, Gemini and Perplexity.
- **Data size:** Semrush lists 28.8 billion keywords and 43 trillion backlinks. Search Atlas lists 5.2 billion+ keywords.
- **Seats:** Search Atlas includes 1 to 10 seats by plan. Semrush plans include 1 user, and extra users start at $45 a month.
- **Free use:** Both offer a 7-day trial. Semrush also has a free account with 10 reports a day.

## What is Search Atlas?

Search Atlas is an SEO platform that does the work as well as reporting on it. Its main part is OTTO SEO. You connect it through a pixel, your CMS, Cloudflare or your code pipeline. It then deploys changes such as page titles, meta descriptions, headings, schema and internal links. Atlas Agent takes requests typed in plain words and suggests next steps.

Around that sit research tools (Site Explorer, Keyword Research, Backlink Analyzer, Site Auditor), content tools (Topical Maps, Content Genius) and local tools (GBP Galactic, Local Citations). LLM Visibility tracks AI answers. Every plan runs on a monthly pool of Universal Credits that all tools draw from.

## What is Semrush?

Semrush is a cloud marketing suite built around SEO. Its main plans combine the SEO Toolkit and the AI Visibility Toolkit. The SEO plan covers SEO alone. Starter, Pro+ and Advanced add AI search tracking, and Semrush sells them under the Semrush One name.

Semrush also sells add-on toolkits for traffic and market data, local SEO, content, social, ads and AI PR. You add only the ones you need.

## Search Atlas vs Semrush keyword research and backlink data

Semrush has the bigger data set. It lists 28.8 billion keywords, 808 million domains and 43 trillion backlinks. Its own backlink crawler reads about 10 billion pages a day.

Search Atlas lists 5.2 billion+ keywords with volume, difficulty, CPC and historical volume. Keyword Magic expands a seed keyword into related terms tagged by intent. Site Explorer shows any domain's organic and paid keywords, traffic, backlinks and competitors. It also shows Domain Rating, Domain Authority, Trust Flow and Citation Flow next to its own Domain Power score. Backlink Analyzer covers referring domains, anchors, new and lost links and spam signals. The pages we checked don't list the size of its backlink index.

**Stronger:** Semrush. Its keyword database is several times larger, and it publishes the size of its link index. For most competitor research, more data means fewer gaps.

## Search Atlas vs Semrush site audit and on-page fixes

This is where Search Atlas stands apart. Semrush's Site Audit finds issues and tells you how to fix them. Starter and up add an AI-ready Site Audit. Your team or developer still makes the changes.

Search Atlas finds the same kinds of issues, then OTTO SEO can push the fix live. It works with WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, Cloudflare and custom sites. Search Atlas says its time-saved figures leave out review and approval time, so plan for someone to check changes before they go out.

| | Search Atlas | Semrush |
|---|---|---|
| Finds issues | Yes, Site Auditor | Yes, Site Audit |
| Applies fixes | Yes, OTTO SEO | No |
| Pages crawled | 50,000 (Starter), 100,000 (Growth), 10 million (Pro), unlimited (Agency) | Not listed on the prices page |

**Stronger:** Search Atlas, if you want fixes made for you and you are fine with changes going live through a pixel or CMS link. Semrush if your team wants full control over every change.

## Search Atlas vs Semrush rank tracking

Semrush tracks rankings daily on every paid plan. The SEO and Starter plans track 500 keywords, Pro+ tracks 1,500 and Advanced 5,000+. Pro+ adds tracking for several locations and devices.

Search Atlas tracks 2,000 keywords on Starter, 3,500 on Growth and 6,000 on Pro. The Agency plan lists 50,000 tracked keywords in its limits and unlimited keyword tracking in its features, so confirm the limit with sales. Rank checks draw from your Universal Credits pool. The pricing page doesn't state how often rankings update.

**Stronger:** Search Atlas for the number of keywords per dollar. Semrush for a clear daily update on every plan.

Semrush also sells its AI Visibility Toolkit alone for $99 a month, with 25 prompts and 1 domain and no free trial.

**Stronger:** About even. Search Atlas covers more AIs on its higher plans, including Claude and Copilot. Semrush gives a clear daily prompt count and Perplexity coverage from its $199 plan.

## Search Atlas vs Semrush content and local tools

Search Atlas puts content and local tools on every plan. Topical Maps turns a seed keyword into topic clusters, and Content Genius drafts articles and publishes to your CMS. Its SCHOLAR feature grades drafts. GBP Galactic manages Google Business Profiles, with 3 profiles on Starter and 100 on Agency. From Growth up, you also get Meta and Google ad studios.

Semrush includes content optimization on Pro+. Article writing, local listings, social and ads each sit in their own add-on toolkit with its own price.

**Stronger:** Search Atlas, if you want all of it on one plan. Semrush, if you only want to pay for the toolkits you use.

## Search Atlas vs Semrush FAQ

### Is Search Atlas better than Semrush?

It depends on the job. Search Atlas is stronger when you want an agent to make SEO changes and want more tools on one plan. Semrush is stronger for research, with a larger keyword and backlink database.

### Is Search Atlas cheaper than Semrush?

Search Atlas starts lower, with Starter at $99 a month against Semrush's $139 SEO plan. At $199, both vendors' second plans add more AI tracking. Search Atlas Pro is $399, and Semrush Pro+ is $299.

### What is OTTO SEO?

OTTO SEO is Search Atlas's agent that deploys SEO fixes to your live site. It handles items such as titles, meta descriptions, schema and internal links. It connects through a pixel, your CMS, Cloudflare or your code pipeline.

### Does Search Atlas track ChatGPT?

Yes. ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode tracking starts on the Growth plan. Pro and Agency add Copilot, Perplexity and Claude. Starter covers 1 AI.

### Does Search Atlas have a free plan?

The pricing page lists no free plan. It offers a 7-day trial with a free onboarding call. Semrush has both a free account and a 7-day trial.

## Summary: Search Atlas vs Semrush

Search Atlas suits teams that want SEO work done for them. OTTO SEO pushes fixes live, and more tools come on the same plan with several seats included.

Semrush suits teams that want the deepest research data and daily rank tracking, and that make site changes themselves. Both have a 7-day trial, so run each on the same site and compare what you get done in a week.
