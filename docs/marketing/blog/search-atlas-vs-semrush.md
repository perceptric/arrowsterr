# Search Atlas vs Semrush: Features, AI Tracking and Pricing Compared in 2026

Search Atlas and Semrush are both all-in-one SEO platforms with AI search tracking. They differ in what they do for you. Search Atlas is built around OTTO SEO, an agent that finds on-page and technical issues and pushes fixes to your live site. Semrush is built around research data. It has a very large keyword and backlink database, and its reports leave the changes to you.

Here is the quick answer. Pick Search Atlas if you want software that makes SEO changes for you, with content and local SEO tools on the same plan. Pick Semrush if research is your main job and you want the bigger data set, or if you want a free account before you pay.

### What Search Atlas does today

[Search Atlas](https://searchatlas.com/) is an SEO platform that does the work as well as reporting on it. Its main part is OTTO SEO. You connect it through a pixel, your CMS, Cloudflare or your code pipeline. It then deploys changes such as page titles, meta descriptions, headings, schema and internal links. Atlas Agent takes requests typed in plain words and suggests next steps.

Around that sit research tools (Site Explorer, Keyword Research, Backlink Analyzer, Site Auditor), content tools (Topical Maps, Content Genius) and local tools (GBP Galactic, Local Citations). LLM Visibility tracks AI answers. Every plan runs on a monthly pool of Universal Credits that all tools draw from. Search Atlas says more than 5,000 agencies use OTTO.

### Search Atlas history

Search Atlas comes from the team behind LinkGraph, an SEO company founded by Manick Bhan in the United States. Bhan is the founder, CEO and CTO of Search Atlas, and Sophia Deluz-Bhan is its co-founder and COO. The company is based in New York City.

It started as an SEO research platform. In 2023 Search Atlas introduced OTTO, the agent that turned it from a reporting tool into one that makes changes on your site. In August 2024 it bought Signal Genesys, a press release distribution service. Search Atlas now describes itself as built by the team behind LinkGraph and Signal Genesys.

As AI search grew, Search Atlas added LLM Visibility. It tracks brand mentions, sentiment, share of voice and your rank inside AI answers. The higher plans now cover six AIs, including Claude and Copilot.

### What Semrush does today

[Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/) is a cloud marketing suite built around SEO. Its main plans combine the SEO Toolkit and the AI Visibility Toolkit. The SEO plan covers SEO alone. Starter, Pro+ and Advanced add AI search tracking, and Semrush sells them under the Semrush One name.

Semrush also sells add-on toolkits for traffic and market data, local SEO, content, social, ads and AI PR. You add only the ones you need.

### Semrush history

Oleg Shchegolev and Dmitri Melnikov founded Semrush in 2008. Its roots go back to Seodigger and SeoQuake, a Firefox add-on that showed SEO data in the browser. The name comes from SEM, short for search engine marketing. In December 2020 the company changed its styling from "SEMrush" to "Semrush."

Semrush raised $40 million in 2018 in a round co-led by Greycroft, E.ventures and Siguler Guff. It went public on the New York Stock Exchange in March 2021 under the ticker SEMR. It then bought other marketing companies, including Backlinko in 2022 and Exploding Topics and Third Door Media in 2024.

AI search came next. Semrush launched its AI Toolkit in March 2025 to track brands in ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity. In October 2025 it launched Semrush One, which joins the SEO Toolkit and the AI Visibility Toolkit in one plan. Adobe agreed to buy Semrush in November 2025 for $1.9 billion and closed the deal on April 28, 2026. Semrush now runs as part of Adobe.

Semrush has the bigger data set. It lists 28.8 billion keywords, 808 million domains and 43 trillion backlinks. Its own backlink crawler reads about 10 billion pages a day.

Site Explorer shows any domain's organic and paid keywords, traffic, backlinks and competitors. It also shows Domain Rating, Domain Authority, Trust Flow and Citation Flow next to its own Domain Power score. Backlink Analyzer covers referring domains, anchors, new and lost links and spam signals. The pages we checked don't list the size of its backlink index.

## Search Atlas vs Semrush FAQ

### Is Search Atlas better than Semrush?

It depends on the job. Search Atlas is stronger when you want an agent to make SEO changes and want more tools on one plan. Semrush is stronger for research, with a larger keyword and backlink database.

### Is Search Atlas cheaper than Semrush?

Search Atlas starts lower, with Starter at $99 a month against Semrush's $139 SEO plan. At $199, both vendors' second plans add more AI tracking. Search Atlas Pro is $399, and Semrush Pro+ is $299.

### What is OTTO SEO?

OTTO SEO is Search Atlas's agent that deploys SEO fixes to your live site. It handles items such as titles, meta descriptions, schema and internal links. It connects through a pixel, your CMS, Cloudflare or your code pipeline. Search Atlas introduced it in 2023.

### Does Search Atlas track ChatGPT?

Yes. ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode tracking starts on the Growth plan. Pro and Agency add Copilot, Perplexity and Claude. Starter covers 1 AI.

### Does Search Atlas have a free plan?

The pricing page lists no free plan. It offers a 7-day trial with a free onboarding call. Semrush has both a free account and a 7-day trial.

### Who owns Search Atlas?

Manick Bhan founded Search Atlas and runs it as CEO and CTO. It comes from the team behind LinkGraph and Signal Genesys, and it is based in New York City.

## Summary: Search Atlas vs Semrush

Search Atlas suits teams that want SEO work done for them. OTTO SEO pushes fixes live, and more tools come on the same plan with several seats included.

Semrush suits teams that want the deepest research data and daily rank tracking, and that make site changes themselves. Both have a 7-day trial, so run each on the same site and compare what you get done in a week.
