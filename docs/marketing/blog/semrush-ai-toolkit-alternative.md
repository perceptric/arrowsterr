## How to switch from the Semrush AI Toolkit

Switching tools is easier with your history in hand. Export these before you cancel.

1. Your tracked prompts. Copy the full list with any tags or topics. You'll paste it into the new tool on day 1.
2. Your AI visibility history. Export the visibility and position numbers by month. This is your baseline, since the new tool starts its own history from the day you add prompts.
3. Competitor lists. Save the rivals you track for each brand.
4. Position tracking keywords. If you're leaving Semrush for SEO too, export each project's keyword list with current ranks.
5. Keyword research lists. Save any keyword lists and content ideas you built.
6. Site audit results. Keep the last audit as a PDF so you can compare after the switch.
7. Report templates. Screenshot or export the reports clients or bosses expect, so you can rebuild them.

Then run both tools side by side for 2 to 4 weeks and compare visibility on the same prompts. AI answers vary between runs, so expect the numbers to differ a little. Look for the same trend.

## Semrush AI Toolkit alternatives FAQ

### Is the Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit worth it?

It's worth it for 1 domain and up to 25 prompts, especially if you already pay for Semrush SEO. Past that, each extra domain, prompt pack and user is a paid add-on.

### Does the Semrush AI Toolkit need a Semrush SEO plan?

The toolkit is sold on its own. Keyword research, audits and position tracking need a separate SEO plan from $139/mo.

### What is the cheapest Semrush AI Toolkit alternative?

Arrowsterr's free plan tracks 10 prompts weekly on ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews. Otterly.ai Lite costs $29/mo for 15 prompts. Arrowsterr Starter costs $39/mo for 50 prompts on 6 AIs.

### Can one tool replace Semrush for SEO and AI?

Arrowsterr and SE Ranking both cover SEO research and AI tracking in one plan. Semrush still has deeper backlink data, so check that part if links are central to your work.

### How many prompts should I track?

Most brands need 30 to 50 prompts per product or category. Teams with 3 brands usually land between 100 and 150.

### Will my AI visibility numbers match after I switch?

Close, but rarely exact. Each tool runs its own checks, and AI answers change from run to run. Compare trends over 2 to 4 weeks.
