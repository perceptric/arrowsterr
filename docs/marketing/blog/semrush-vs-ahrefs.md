# Semrush vs Ahrefs: Features, Data and Pricing Compared in 2026

Semrush and Ahrefs are the two biggest all-in-one SEO suites. Both do keyword research, backlink analysis, rank tracking and site audits. Both now track how AI answers mention your brand. The real gaps are in how often rankings update and how each vendor sells AI tracking.

Pick Semrush if you want daily rank tracking, a 7-day trial and AI search tracking bundled into one SEO plan. Pick Ahrefs if backlink research is your main job, you want a free account for your own site, or you want a cheap $29 plan for quick keyword and backlink checks.

## Semrush vs Ahrefs: main points

- **Rank tracking speed:** Semrush tracks keywords daily on every plan. Ahrefs lists weekly updates for tracked keywords on every plan, Enterprise included.
- **Entry price:** Ahrefs Starter is $29 a month and Lite is $129. Semrush's SEO plan is $139 a month, and its free account allows 10 reports a day.
- **Trials:** Semrush offers a 7-day free trial. Ahrefs offers no trial and says it never runs discounts, but its free account gives limited access to your own verified sites.
- **AI search tracking:** Semrush One bundles SEO and AI tracking from $199 a month with 50 prompts tracked daily. Ahrefs plans include a small daily prompt quota, and Brand Radar's full AI index starts from $199 a month.
- **Backlink data:** Both run their own link crawlers. Semrush lists 43 trillion backlinks. Ahrefs lists 35 trillion in its external backlink history and says its live index refreshes every 15 to 30 minutes.
- **Seats:** Every Ahrefs plan includes 1 user, and extra users cost $40 to $80 a month each. Semrush extra users start at $45 a month.

## What is Semrush?

Semrush is a marketing suite built around SEO. It sells toolkits for SEO, AI visibility, traffic and market research, local, content, social and ads. Its SEO toolkit covers keyword research, competitor analysis, Position Tracking and Site Audit. Semrush One is its bundle of the SEO and AI Visibility toolkits.

## What is Ahrefs?

Ahrefs is an SEO suite best known for its backlink index. Its core tools are Site Explorer, Keywords Explorer, Site Audit, Rank Tracker and Brand Radar for AI search. Ahrefs runs its own crawler and lists 494 billion pages in its index. It sells core plans from Lite to Enterprise, plus a $29 Starter plan and a free account.

## Semrush vs Ahrefs keyword research

Both tools have huge keyword databases. Semrush lists 28.8 billion keywords. Ahrefs lists 29 billion filtered keywords across 217 locations. At this size, the database total won't decide it for you.

The difference is in limits and extras. On Ahrefs Lite, each user gets 1,000 credits a month and reports show up to 2,500 rows. Standard and above give unlimited credits. Ahrefs puts keyword clusters, search intents and AI keyword suggestions on Standard ($249 a month) and up.

Semrush's SEO plan includes basic keyword research. The full keyword research and optimization tools come with Semrush One Starter, and Pro+ adds keyword cannibalization analysis and content optimization.

**Stronger:** About even. Ahrefs is easier to judge by its plan table. Semrush gives more keyword tools on its bundles.

## Semrush vs Ahrefs backlink data

Backlinks are where Ahrefs built its name. It says its live backlink index updates every 15 to 30 minutes and that it crawls 5 million pages every minute. It lists 35 trillion records in its external backlink history. Domain Rating and URL Rating come from this index.

Semrush also runs its own backlink crawler. It says the crawler reads about 10 billion pages a day and lists 43 trillion backlinks and 808 million domains.

Each vendor counts links its own way, so the totals don't compare one to one. Test both on a site you know well.

**Stronger:** Ahrefs, for most link builders. Its link tools sit at the center of the product, and Standard adds broken backlinks, linking authors and more Site Explorer reports.

## Semrush vs Ahrefs rank tracking and site audit

This is the clearest gap between the two.

| | Semrush | Ahrefs |
|---|---|---|
| Update frequency | Daily | Weekly |
| Entry plan keywords | 500 (SEO plan) | 50 (Starter), 750 (Lite) |
| Mid plan keywords | 1,500 (Pro+) | 2,000 (Standard) |
| Top listed plan | 5,000+ (Advanced) | 5,000 (Advanced) |
| Site audit | Included on all paid plans | 100,000 to 1.5M crawl credits a month |

Ahrefs Lite tracks more keywords than Semrush's SEO plan, but it updates them weekly. Semrush tracks fewer keywords on its entry plan, but it checks them daily.

For site audits, Ahrefs gives clear crawl limits. Lite allows up to 25,000 pages per project and Advanced up to 250,000. Ahrefs Free includes Site Audit for verified sites with 5,000 crawl credits a month per project. Always-on audits cost extra through Project Boost, from $20 a month per project.

Semrush includes Site Audit on its SEO plan and an AI-ready Site Audit on Semrush One. The prices page doesn't list a page limit per plan (check).

**Stronger:** Semrush for rank tracking, because daily data catches drops sooner. Ahrefs for audit limits you can read up front.

## Semrush vs Ahrefs AI visibility tracking

Both vendors now track how AI answers mention brands. They sell it in different ways.

**Semrush** has two routes:

- **AI Visibility Toolkit:** $99 a month on its own. It includes 25 prompts, 1 domain for brand performance and 1 user. Its help page lists ChatGPT and Google AI Mode for prompt tracking. It has no free trial. Extra prompts cost $60 a month per 50, and extra domains and users cost $99 a month each.
- **Semrush One:** Starter tracks 50 prompts daily, and Pro+ tracks 100. Advanced lists unlimited prompts.

**Ahrefs** also has two routes:

- **Prompts in core plans:** Lite includes 150 checks a month, about 5 prompts daily on 1 platform. Standard gives 10 prompts and Advanced 20. Claude uses 8 checks per update.
- **Brand Radar:** From $199 a month. Its AI Visibility Index covers 525 million+ prompts across AI Overviews, AI Mode, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Copilot. You can look up any brand with no setup. Custom prompt packages start at $50 a month for 2,500 checks.

Ahrefs' index works best for brands that already have search demand. Ahrefs says coverage may be limited for brands with little or no search volume.

**Stronger:** Semrush for tracking your own prompt list inside an SEO plan. Ahrefs for researching any brand or market in AI answers without writing prompts first.

## Semrush vs Ahrefs traffic and content tools

Ahrefs sells Traffic Analytics as a $99 a month add-on. It shows estimated traffic for any domain with a channel breakdown that includes AI traffic. Its Content Kit is listed from $99 a month and marked as coming soon. Ahrefs Free includes AI Content Helper with 1 free doc a month.

Semrush sells traffic and market data in a separate Traffic & Market toolkit (price: check). Semrush says that data comes from a panel of over 200 million anonymized users. Its content tools generate articles from a brief and optimize existing pages. Content optimization is listed on Semrush One Pro+.

**Stronger:** Semrush for content and market research in one vendor. Ahrefs if you only need traffic estimates as an add-on.

## Semrush vs Ahrefs FAQ

### Is Semrush or Ahrefs better for beginners?

Semrush is easier to start with because it has a 7-day trial and a free account with 10 reports a day. Ahrefs has no trial. Ahrefs Free and the $29 Starter plan are also low-risk ways to learn its tools.

### Is Ahrefs cheaper than Semrush?

Ahrefs has the lower entry price, with Starter at $29 a month and Lite at $129. Semrush's SEO plan is $139 a month. At the top listed plans, Ahrefs Advanced is $449 a month, and Semrush One Advanced is priced on request.

### Which has better backlink data, Semrush or Ahrefs?

Both run their own crawlers and list tens of trillions of links. Ahrefs puts backlinks at the center of its product and refreshes its live index every 15 to 30 minutes. Most link builders lean toward Ahrefs, but test both on your own site.

### Does Ahrefs track rankings daily?

Ahrefs' pricing page lists weekly updates for tracked keywords on all plans, Enterprise included. Semrush tracks rankings daily on all paid plans.

### Do Semrush and Ahrefs track ChatGPT?

Yes. Semrush tracks prompts in ChatGPT and Google AI Mode through its AI Visibility Toolkit and Semrush One. Ahrefs Brand Radar covers ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot, AI Overviews and AI Mode, and custom prompts add Claude.

## Summary: Semrush vs Ahrefs

Semrush is the better fit when you want daily rankings, a trial and AI prompt tracking bundled with your SEO plan. Ahrefs is the better fit when backlinks drive your work, or when you want a free account or a $29 plan to start.

Both are strong SEO suites, and both now sell AI search tracking. Start Semrush's trial and open Ahrefs Free on your own site. Then compare the reports you use most.
