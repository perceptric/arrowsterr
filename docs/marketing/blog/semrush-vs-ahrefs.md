# Semrush vs Ahrefs: Features, Data and Pricing Compared in 2026

Semrush and Ahrefs are the two biggest all-in-one SEO suites. Both do keyword research, backlink analysis, rank tracking and site audits. Both now track how AI answers mention your brand. The real gaps are in how often rankings update and how each vendor sells AI tracking.

Pick Semrush if you want daily rank tracking and AI search tracking bundled into one SEO plan, with a 7-day trial first. Pick Ahrefs if backlink research is your main job, or if you want to start on a free account or a $29 plan for quick keyword and backlink checks.

## Semrush vs Ahrefs: main points

- **Rank tracking speed:** Semrush tracks keywords daily on every plan. Ahrefs lists weekly updates for tracked keywords on every plan, Enterprise included.
- **Entry price:** Ahrefs Starter is $29 a month and Lite is $129. Semrush's SEO plan is $139 a month, and its free account allows 10 reports a day.
- **Trials:** Semrush offers a 7-day free trial. Ahrefs offers no trial and says it never runs discounts, but its free account gives limited access to your own verified sites.
- **AI search tracking:** Semrush One bundles SEO and AI tracking from $199 a month with 50 prompts tracked daily. Ahrefs plans include a small daily prompt quota, and Brand Radar's full AI index starts from $199 a month.
- **Backlink data:** Both run their own link crawlers. Semrush lists 43 trillion backlinks. Ahrefs lists 35 trillion in its external backlink history and says its live index refreshes every 15 to 30 minutes.
- **Seats:** Every Ahrefs plan includes 1 user, and extra users cost $40 to $80 a month each. Semrush extra users start at $45 a month.

### Semrush today

[Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/) is a marketing suite built around SEO. It sells toolkits for SEO, AI visibility, traffic and market research, local, content, social and ads. Its SEO toolkit covers keyword research, competitor analysis, Position Tracking and Site Audit. Semrush One is its bundle of the SEO and AI Visibility toolkits.

Semrush says over 28 million people use its platform, from startups to large enterprises. Since April 2026, Semrush has been part of Adobe.

### Semrush history

Oleg Shchegolev and Dmitri Melnikov founded Semrush in 2008. The product grew out of an early SEO tool called Seodigger, which later became a Firefox extension. The "SEM" in the name stands for search engine marketing. In December 2020 the company changed how it wrote its name, from "SEMrush" to "Semrush".

Semrush went public on the New York Stock Exchange in March 2021 under the ticker SEMR. It then bought its way into new areas. In 2022 it bought Backlinko and Kompyte. Traffic Think Tank followed in 2023. In 2024 it added Third Door Media, Brand24, Ryte, Datos and Exploding Topics.

The AI shift came next. On October 29, 2025, Semrush launched Semrush One, one plan for SEO and AI search visibility. In November 2025, Adobe agreed to buy Semrush for about $1.9 billion. The deal closed on April 28, 2026. Semrush now runs as "Semrush, an Adobe company", and its product continues with no immediate changes for customers.

### Ahrefs today

[Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/) is an SEO suite best known for its backlink index. Its core tools are Site Explorer, Keywords Explorer, Site Audit, Rank Tracker and Brand Radar for AI search. Ahrefs runs its own crawler, AhrefsBot, and lists 494 billion pages in its index. It sells core plans from Lite to Enterprise, plus a $29 Starter plan and a free account.

Most of its users are SEOs and link builders who spend their day in backlink data.

### Ahrefs history

Dmytro Gerasimenko founded Ahrefs in 2010. The company is based in Singapore. From the start it built its own web crawler, and that link index still powers the product today.

Ahrefs grew without outside money. It says it has stayed profitable without a dollar of outside funding. By June 2022, TechCrunch reported about $100 million in yearly revenue from more than 50,000 customers. That same year Ahrefs put about $60 million into Yep, its own search engine. Yep runs on its own index and says it shares 90% of ad revenue with content creators.

AI search work picked up in 2025. Brand Radar, its AI visibility tool, was sold on its own by November 2025. In December 2025, Brand Radar added beta indexes for YouTube and TikTok videos. It also began tracking Reddit results in Google. Custom AI prompt tracking followed in January 2026. In early 2026 it also launched Bot Analytics, which shows how AI crawlers such as GPTBot visit your site.

**Stronger:** Semrush for rank tracking, because daily data catches drops sooner. Ahrefs for audit limits you can read up front.

## Which should you choose: Semrush or Ahrefs?

**Pick Semrush if:**

- You need daily rank tracking.
- You want SEO and AI prompt tracking in one plan.
- You want a 7-day trial before you pay.
- Your team also runs content and local SEO from the same account.

**Pick Ahrefs if:**

- Backlink research and link building are your main jobs.
- You want a free account for your own verified sites.
- You need a $29 plan for quick keyword and backlink checks.
- You want to research any brand's AI visibility without setting up prompts.

## Semrush vs Ahrefs FAQ

### Is Semrush or Ahrefs better for beginners?

Semrush is easier to start with because it has a 7-day trial and a free account with 10 reports a day. Ahrefs has no trial. Ahrefs Free and the $29 Starter plan are also low-risk ways to learn its tools.

### Is Ahrefs cheaper than Semrush?

Ahrefs has the lower entry price, with Starter at $29 a month and Lite at $129. Semrush's SEO plan is $139 a month. At the top listed plans, Ahrefs Advanced is $449 a month, and Semrush One Advanced is priced on request.

### Which has better backlink data, Semrush or Ahrefs?

Both run their own crawlers and list tens of trillions of links. Ahrefs puts backlinks at the center of its product and refreshes its live index every 15 to 30 minutes. Most link builders lean toward Ahrefs, but test both on your own site.

### Does Ahrefs track rankings daily?

Ahrefs' pricing page lists weekly updates for tracked keywords on all plans, Enterprise included. Semrush tracks rankings daily on all paid plans.

### Do Semrush and Ahrefs track ChatGPT?

Yes. Semrush tracks prompts in ChatGPT and Google AI Mode through its AI Visibility Toolkit and Semrush One. Ahrefs Brand Radar covers ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot, AI Overviews and AI Mode, and custom prompts add Claude.

### Who owns Semrush and Ahrefs?

Adobe owns Semrush. It closed the deal on April 28, 2026. Ahrefs is privately owned and has never taken outside funding.

## Summary: Semrush vs Ahrefs

Semrush is the better fit when you want daily rankings and AI prompt tracking bundled with your SEO plan, with a trial to test it first. Ahrefs is the better fit when backlinks drive your work, or when you want a free account or a $29 plan to start.

Both are strong SEO suites, and both now sell AI search tracking. Start Semrush's trial and open Ahrefs Free on your own site. Then compare the reports you use most.
