# Semrush vs Ahrefs: Features, Data and Pricing Compared in 2026

Semrush checks your tracked keywords every day. Ahrefs checks them once a week, even on Enterprise. That gap matters more than the size of their databases, and so does the way each one sells AI search tracking.

They are the two biggest all in one SEO suites. Each covers keyword research, backlink analysis, rank tracking and site audits, and both now track how AI answers mention your brand. Go with Semrush if you want daily rank tracking and AI search tracking on one SEO plan, with a 7 day trial first. Go with Ahrefs if backlink research is your main job, or if you want to start on a free account or a $29 plan for quick keyword and backlink checks. (If AI tracking is why you're here, the AI visibility section below has the prompt counts.)

## Semrush vs Ahrefs: main points

- Semrush tracks keywords daily on every plan. Ahrefs updates tracked keywords weekly on every plan, Enterprise included.
- Ahrefs costs less to start. Starter is $29 a month and Lite is $129. Semrush's SEO plan is $139 a month, and its free account allows 10 reports a day.
- Semrush has a 7 day free trial. Ahrefs has no trial and never runs discounts, but its free account gives limited access to your own verified sites.
- For AI search, Semrush One bundles SEO and AI tracking from $199 a month with 50 prompts tracked daily. Ahrefs plans include a small daily prompt quota, and Brand Radar's full AI index starts from $199 a month.
- Each runs its own link crawler. Semrush counts 43 trillion backlinks. Ahrefs holds 35 trillion in its external backlink history, and its live index refreshes every 15 to 30 minutes.
- Every Ahrefs plan includes 1 user, and extra users cost $40 to $80 a month each. Semrush extra users start at $45 a month.

### Semrush today

[Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/) is a marketing suite built around SEO. It sells toolkits for SEO, AI visibility, traffic and market research, local, content, social and ads. Its SEO toolkit covers keyword research, competitor analysis, Position Tracking and Site Audit. Semrush One bundles the SEO and AI Visibility toolkits.

Over 28 million people use Semrush, from startups to large enterprises. It has been part of Adobe since April 2026.

### Semrush history

Oleg Shchegolev and Dmitri Melnikov founded Semrush in 2008. It grew out of Seodigger, an early SEO tool that later became a Firefox extension. The "SEM" in the name stands for search engine marketing. In December 2020 the company changed the spelling from "SEMrush" to "Semrush".

Semrush went public on the New York Stock Exchange in March 2021 under the ticker SEMR, then bought its way into new areas. Backlinko and Kompyte came in 2022, and Traffic Think Tank in 2023. The 2024 buys were Third Door Media, Brand24, Ryte, Datos and Exploding Topics.

Then came AI search. On October 29, 2025, Semrush launched Semrush One, one plan for SEO and AI search visibility. In November 2025, Adobe agreed to buy Semrush for about $1.9 billion, and the deal closed on April 28, 2026. Semrush now runs as "Semrush, an Adobe company", and the product continues with no immediate changes for customers.

### Ahrefs today

[Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/) is an SEO suite best known for its backlink index. Its core tools are Site Explorer, Keywords Explorer, Site Audit, Rank Tracker and Brand Radar for AI search. Ahrefs runs its own crawler, AhrefsBot, and holds 494 billion pages in its index. Plans start with a free account and a $29 Starter plan, then run from Lite to Enterprise.

Most of its users are SEOs and link builders who spend their day in backlink data.

### Ahrefs history

Dmytro Gerasimenko founded Ahrefs in 2010, and the company is based in Singapore. From the start it built its own web crawler. That link index still powers the product today.

Ahrefs has never taken a dollar of outside funding, and it has stayed profitable. By June 2022 it made about $100 million a year from more than 50,000 customers. That same year it put about $60 million into Yep, its own search engine. Yep runs on its own index and shares 90% of ad revenue with content creators.

AI search work picked up in 2025. Brand Radar, its AI visibility tool, was sold on its own by November 2025. In December 2025 Brand Radar added beta indexes for YouTube and TikTok videos and began tracking Reddit results in Google. Custom AI prompt tracking followed in January 2026. Bot Analytics came in early 2026, and it shows how AI crawlers such as GPTBot visit your site.

## Semrush vs Ahrefs rank tracking and site audit

Rank tracking is the biggest gap between the two.

| Feature | Semrush | Ahrefs |
|---|---|---|
| Update frequency | Daily | Weekly |
| Entry plan keywords | 500 (SEO plan) | 50 (Starter), 750 (Lite) |
| Mid plan keywords | 1,500 (Pro+) | 2,000 (Standard) |

| Site audit | Included on all paid plans | 100,000 to 1.5M crawl credits a month |

Ahrefs Lite tracks 750 keywords, more than the 500 on Semrush's SEO plan. It updates them once a week. Semrush checks all 500 every day.

Ahrefs caps audits with crawl credits, from 100,000 a month on Lite to 1.5 million on Advanced. Project Boost adds always on audits, from $20 a month per project. Semrush includes Site Audit on its SEO plan and an AI ready Site Audit on Semrush One. To see if your robots.txt blocks AI bots, run the free [AI crawler checker](https://arrowsterr.com/free-tools/ai-crawler-checker/).

**Stronger:** Semrush for rank tracking. Daily data catches drops sooner. Ahrefs for audits, with crawl limits you can read up front.

## Which should you choose: Semrush or Ahrefs?

**Pick Semrush if:**

- You need daily rank tracking.
- You want SEO and AI prompt tracking in one plan.
- You want a 7 day trial before you pay.
- Your team also runs content and local SEO from the same account.

**Pick Ahrefs if:**

- Backlink research and link building are your main jobs.
- You want a free account for your own verified sites.
- You need a $29 plan for quick keyword and backlink checks.
- You want to research any brand's AI visibility without setting up prompts.

## Semrush vs Ahrefs FAQ

### Is Semrush or Ahrefs better for beginners?

Semrush is easier to start with. You get a 7 day trial and a free account with 10 reports a day. Ahrefs has no trial, though Ahrefs Free and the $29 Starter plan let you learn its tools for little or no money.

### Is Ahrefs cheaper than Semrush?

Ahrefs has the lower entry price, with Starter at $29 a month and Lite at $129. Semrush's SEO plan is $139 a month. At the top plans, Ahrefs Advanced is $449 a month, and Semrush One Advanced is priced on request.

### Which has better backlink data, Semrush or Ahrefs?

Both run their own crawlers and count tens of trillions of links. Ahrefs puts backlinks at the center of its product and refreshes its live index every 15 to 30 minutes. Most link builders lean toward Ahrefs, but test both on your own site.

### Does Ahrefs track rankings daily?

No. Ahrefs updates tracked keywords weekly on all plans, Enterprise included. Semrush tracks rankings daily on all paid plans.

### Do Semrush and Ahrefs track ChatGPT?

Yes. Semrush tracks prompts in ChatGPT and Google AI Mode through its AI Visibility Toolkit and Semrush One. Ahrefs Brand Radar covers ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot, AI Overviews and AI Mode, and custom prompts add Claude.

### Who owns Semrush and Ahrefs?

Adobe owns Semrush. It closed the deal on April 28, 2026. Ahrefs is privately owned and has never taken outside funding.

## Summary: Semrush vs Ahrefs

Semrush wins on daily rankings and on AI prompt tracking inside your SEO plan. For backlinks, or a cheap way in through a free account or the $29 plan, Ahrefs is the better start.

Open Ahrefs Free on your own site, then start Semrush's 7 day trial on the same domain. Compare the reports you use most.
