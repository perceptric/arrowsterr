# Ahrefs vs Semrush vs Moz: Three SEO Suites Compared in 2026

Pick Ahrefs if backlink research is your main job. Pick Semrush if you want daily rank tracking and the widest set of marketing tools. Pick Moz if you want the lowest entry price for a full suite, with AI prompt tracking in every plan.

Ahrefs, Semrush and Moz Pro are the best known SEO suites. Each one covers keyword research, backlinks, rank tracking and site audits, and each now tracks how AI answers mention your brand. They split on how fresh the data is and on how they sell AI tracking.

## Ahrefs vs Semrush vs Moz: main points

- Ahrefs Starter is $29 a month and Lite is $129. Moz Starter is $49 and Standard is $99. Semrush's SEO plan is $139.
- Semrush updates rankings daily. Ahrefs updates them weekly on all plans. How often Moz campaigns update isn't public.
- Moz includes 150 to 600 AI prompts in every Moz Pro plan, checked weekly. Semrush bundles 50 or 100 daily prompts in Semrush One. Ahrefs includes 5 to 20 daily prompts on core plans and sells Brand Radar from $199 a month.
- Ahrefs and Semrush each hold about 29 billion keywords. Moz has a 1.25 billion+ keyword index. All three count tens of trillions of backlinks.
- Semrush has a 7 day trial and a free account. Ahrefs has no trial, but Ahrefs Free covers your own verified sites. Moz has a free community account and a free trial on its Medium plan. The trial length isn't public.

### Ahrefs at a glance

[Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/) is an SEO suite best known for its backlink index. Its core tools are Site Explorer, Keywords Explorer, Site Audit, Rank Tracker and Brand Radar for AI search. Ahrefs runs its own crawler and holds 494 billion pages in its index. You can start on a free account or a $29 Starter plan, and core plans run from Lite to Enterprise. It suits link builders and SEOs who work in backlink data most days.

### Where Ahrefs came from

Dmytro Gerasimenko started Ahrefs in 2010, with its headquarters in Singapore. It built its own web crawler, AhrefsBot, early on, and that crawl data has powered its tools ever since.

The company is bootstrapped and has stayed profitable without outside funding. In June 2022 its revenue was about $100 million a year, from more than 50,000 customers. In the same year Ahrefs spent about $60 million building Yep, a search engine with its own index that gives creators 90% of its ad revenue.

Since 2025, Ahrefs has put more of its work into AI search. Brand Radar, its AI visibility tool, was on sale as a standalone product by November 2025. In December 2025 it gained beta indexes for YouTube and TikTok, along with Reddit results in Google. Custom prompt tracking arrived in January 2026, and Bot Analytics for AI crawler visits launched in early 2026.

### Semrush at a glance

[Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/) is a marketing suite built around SEO. It sells toolkits for SEO, AI visibility, traffic and market research, local, content, social and ads. Semrush One bundles the SEO and AI Visibility toolkits on one plan. Over 28 million people use it, from startups to large enterprises.

### Where Semrush came from

Oleg Shchegolev and Dmitri Melnikov started Semrush in 2008. The first product, Seodigger, later became a Firefox extension. "SEM" stands for search engine marketing. The brand moved from "SEMrush" to "Semrush" in December 2020.

Semrush went public on the New York Stock Exchange in March 2021, ticker SEMR. It used the next years to buy content and data brands. It picked up Backlinko and Kompyte in 2022 and Traffic Think Tank in 2023. Its 2024 deals were Third Door Media, Brand24, Ryte, Datos and Exploding Topics.

On October 29, 2025, it launched Semrush One to combine SEO and AI visibility in a single plan. Adobe agreed in November 2025 to buy Semrush for about $1.9 billion and closed the deal on April 28, 2026. Semrush now runs as "Semrush, an Adobe company".

### Moz at a glance

[Moz](https://moz.com/) is a veteran SEO software company and part of Ziff Davis. Moz Pro is its SEO suite, with Keyword Explorer, Link Explorer, Site Crawl, rank tracking and AI visibility dashboards. Moz created Domain Authority, a score many SEOs use to judge sites. It also sells Moz Local, STAT and AI Explorer as separate products.

### Where Moz came from

Moz is the oldest of the three. Rand Fishkin and Gillian Muessig founded it in 2004 under the name SEOmoz. It began as a blog and community for early SEO experts, and it published the Beginner's Guide to SEO and a Search Ranking Factors study. A small consulting firm grew out of that work and led to the first Moz tools.

The company took its first funding in 2007 and reached 5,000 subscribers by 2009. In 2012 Foundry Group led an $18 million Series B. Moz also bought Followerwonk and GetListed, and GetListed grew into Moz Local.

SEOmoz became Moz in 2013. After a few years of broader marketing tools, it went back to search in 2016. It bought STAT Search Analytics in 2018 to add daily rank and SERP data. iContact, part of Ziff Davis, bought Moz in 2021. Today AI Visibility Dashboards come with every Moz Pro plan, and AI Explorer is sold on its own.

## Which should you choose: Ahrefs, Semrush or Moz?

**Pick Ahrefs if:**

- Backlinks and link building are your main jobs.
- You want a free account for your own sites.
- You want a $29 plan for quick checks.
- You want to research any brand's AI visibility without writing prompts.

**Pick Semrush if:**

- You need daily rank tracking and daily AI prompt checks.
- You want content, local and ads tools from one vendor.
- You want a 7 day trial before you pay.

**Pick Moz if:**

- You want the lowest entry price for a full SEO suite.
- You want AI prompt tracking in your base plan, and weekly data is enough for the sites you manage.

## Ahrefs vs Semrush vs Moz FAQ

### Which is best for beginners: Ahrefs, Semrush or Moz?

Moz has the cheapest full plan and fixed monthly limits, which helps when you're starting out. Semrush's 7 day trial and free account make it easy to test too. Ahrefs has no trial, but Ahrefs Free lets you learn its tools on your own site.

### Which is cheapest: Ahrefs, Semrush or Moz?

Ahrefs Starter is the cheapest paid plan at $29 a month, with tight limits. Moz Standard, at $99 a month, is the cheapest full plan of the three. Semrush starts at $139 a month for its SEO plan.

### Which has the best backlink data?

Ahrefs is the usual pick for link builders because backlinks are the core of its product. Semrush has the largest backlink count, at 43 trillion. Moz's Domain Authority is still the best known authority score. Test each on a site you know well.

### Do Ahrefs, Semrush and Moz track ChatGPT?

Yes, all three track ChatGPT. Ahrefs Brand Radar also covers Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot, AI Overviews and AI Mode. Moz adds Gemini and Google AI Mode. Semrush tracks prompts in ChatGPT and Google AI Mode.

### Which updates rankings daily?

Semrush, on its paid plans. Ahrefs updates tracked keywords weekly on every plan. How often Moz campaigns update rankings isn't public.

### Which is the oldest: Ahrefs, Semrush or Moz?

Moz is the oldest. It started as SEOmoz in 2004. Semrush followed in 2008 and Ahrefs in 2010. Adobe now owns Semrush, Ziff Davis owns Moz, and Ahrefs is still independent.

## Summary: Ahrefs vs Semrush vs Moz

Your main job and how fresh your data needs to be decide this. If you need daily rankings, pick Semrush. If weekly is enough, Moz and Ahrefs both stay on the list.

Start free. Open Ahrefs Free on your verified site, start Semrush's 7 day trial and sign up for a Moz Community account. Run the same site through each and keep the one whose reports you use every week.
