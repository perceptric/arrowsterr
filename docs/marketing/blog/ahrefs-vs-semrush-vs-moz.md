# Ahrefs vs Semrush vs Moz: Three SEO Suites Compared in 2026

Ahrefs, Semrush and Moz Pro are the best-known SEO suites. All three do keyword research, backlinks, rank tracking and site audits. All three now track how AI answers mention your brand. They differ most on update speed and on how each one sells AI tracking.

Pick Ahrefs if backlink research is your main job. Pick Semrush if you want daily rank tracking and the widest set of marketing tools. Pick Moz if you want the lowest entry price for a full suite, with AI prompt tracking included in every plan.

## Ahrefs vs Semrush vs Moz: main points

- **Entry prices:** Ahrefs Starter is $29 a month and Lite is $129. Moz Starter is $49 and Standard is $99. Semrush's SEO plan is $139.
- **Rank tracking speed:** Semrush updates rankings daily. Ahrefs lists weekly updates on all plans. Moz campaigns update weekly (check).
- **AI tracking:** Moz includes 150 to 600 AI prompts in every Moz Pro plan, checked weekly. Semrush bundles 50 or 100 daily prompts in Semrush One. Ahrefs includes 5 to 20 daily prompts on core plans and sells Brand Radar from $199 a month.
- **Data size:** Ahrefs and Semrush each list about 29 billion keywords. Moz lists a 1.25 billion+ keyword index. All three list tens of trillions of backlinks.
- **Trials and free plans:** Semrush has a 7-day trial and a free account. Ahrefs has no trial, but Ahrefs Free covers your own verified sites. Moz shows a free trial on its Medium plan (length: check) and a free community account.

## What is Ahrefs?

Ahrefs is an SEO suite best known for its backlink index. Its core tools are Site Explorer, Keywords Explorer, Site Audit, Rank Tracker and Brand Radar for AI search. Ahrefs runs its own crawler and lists 494 billion pages in its index. It sells a free account, a $29 Starter plan and core plans from Lite to Enterprise.

## What is Semrush?

Semrush is a marketing suite built around SEO. It sells toolkits for SEO, AI visibility, traffic and market research, local, content, social and ads. Semrush One bundles the SEO and AI Visibility toolkits on one plan.

## What is Moz?

Moz is a long-running SEO software company and part of Ziff Davis. Moz Pro is its SEO suite, with Keyword Explorer, Link Explorer, Site Crawl, rank tracking and AI visibility dashboards. Moz created Domain Authority, a score many SEOs use to judge sites. It also sells Moz Local, STAT and AI Explorer as separate products.

## Ahrefs vs Semrush vs Moz keyword research

| | Ahrefs | Semrush | Moz Pro |
|---|---|---|---|
| Keyword database | 29 billion filtered, 217 locations | 28.8 billion | 1.25 billion+ |
| Entry plan limit | 1,000 credits per user (Lite) | Basic keyword research (SEO plan) | 150 keyword queries a month (Standard) |
| Clusters and intent | Standard and up | Semrush One | Search intent from Moz AI |

Ahrefs and Semrush are close on database size. Moz's index is much smaller, which matters most for long-tail ideas and smaller countries. Moz Standard also caps keyword queries at 150 a month, so it suits light research. Medium raises that to 5,000.

On Ahrefs, Lite caps credits at 1,000 per user, and Standard and above are unlimited. Ahrefs puts keyword clusters and search intents on Standard. Semrush puts its full keyword tools on Semrush One, and Pro+ adds cannibalization analysis.

**Stronger:** Ahrefs and Semrush, about even. Moz is fine for small sites with a short keyword list.

## Ahrefs vs Semrush vs Moz backlink data

All three run link indexes, and each counts links its own way. Their totals don't compare one to one.

- **Ahrefs** lists 35 trillion records in its external backlink history. It says its live index updates every 15 to 30 minutes and that it crawls 5 million pages a minute. Domain Rating and URL Rating come from this index.
- **Semrush** lists 43 trillion backlinks and 808 million domains. It says its crawler reads about 10 billion pages a day. Its main metric is Authority Score.
- **Moz** Link Explorer lists over 40 trillion links. Its metrics are Domain Authority, Page Authority and Spam Score. Moz plans cap backlink queries at 5,000 to 70,000 a month.

**Stronger:** Ahrefs for most link builders, since link data is the center of its product. Moz has the best-known authority score. Semrush is a solid middle choice inside a broader toolkit.

Semrush is the only one of the three with daily rank updates on every plan. That matters when you need to spot a drop fast. Ahrefs tracks the most keywords on its Lite and Standard plans, but it updates them weekly.

Moz Standard crawls 100,000 pages a week. Semrush includes Site Audit on every paid plan and an AI-ready Site Audit on Semrush One.

**Stronger:** Semrush for rank tracking. Ahrefs for audit depth and its free audit on verified sites.

## Ahrefs vs Semrush vs Moz AI visibility tracking

Each vendor sells AI tracking in its own way.

| | Ahrefs | Semrush | Moz Pro |
|---|---|---|---|
| In base plans | 5 to 20 daily prompts (150 to 600 checks a month) | None on the SEO plan | 150 to 600 prompts |
| Bundle or add-on | Brand Radar from $199/mo | Semrush One from $199/mo, or AI Visibility Toolkit $99/mo | AI Explorer on its own from $45/mo |
| Prompt refresh | Daily | Daily | Weekly |
| AIs covered | AI Overviews, AI Mode, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot; Claude on custom prompts | ChatGPT and Google AI Mode for prompt tracking | ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Mode |
| Research without setup | AI Visibility Index, 525 million+ prompts | Prompt Research, 317 million AI prompts in its data | Prompt suggestions |

**Ahrefs** is strongest for research. Brand Radar lets you look up any brand across its prompt index with no setup. Ahrefs notes that coverage may be limited for brands with little or no search volume. Custom prompt packages start at $50 a month for 2,500 checks.

**Semrush** is strongest for daily tracking inside an SEO plan. Semrush One Starter tracks 50 prompts daily, and Pro+ tracks 100. The $99 toolkit includes 25 prompts and has no free trial.

**Moz** gives the most prompts on a base plan. Standard tracks 150 prompts weekly, with sentiment and citation data. Extra prompts cost $50 a month per 100.

**Stronger:** It depends on the job. Ahrefs leads for market research. Semrush leads for daily tracking. Moz gives the most prompts on a budget.

## Which should you choose: Ahrefs, Semrush or Moz?

**Pick Ahrefs if:**

- Backlinks and link building are your main jobs.
- You want a free account for your own sites, or a $29 plan for quick checks.
- You want to research any brand's AI visibility without writing prompts.

**Pick Semrush if:**

- You need daily rank tracking and daily AI prompt checks.
- You want content, local and ads tools from one vendor.
- You want a 7-day trial before you pay.

**Pick Moz if:**

- You want the lowest entry price for a full SEO suite.
- You want AI prompt tracking included in your base plan.
- Weekly data is enough for the sites you manage.

## Ahrefs vs Semrush vs Moz FAQ

### Which is best for beginners: Ahrefs, Semrush or Moz?

Moz has the lowest-priced full plan and clear monthly limits, which helps when you're starting out. Semrush's 7-day trial and free account also make it easy to test. Ahrefs has no trial, but Ahrefs Free lets you learn its tools on your own site.

### Which is cheapest: Ahrefs, Semrush or Moz?

Ahrefs Starter is the lowest-priced paid plan at $29 a month, with tight limits. Moz Standard at $99 a month is the lowest-priced full plan of the three. Semrush starts at $139 a month for its SEO plan.

### Which has the best backlink data?

Ahrefs is the usual pick for link builders because backlinks are the core of its product. Semrush lists the largest backlink total, at 43 trillion. Moz's Domain Authority remains the best-known authority score. Test each on a site you know well.

### Do Ahrefs, Semrush and Moz track ChatGPT?

Yes. All three track ChatGPT. Ahrefs Brand Radar also covers Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot, AI Overviews and AI Mode. Moz tracks Gemini and Google AI Mode too. Semrush lists ChatGPT and Google AI Mode for prompt tracking.

### Which updates rankings daily?

Semrush updates tracked keywords daily on its paid plans. Ahrefs lists weekly updates on every plan. Moz campaigns update weekly (check).

## Summary: Ahrefs vs Semrush vs Moz

Ahrefs is the pick for backlink work and AI market research. Semrush is the pick for daily data and the broadest toolkit. Moz is the pick for a lower-priced suite with AI prompts built into every plan.

Your choice depends on your main job and how fresh your data needs to be. Start with the free plans or trials and run your own site through each. Keep the one whose reports you use every week.
