# Ahrefs vs Semrush vs Moz: Three SEO Suites Compared in 2026

Ahrefs, Semrush and Moz Pro are the best-known SEO suites. All of them do keyword research, backlinks, rank tracking and site audits. Each one now tracks how AI answers mention your brand. They differ most on update speed and on how each one sells AI tracking.

Pick Ahrefs if backlink research is your main job. Pick Semrush if you want daily rank tracking and the widest set of marketing tools. Pick Moz if you want the lowest entry price for a full suite, with AI prompt tracking included in every plan.

## Ahrefs vs Semrush vs Moz: main points

- **Entry prices:** Ahrefs Starter is $29 a month and Lite is $129. Moz Starter is $49 and Standard is $99. Semrush's SEO plan is $139.
- **Rank tracking speed:** Semrush updates rankings daily. Ahrefs lists weekly updates on all plans. Moz doesn't list how often its campaigns update.
- **AI tracking:** Moz includes 150 to 600 AI prompts in every Moz Pro plan, checked weekly. Semrush bundles 50 or 100 daily prompts in Semrush One. Ahrefs includes 5 to 20 daily prompts on core plans and sells Brand Radar from $199 a month.
- **Data size:** Ahrefs and Semrush each list about 29 billion keywords. Moz lists a 1.25 billion+ keyword index. All of them list tens of trillions of backlinks.
- **Trials and free plans:** Semrush has a 7-day trial and a free account. Ahrefs has no trial, but Ahrefs Free covers your own verified sites. Moz shows a free trial on its Medium plan and a free community account. Moz doesn't list the trial length.

### Ahrefs at a glance

[Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/) is an SEO suite best known for its backlink index. Its core tools are Site Explorer, Keywords Explorer, Site Audit, Rank Tracker and Brand Radar for AI search. Ahrefs runs its own crawler and lists 494 billion pages in its index. It sells a free account, a $29 Starter plan and core plans from Lite to Enterprise. It suits link builders and SEOs who work in backlink data most days.

### Where Ahrefs came from

Ahrefs was started in 2010 by Dmytro Gerasimenko and has its headquarters in Singapore. It built its own web crawler, AhrefsBot, early on, and that crawl data has powered its tools ever since.

The company is proudly bootstrapped. It says it has stayed profitable without outside funding. In June 2022, TechCrunch put its revenue at about $100 million a year, from more than 50,000 customers. In the same year Ahrefs spent about $60 million building Yep, a search engine with its own index that promises creators 90% of its ad revenue.

Since 2025, Ahrefs has put more of its work into AI search. Brand Radar, its AI visibility tool, was on sale as a standalone product by November 2025. In December 2025 it gained beta indexes for YouTube and TikTok, along with Reddit results in Google. Custom prompt tracking arrived in January 2026, and Bot Analytics for AI crawler visits launched in early 2026.

### Semrush at a glance

[Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/) is a marketing suite built around SEO. It sells toolkits for SEO, AI visibility, traffic and market research, local, content, social and ads. Semrush One bundles the SEO and AI Visibility toolkits on one plan. Semrush says over 28 million people use it, from startups to large enterprises.

### Where Semrush came from

Oleg Shchegolev and Dmitri Melnikov started Semrush in 2008. The first product, Seodigger, later became a Firefox extension. "SEM" stands for search engine marketing. The brand moved from "SEMrush" to "Semrush" in December 2020.

Semrush went public on the New York Stock Exchange in March 2021, ticker SEMR. It used the next years to buy content and data brands. It picked up Backlinko and Kompyte in 2022 and Traffic Think Tank in 2023. Its 2024 deals were Third Door Media, Brand24, Ryte, Datos and Exploding Topics.

Its recent story is about AI search. On October 29, 2025, it launched Semrush One to combine SEO and AI visibility in a single plan. Adobe agreed in November 2025 to buy Semrush for about $1.9 billion and closed the deal on April 28, 2026. Semrush now trades as "Semrush, an Adobe company".

### Moz at a glance

[Moz](https://moz.com/) is a long-running SEO software company and part of Ziff Davis. Moz Pro is its SEO suite, with Keyword Explorer, Link Explorer, Site Crawl, rank tracking and AI visibility dashboards. Moz created Domain Authority, a score many SEOs use to judge sites. It also sells Moz Local, STAT and AI Explorer as separate products.

### Where Moz came from

Moz is the oldest of the three. Rand Fishkin and Gillian Muessig founded it in 2004 under the name SEOmoz. It began as a blog and community for early SEO experts, and it published the Beginner's Guide to SEO and a Search Ranking Factors study. A small consulting firm grew out of that work, and it led to the first Moz tools.

The company took its first funding in 2007 and reached 5,000 subscribers by 2009. In 2012 Foundry Group led an $18 million Series B. Moz also bought Followerwonk and GetListed, and GetListed grew into Moz Local.

SEOmoz became Moz in 2013. After a few years of broader marketing tools, it went back to a pure search focus in 2016. It bought STAT Search Analytics in 2018 to add daily rank and SERP data. iContact, part of Ziff Davis, bought Moz in 2021. Today AI Visibility Dashboards come with every Moz Pro plan, and AI Explorer is sold on its own.

## Which should you choose: Ahrefs, Semrush or Moz?

**Pick Ahrefs if:**

- Backlinks and link building are your main jobs.
- You want a free account for your own sites, or a $29 plan for quick checks.
- You want to research any brand's AI visibility without writing prompts.

**Pick Semrush if:**

- You need daily rank tracking and daily AI prompt checks.
- You want content, local and ads tools from one vendor.
- You want a 7-day trial before you pay.

**Pick Moz if:**

- You want the lowest entry price for a full SEO suite.
- You want AI prompt tracking included in your base plan.
- Weekly data is enough for the sites you manage.

## Ahrefs vs Semrush vs Moz FAQ

### Which is best for beginners: Ahrefs, Semrush or Moz?

Moz has the lowest-priced full plan and clear monthly limits, which helps when you're starting out. Semrush's 7-day trial and free account also make it easy to test. Ahrefs has no trial, but Ahrefs Free lets you learn its tools on your own site.

### Which is cheapest: Ahrefs, Semrush or Moz?

Ahrefs Starter is the lowest-priced paid plan at $29 a month, with tight limits. Moz Standard at $99 a month is the lowest-priced full plan of the three. Semrush starts at $139 a month for its SEO plan.

### Which has the best backlink data?

Ahrefs is the usual pick for link builders because backlinks are the core of its product. Semrush lists the largest backlink total, at 43 trillion. Moz's Domain Authority remains the best-known authority score. Test each on a site you know well.

### Do Ahrefs, Semrush and Moz track ChatGPT?

Yes. All of them track ChatGPT. Ahrefs Brand Radar also covers Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot, AI Overviews and AI Mode. Moz tracks Gemini and Google AI Mode too. Semrush lists ChatGPT and Google AI Mode for prompt tracking.

### Which updates rankings daily?

Semrush updates tracked keywords daily on its paid plans. Ahrefs lists weekly updates on every plan. Moz doesn't list how often its campaigns update rankings.

### Which is the oldest: Ahrefs, Semrush or Moz?

Moz is the oldest. It started as SEOmoz in 2004. Semrush followed in 2008 and Ahrefs in 2010. Adobe now owns Semrush, Ziff Davis owns Moz, and Ahrefs is still independent.

## Summary: Ahrefs vs Semrush vs Moz

Ahrefs is the pick for backlink work and AI market research. Semrush is the pick for daily data and the broadest toolkit. Moz is the pick for a lower-priced suite with AI prompts built into every plan.

Your choice depends on your main job and how fresh your data needs to be. Start with the free plans or trials and run your own site through each. Keep the one whose reports you use every week.
