## What agencies need from an AI visibility tool

A tool that works for one in-house brand can break at 10 clients. These are the points we weighed.

### Cost per client

Agency margins live or die on the cost of each client. A $95 plan looks cheap until you need it 10 times. We worked out the monthly cost for 10 clients with 40 prompts each. That's 400 prompts in total.

### White-label reports

Clients want a report with your logo on it. A PDF with the vendor's branding makes the client ask why they pay you. We checked which tools let you put your own brand on reports and which ones keep their own.

### Reports that send themselves

Building 10 client reports by hand each month eats a full day. A tool that emails a PDF on a schedule gives that day back. Share links help too, as long as you can lock them with a password and turn them off later.

### Brands, workspaces and seats

Each client needs its own brand or workspace. Your team needs seats. Some tools cap brands low and charge per extra workspace. Others give you unlimited brands on the agency plan.

### AI coverage

Clients ask about ChatGPT first. Google AI Overviews and AI Mode come next, then Gemini, Perplexity and Claude. A plan that covers 3 AIs may need add-ons before it matches what your client asks for.

## What a client-ready AI visibility report should show

A good monthly report answers 4 questions in plain words. Your client should get the answer to each one on a single page.

### Are we named, and how often?

Lead with visibility: the share of AI answers that name the brand. Show it next to last month's number. "Named in 34% of answers, up 6 points" tells the story in one line.

### Who gets named instead?

Clients care about rivals more than any metric. Show the top competitors and their visibility on the same prompts. A chart of visibility against average position helps a client see who leads.

### Which AI is the problem?

Break visibility down by AI. A brand can do well in ChatGPT and poorly in Google AI Overviews. That split tells you where to work next.

### What did we do, and what's next?

End with the work. List the pages you published or fixed and the prompts they target. Then list the next 3 to 5 moves. This is the page that keeps the retainer.

Two more details make a report look professional:

- **Sources.** Show which sites the AIs cite for your client's topics. Review sites and listicles often show up here, and they point to outreach work.
- **Your brand on every page.** Logo, colors and footer should be yours. The client should never see the tool vendor's name.

## How to roll out AI visibility tracking across your clients

1. **Pick 3 pilot clients.** Choose ones who already asked about AI search.
2. **Write 30 to 40 prompts per client.** Use the questions their buyers ask: "best X for Y", "X alternatives", "X vs Y".
3. **Run a 30-day baseline.** Daily checks for a month give you a stable starting number.
4. **Send the first report.** Show the baseline and 3 moves you'll make.
5. **Add the fix to your retainer.** Content, review sites and page updates are where the results come from.
6. **Roll out to the rest.** Once the pilot shows movement, add AI visibility to every SEO client.

## FAQ

### How many prompts does each client need?

Most clients need 30 to 50 prompts. That covers their main product category, competitor comparisons and key questions. Larger clients with many products may need 100 or more.

### How often should prompts be checked?

Daily is best. AI answers vary from run to run, so more checks give a steadier number. Weekly checks work for small clients on a tight budget.

### Can I white-label AI visibility reports?

Some tools allow it. Arrowsterr's Agency plan downloads every page in your own Brand Kit with no Arrowsterr branding. Rankscale lists white-label options on Growth. Ask any other vendor before you promise branded reports.

### Should I charge clients for AI visibility separately?

Many agencies add it as a line in the SEO retainer. Others sell a one-off AI audit first and then a monthly tracking and content package. The free AI Snapshot works well as a lead-in.

### Which AIs should I track for clients?

Start with ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews. They reach the most people. Add Gemini, AI Mode, Perplexity and Claude as the client's budget allows.

### Can clients log in themselves?

Most tools let you add seats. A share link is often simpler. Arrowsterr's share links can have a password and an expiry date, and you can revoke them.

### How long before clients see results?

Tracking shows a baseline in the first month. Changes from new content and review-site work usually show within 1 to 3 months. Set that timeline in your first report.
