# SE Ranking vs Semrush: Pricing and Features Compared in 2026

SE Ranking and Semrush are both full SEO suites. Each one covers keyword research, rank tracking, site audits and backlink checks, and both now track how AI answers mention your brand. The real gaps are data size and what each plan includes for the price.

Here is the quick answer. Pick SE Ranking if you want lots of tracked keywords and AI prompt tracking on one plan that starts at $129 a month. Pick Semrush if you need the largest keyword and backlink data, or ads and market research in the same login.

## SE Ranking vs Semrush: main points

- **Entry plans:** SE Ranking Core is $129 a month for 2,000 keywords tracked daily and 25 AI prompts tracked daily. Semrush's SEO plan is $139 a month for 500 tracked keywords.
- **Data size:** Semrush lists more than 26 billion keywords and more than 43 trillion backlinks. SE Ranking lists 5.4 billion keywords and 2.7 trillion backlinks.
- **AI tracking:** SE Ranking includes AI prompt tracking on Core and Growth. Semrush sells it as the AI Visibility Toolkit at $99 a month, or inside Semrush One from $199 a month.
- **Trying it out:** SE Ranking has a 14-day trial with no credit card. Semrush has a free account with 10 reports a day and a 7-day trial.
- **Seats:** SE Ranking Core includes 1 manager seat, with extra seats from $16. Semrush lists extra users from $45 a month.
- **Claude:** Neither tool lists Claude among the AIs it tracks.

## What is SE Ranking?

SE Ranking is an SEO suite built around daily rank tracking. It also covers keyword research, competitor research, backlinks, site audits and content. Its Core, Growth and Enterprise plans include an AI Results Tracker. The company also sells SE Visible, a separate AI visibility tool that starts at $71 a month.

## What is Semrush?

Semrush is one of the largest SEO suites. It is split into toolkits, such as SEO, AI Visibility, Advertising and Local. You can buy them one at a time. Semrush One bundles the SEO Toolkit and the AI Visibility Toolkit on one plan.

## SE Ranking vs Semrush keyword research

Both tools give you search volume, keyword difficulty and long lists of related keywords. The difference is how much data sits behind them.

Semrush's Keyword Magic Tool lists more than 26 billion keywords. SE Ranking lists 5.4 billion keywords across 188 country databases, with 591 million in the US.

Semrush is stronger here. Its bigger database helps most when you research small countries, long-tail topics or large sites. For a small or mid-size site in one main market, SE Ranking's data covers what most teams need.

## SE Ranking vs Semrush rank tracking

Rank tracking is where SE Ranking stands out. Its entry plan tracks more keywords than Semrush's entry plan, and both update daily.

| Plan | Tracked keywords | Price per month |
|---|---|---|
| SE Ranking Core | 2,000 | $129 |
| SE Ranking Growth | 5,000 | $279 |
| Semrush SEO | 500 | $139 |
| Semrush One Starter | 500 | $199 |

SE Ranking includes 3 local locations on Core and Growth, and you can add more. Semrush adds multi-location and device tracking on Pro+.

SE Ranking is stronger for rank tracking. If you track thousands of keywords for clients, its plans give you more room.

## SE Ranking vs Semrush backlink data

Semrush is clearly stronger on backlinks. Its backlink tool lists more than 43 trillion backlinks and 390 million referring domains. Semrush says the database refreshes at least hourly.

SE Ranking's data page lists 2.7 trillion backlinks and 420 million indexed domains. Its backlink checker page gives 2.9 trillion. SE Ranking says 58% of its backlinks are updated within 90 days.

Both work for checking your own links and a few rivals. Pick Semrush if link building is a core part of your work and you need the widest view of who links where.

## SE Ranking vs Semrush site audit

Both suites crawl your site and flag technical issues. SE Ranking puts clear limits on its pricing page: 250,000 pages a month on Core and 2 million on Growth.

Semrush includes Site Audit in its SEO Toolkit. Semrush One Starter adds an AI-ready Site Audit. It checks whether your site blocks eight AI crawlers, including bots from ChatGPT, Perplexity and Claude.

This one is close. SE Ranking is easier to plan around because its page limits are on the pricing page. Semrush adds checks for AI crawlers.

## SE Ranking vs Semrush AI visibility tracking

SE Ranking's AI Results Tracker comes with Core, Growth and Enterprise. Both main plans track 25 prompts daily on 5 AIs. SE Ranking's help center lists them as Google AI Overviews, AI Mode, Gemini, ChatGPT and Perplexity. If you need more prompts, SE Visible starts at $71 a month for 75 prompts.

Semrush's AI Visibility Toolkit is $99 a month on its own. It includes 1 project, 1 Brand Performance domain and 25 tracked prompts. Extra prompts cost $60 a month for each 50, and each extra user needs a $99 license. The toolkit has no free trial. Semrush One raises the limits to 50 prompts on Starter and 100 on Pro+.

Semrush's AI reports cover Google AI Overviews, AI Mode, ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity. Coverage changes by report. Semrush's help page lists Prompt Tracking on AI Overviews, AI Mode and ChatGPT.

| Trial | 14 days, no card | None for the toolkit, 7 days for Semrush One |

SE Ranking is stronger if you want AI tracking inside your SEO plan with no add-on. Semrush is stronger if you want to study AI answers for any domain, since its AI Analysis reports are not limited to your own brand.

## Which should you choose: SE Ranking or Semrush?

**Pick SE Ranking if:**

- You track thousands of keywords and want them updated daily.
- You want AI prompt tracking included in your SEO plan.
- You want a full trial with no credit card.
- You run a small agency and want local tracking and client projects on one plan.

**Pick Semrush if:**

- You need the largest keyword and backlink databases.
- Link building or research in many countries is a big part of your work.
- You also run Google Ads and want ads research from the same vendor.
- You want to study AI answers for competitor domains, beyond your own prompts.

## SE Ranking vs Semrush FAQ

### Is SE Ranking cheaper than Semrush?

SE Ranking's entry plan is $129 a month, and Semrush's SEO plan is $139 a month. SE Ranking Core also includes 2,000 tracked keywords and AI prompt tracking. Semrush's SEO plan includes 500 tracked keywords, and AI tracking comes with Semrush One or the AI Visibility Toolkit.

### Does SE Ranking have as much data as Semrush?

No. Semrush lists more than 26 billion keywords and more than 43 trillion backlinks. SE Ranking lists 5.4 billion keywords and 2.7 trillion backlinks. For most single-market sites, SE Ranking's data is enough.

### Do SE Ranking and Semrush track ChatGPT?

Yes, both do. SE Ranking tracks ChatGPT along with Google AI Overviews, AI Mode, Gemini and Perplexity. Semrush covers ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, AI Mode, Gemini and Perplexity, with coverage that varies by report.

### Does SE Ranking have a free plan?

SE Ranking has no free plan. It offers a 14-day trial with no credit card. Semrush has a free account with 10 reports a day.

### Which is better for agencies, SE Ranking or Semrush?

SE Ranking suits agencies that track many keywords for clients. Growth includes 5,000 keywords, 30 projects and 3 seats, and the Agency Pack is $69 a month on yearly billing. Semrush suits agencies that need deep research data, and its Advanced plan adds team users and reporting.

## Summary: SE Ranking vs Semrush

SE Ranking gives you more tracked keywords and built-in AI prompt tracking at a lower entry price. Semrush gives you the largest keyword and backlink data, plus separate toolkits for ads, local and AI research.

If daily rankings drive your work, start with SE Ranking's free trial. If research depth drives your work, start with Semrush's free account or its 7-day trial.
